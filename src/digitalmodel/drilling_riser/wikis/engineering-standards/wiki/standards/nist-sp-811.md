# Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI)

Section 4.3, Table 5 defines the kilo prefix. Appendix B.8 gives the
international foot and inch conversions. Notes 22 and 23 give the exact
pound and pound-force definitions. Mass and force conversion coefficients
supplied by a caller retain their own basis. [NIST SP 811, 2008 edition](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/SP/nistspecialpublication811e2008.pdf)
