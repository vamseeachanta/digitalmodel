## 1. Purpose and scope

The drilling model connects the upper flex-joint pivot, inner barrel, sliding tension ring, riser, lower flex joint and fixed stack datum; the bore contents are separate from structural mass, and buoyancy-equivalent diameter is separate from drag diameter. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/spec.py:L1-L18]
The builder converts the specification to a generic modular-generator input and writes text YAML, with engineering inputs in m, kg, N and Pa converted to model units te, kN, m and kN.m/deg. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/build.py:L1-L5] [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/build.py:L112-L143] [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/build.py:L475-L492]
Open-water completion models instead connect a tension frame through a laterally restrained, vertically free rotary to the riser and stack; a foundation may replace the fixed datum. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/open_water.py:L3-L20]
Hard/soft hang-off and running configurations are transformations of the drilling specification, with no conductor foundation in the hanging string. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/hang_off.py:L3-L16] [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/hang_off.py:L56-L86]
The parent routing entry has maturity `development`; this is a registry classification, not a claim of validated engineering coverage. [docs/registry/module-routing.yaml:L87-L92]
Geometry closure, positive property ranges and topology constraints are enforced, but no maximum water depth or qualified program-version range is specified by the model schema. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/spec.py:L41-L70] [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/spec.py:L366-L436]

## 2. Inputs and outputs

### Input conventions

Global z is positive upward, MSL is zero, and section lists are top to bottom; vessel offset is global (x,y) from the well centre in metres. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/spec.py:L3-L17] [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/spec.py:L384-L386]
RAO axes are x forward, y port, z up; rotations are starboard-down roll, bow-down pitch and bow-to-port yaw; phases lead or lag the crest at the RAO origin, and wave directions denote propagation relative to vessel x. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/spec.py:L169-L195]
Current direction is the direction of travel from global x, with depth positive below MSL and terminal speed held beyond the final depth. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/spec.py:L239-L256]
Required means no schema default; unrestricted means no numeric bound is declared for that field, rather than engineering applicability. [src/digitalmodel/drilling_riser/global_model/spec.py:L366-L397]
