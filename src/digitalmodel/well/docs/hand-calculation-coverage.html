Well calculation test coverage and evidence limits

Summary

The added tests compare specified casing geometry and axial yield, pipe-displacement geometry, two-column hydrostatics, interpolation and drilling-window bookkeeping with independent hand values. All six workflow adapters receive direct execution, output readback and missing-input checks. Unopened standard equations and nonzero friction normalization remain NOT_EVALUATED. Passing invariants do not establish the absolute engineering ratings.

Worked example: specified casing section

Inputs: outside diameter 9.625 in, wall 0.472 in, specified axial yield stress 80,000 psi. The inner diameter is 9.625 − 2 × 0.472 = 8.681 in. The diameter-to-wall ratio is 20.4, displayed to one decimal place.

Method: the metal area is mean circumference times wall, A = π × (9.625 − 0.472) × 0.472 = 13.6 in2 displayed. Equation 12.34 of [1] gives F = stress × A. Using the unrounded area, the answer is 1,085,788.7 lbf. The tests retain the unrounded reference and compare at relative tolerance 1e-12, which covers reference rounding and binary arithmetic. It is not an engineering allowance.

The second section uses outside diameter 7.000 in and wall 0.362 in, giving inner diameter 6.276 in and axial yield force 603,928.7 lbf at the same specified strength. At the second specified strength, 110,000 psi, the two force answers are 1,492,959.5 lbf and 830,402.0 lbf, respectively. Both sizes are tested at both strengths. The wall factor and an absolute burst answer are omitted because the standard clause evidence is absent.

Worked example: open and closed pipe motion

Inputs: hole diameter 10 in, pipe OD 6 in, pipe ID 4 in, speed magnitude 160 ft/min, and specified clinging input 0.25. Hole area is 25π, pipe envelope area 9π, bore area 4π and annular area 16π, all in in2.

Closed displacement is 9/16; open steel displacement is 5/(16+4) = 1/4. The module's specified clinging input adds to those ratios, giving effective velocities 130.0 ft/min closed and 80.0 ft/min open. This checks the area balance under the documented open-pipe split assumption; it does not establish an empirical clinging calibration. With specified zero viscosity and zero yield stress, pressure is exactly zero and both equivalent mud weights remain at the supplied 12 ppg.

Worked example: hydrostatics and interval margins

Inputs: upper column 1,000 ft at 8 ppg; lower column 1,000 ft at 16 ppg. The conversion in [2] is 0.052 psi/(ppg ft). The column pressures are 416.0 psi and 832.0 psi, giving 1,248.0 psi total, compared with 1,664.0 psi for a single 16 ppg column over 2,000 ft. The equivalent two-column weight is 12 ppg. For a supplied pore bound 10 ppg, the after-loss riser margin is +2 ppg under the implemented static criterion.

For supplied pore and fracture bounds 10 and 14 ppg, kick margin 0.5 ppg, loss margin 1 ppg and ECD increment 0.75 ppg, the static interval is [10.5, 13] ppg, width 2.5 ppg. The dynamic interval is [10.5, 12.3] ppg displayed, width 1.8 ppg displayed; the tests retain the unrounded interval and width. An ECD increment 2.5 ppg closes that interval; 3 ppg gives width −0.5 ppg. The implementation requires positive width, so both closed and inverted intervals are infeasible.

Evidence and NOT_EVALUATED items

  • API TR 5C3 / ISO 10400 edition, applicable clause, wall factor, collapse coefficients, absolute collapse pressure and independent regime-boundary values: NOT_EVALUATED. The public API reference request returned a general page without the requested clause text [4]. No licensed standard is copied.
  • The nonzero swab/surge pressure and independent nonzero trip-speed benchmark: NOT_EVALUATED. The cited journal endpoint returned HTTP 403 [3]. Scaling and inverse round trips cannot qualify its dimensional coefficient or the engineering applicability of the screening model.
  • Negative trip speeds are interpreted as magnitudes by the documented API; zero speed is permitted. A requirement to reject nonpositive speed is not established. Nonpositive diameters are refused.
  • Nonzero ROP predictions, bore-design lookup values, cost calibration, catalog strengths/dimensions, standard rounding rules, design factors, envelope limits and damaged-section applicability remain NOT_EVALUATED by these added tests. Casing-adapter output checks exercise the current catalog and nearest-thousand display contract; these do not qualify the catalog source.
  • The complete well test command encounters an unchanged missing-dependency collection error in tests/well/test_swab_surge.py: ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'assetutilities'. That engine smoke test is not established by the direct adapter tests.
