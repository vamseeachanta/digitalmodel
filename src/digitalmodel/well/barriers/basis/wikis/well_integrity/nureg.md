Public basis record, checked 2026-11-02. Fault Tree Handbook, January 1981:

https://www.nrc.gov/regulations-legislation/nureg-series-publications/publications-prepared-by-nrc-staff/sr0492

Chapter XI, pages 12–13: periodically tested standby failures, perfect testing
and restoration, negligible repair time, uniform demand time. The first-order
average unavailability is lambda times interval divided by two. This module
restricts lambda times interval to at most 0.1 at the upper rate bound.
Chapter VII and equation XI-24 give Boolean operations and the independent-event
product for a minimal cut set. Shared events remain shared in the Boolean union.
Products of average PFDs here additionally assume independent random test phases;
synchronized tests and unmodelled common causes are outside this calculation.
