The topside shutdown/isolation valve and actuator generic DU rate is 2.3 per
million hours. The aggregate operational-review interval is 2.1–2.6 per million
hours (5–95%). The reported mode fractions include 45% failure to close and
15% closed-position leakage. Applying those fractions to the DU rate and its
interval is a screening assumption; the resulting per-mode bands are not measured
confidence intervals. Delayed operation is excluded. The source excludes pilot
valves. No cement, downhole-valve or external-leak rate is supplied by this module.
