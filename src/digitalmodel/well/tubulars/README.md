# Well tubulars

The [module analysis](../../../../docs/reports/modules/well_tubulars/index.html)
inventories casing, tubing, stress-envelope and rod-string entry points, with
input/output units, source metadata and explicit limits. Its synthetic thin-wall
results are checked against independent equilibrium and principal stresses.
Design acceptance and unqualified methods remain `NOT_EVALUATED`.

The [worked stress derivation](docs/worked-tubular-stress-invariants.md) gives
the unchanged assumptions, equations and executable public-helper example.
The [report guide](../../../../docs/reports/modules/well_tubulars/README.md)
describes reproducible builds and the delivered byte manifest.
