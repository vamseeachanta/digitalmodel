## 1. Purpose and scope

The package computes equivalent and principal stresses, circular pipe pressure/axial/bending stresses, tension-moment envelopes, material curves and incremental plasticity responses. [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/vm_stress.py:L164-L200] [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/vm_stress.py:L241-L449] [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/vm_stress.py:L469-L492] [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/stress_strain.py:L328-L384] [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/nonlinear.py:L359-L444]

The workflow generates a material curve CSV and replaces the input `stress_strain` mapping with a summary; the registry classifies its runtime as offline. [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/workflow.py:L21-L45] [docs/registry/workflows.yaml:L265-L272]

The domain has `stable` maturity; that is a routing classification, while material-curve support is limited to linear elastic, Ramberg-Osgood and bilinear models despite additional enum members. [docs/registry/module-routing.yaml:L429-L434] [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/stress_strain.py:L29-L35] [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/stress_strain.py:L336-L359]

Combined pipe stress uses pressure, axial force and one signed outer-fibre bending contribution; `torque` and `temperature_change` are accepted load fields but contribute no stress in that calculation. [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/vm_stress.py:L49-L69] [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/vm_stress.py:L312-L358]

The nonlinear single-axis route deliberately selects a uniaxial coupon response instead of the full three-dimensional constitutive matrix; it must not serve as an independent three-dimensional constraint check. [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/nonlinear.py:L331-L357] [src/digitalmodel/structural/stress/nonlinear.py:L446-L467]
