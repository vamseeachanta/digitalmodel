# Issue 487 plan-criterion screening basis

The plan specifies MBL/static tension >= 1.67 and MBL/dynamic tension >= 3.0.
These factors are retained as project screening criteria. Their attribution to
DNV-ST-N001 section 11 has not been established by clause or table evidence.
The checks therefore make no DNV-ST-N001 strength or compliance determination.

Fairlead ratings, bridle bounds, scope and clearance are caller inputs. Tug
continuous bollard pull is not the plan's line-strength load basis. The worked
example's 430 kN MBL is an explicit synthetic assumption below its 784.532 kN
tug capacity. A screening PASS does not qualify that line for full tug power.
Certified line data and the governing standard's equipment-strength criterion
are required before operational acceptance can be established.
