## 1. Purpose and scope

`DynacardWorkflow.run_full_analysis` converts a surface dynamometer card to a pump card, screens compression, computes fluid load, intake pressure, fillage and theoretical displacement, then classifies the card. [src/digitalmodel/marine_ops/artificial_lift/dynacard/solver.py:L225-L276]

The default is `everitt_jennings`; `gibbs` and `finite_difference` remain comparison paths with explicit output restrictions, rather than qualified diagnostic references. [src/digitalmodel/marine_ops/artificial_lift/dynacard/solver.py:L28-L94][src/digitalmodel/marine_ops/artificial_lift/dynacard/solver_guards.py:L16-L26]

The field-health route runs the configured wells and writes CSV and JSON summaries; the single-well route optionally writes an HTML diagnostic report. [src/digitalmodel/marine_ops/artificial_lift/field_health.py:L38-L84][src/digitalmodel/marine_ops/artificial_lift/dynacard/solver.py:L125-L137]

The separate `rod_pump.analyse` entry point computes elastic string properties, timing and displacement, enforces its `Class I` correlation envelope, and leaves efficiency undetermined without runtime and formation volume factor. [src/digitalmodel/marine_ops/artificial_lift/dynacard/rod_pump/analysis.py:L75-L255]

The containing domain is registered as beta; this is domain maturity, not numerical qualification of every calculator. [docs/registry/module-routing.yaml:L188-L193]

UNVERIFIED: field diagnostic accuracy and operational fitness are not established by the synthetic classifier tests; confirm against independently labelled recorded cards before using labels for intervention decisions (section 10, #1864). [src/digitalmodel/marine_ops/artificial_lift/dynacard/training.py:L10-L18]
