# Engineering equation and assumption sidecar

This sidecar governs the direct-file audit corrections. All checks are offline.
Native qualification is NOT_RUN; `qa_gates.require_native_gate` raises instead of
starting or importing a commercial solver. Synthetic screening data are not
qualified analysis results.

| ID / issue sections | Governing clause, table or equation | Implementation and independent reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 / 1, 6, 9 | Gauss divergence theorem with F=(0,0,z), div F=1: V=integral(z nz dS). Green area theorem: A=1/2 integral(x dy-y dx). WAMIT 7.5 manual 3.1 gives the equivalent volume/first-moment surface integrals with the opposite normal convention. | `compute_mesh_summary` uses outward-from-body normals, signed V, exact triangle integration and oriented waterline edges. No division by 3 and no absolute volume. A 2x2x2 box is 8 m3; lidless waterplane is 4 m2; y>=0 half reconstruction uses factor 2. Requires submerged panels terminating at z=0; clip crossing panels upstream. A nonpositive volume and an open boundary with no reconstructable waterline are explicitly flagged. Scalar displaced-volume and waterplane-area helpers raise for nonpositive volume and missing waterline area, respectively. A closed surface wholly below z=-1 mm (the C4-WATERLINE band) has established Awp=0; a closed surface within that band still requires a waterline and is flagged/rejected when none is available. Closure requires exact shared vertices and opposite directed edges, or unpaired edges lying exactly on a declared x=0/y=0 symmetry plane; no snapping hides gaps. Open submerged surfaces remain flagged and rejected by the waterplane helper. |
| M2 / 6, 9 | WAMIT 7.5 manual 6.1, low-order GDF geometry: ISX/ISY symmetry declarations, NPAN and four vertex triples per panel. | Package defaults both flags to 0, accepts only 0/1. | R1 / 4, 5, 13 | Response spectrum S_r=abs(RAO)²*S_wave. Narrow-band Rayleigh peak law q(x)=exp(-x²/(2 sigma²)). Transform Y=X²/(2 sigma²) to unit exponential; E[max Y]=H_N=ln(N)+Euler+O(1/N). First-order square-root expansion gives E[max X] approximately sigma*(sqrt(2ln N)+Euler/sqrt(2ln N)). Significant single amplitude=2sigma; double amplitude=4sigma. | `short_term_statistics`; Euler=0.5772156649015329. sigma=1,N=1000 gives 3.71692218885 and 3.872214 (asymptote, not exact finite-N mean). Deprecated `significant` retains its previous single-amplitude meaning with a warning. This is a mathematical derivation from the peak law, not a broadband qualification. |
| R2 / 2, 13 | Expected exceedance count equation: R_seconds * sum(p_i/Tz_i * exp(-x²/(2 sigma_i²)))=1; Tz=2pi*sqrt(m0/m2). | `long_term_extreme` treats scatter probabilities as time fractions and validates their sum within absolute 1e-6. One cell with Tz=10 s over 100 Julian years has 315576000 cycles and x=sqrt(2*ln(315576000))=6.2561826 sigma. Two cells: p=.4,.6, Tz=10,20 s, sigma=1,2; x=12.12136537. Sea-state duration cancels from this expected-count definition; the compatibility parameter is deprecated explicitly and does not affect the result. |
| S1 / 3, 13 | ISO 2631-1:1997 Annex D, D.1(b), Eq. (D.2): MSDVz=aw*sqrt(T_seconds). D.2: MSI percent=Km*MSDVz, Km=1/3 for the reference population. | `motion_sickness_incidence` requires explicit exposure; frequency is required only for unweighted input. Already Wf-weighted aw=.5 m/s², T=7200 s gives MSDVz=42.42640687 and MSI=14.14213562%. Unweighted input must represent one sinusoidal component. Broadband RMS must be weighted component by component externally. The estimate is capped at 100%; Annex D's empirical guidance is approximately 20 min–6 h and up to 70%, so a cap does not extend qualification. Citation identifiers and a computational-reference qualification accompany each result. Licensed-edition verification is pending, so issue sections 3 and 13 remain open for that requirement. |
| S2 / 3 | ISO 8041:2005 5.6, Eqs. (8)–(12), Table 3 Wf row; independent ordinates in Table B.5. | `wf_weighting` implements high/low Butterworth band limits f1=.08,f2=.63 Hz,Q1=Q2=1/sqrt(2); transition f3=infinity,f4=.25 Hz,Q4=.86; step f5=.0625,f6=.1 Hz,Q5=Q6=.8,K=1. | W1 / 7 | Ochi-Hubble component equation, BOEM Metocean Recommended Practices, 5.2, Eq. | W2 / 10, 12 | DNV-RP-C205 (2014) 3.5.5, JONSWAP angular spectrum: S=A_gamma*(5/16)*Hs²*omega_p^4*omega^-5*exp[-1.25*(omega_p/omega)^4]*gamma^b; A_gamma=1-.287ln gamma; sigma=.07/.09 below/above peak. | `jonswap` retains the published approximate analytic normalizer; no per-grid renormalization masks the constants. | W3 / 11, 12 | ISSC expression S=Hs²/(4pi)*omega_z^4*omega^-5*exp[-(omega_z/omega)^4/pi]. Analytic moments imply Tp/Tz=(5pi/4)^(1/4)=1.40771588. | `generate_spectrum` uses the analytic conversion. | H1 / 18 | Explicit package heuristic, with no published-model attribution: partition threshold 6.6*Hs^(1/3), secondary Hs=.1*Hs, component gamma=3.3/1, normalize their sum to Hs²/16. | Renamed `heuristic_two_peak` / `HeuristicTwoPeak`. | C1 / 8, 15 | WAMIT 7.5 manual 3.2 and 5.2: .1 contains PER,I,J,Abar,Bbar; .3 contains exciting forces. Abar=A/(rho L^k), Bbar=B/(rho omega L^k), k=3/4/5. Reciprocity gives Aij=Aji at zero forward speed. | Narrower permitted fix: native dimensional helpers explicitly named `to_native_added_mass`, `to_native_damping`, `from_native_added_mass`; rows are omega(rad/s),i,j,value(SI). Former WAMIT helpers raise rather than accept authentic .1 data as native rows. Matrices require finite 6x6 values and preserve raw discretisation asymmetry for the reciprocity gate. No WAMIT converter is claimed. |
| C2 / 15 | WAMIT 7.5 manual 3.1, hydrostatic restoring equations; reduced about principal axes through centre of flotation: C33=rho*g*Awp; C44=rho*g*V*GMt; C55=rho*g*V*GMl. | Defaults rho=1025 kg/m3,g=9.81 m/s² are explicit assumptions. For V=10000,Awp=3000,GMt=2,GMl=100: 30165750 N/m,201105000 and10055250000 N m/rad. C35/C53 and other coupling are explicitly omitted; offset axes require an external full matrix transformation. |
| R4 / 17 | Definition Cb=V/(LBT), free-floating mass=rho*V (WAMIT 7.5 3.4). Deep-water dispersion k=omega²/g; rotation per wave amplitude tends to wave slope k*180/pi deg/m (Orcina conventions and checking RAOs). | `VesselParameters` uses rho=1.025 t/m3 and a 1% consistency band. All five entries now equal rho*LBT*Cb, including the multi-column entry. `get_representative_raos` marks output synthetic and multiplies deep-water slope by the oscillator gain 1/sqrt((1-r²)²+(2*zeta*r)²), r=Tn/T, and the synthetic short-wave envelope min(1,(T/Tn)^2), before applying heading scales. For T<Tn this holds the excitation at the Tn slope and restores a T^2 decay as T tends to zero; it is an explicit screening assumption, not a hull-cancellation model. Relative to slope times oscillator gain alone, attenuation lies in [0,1], is exactly one at/above Tn, and for T>=a*Tn (0<a<1) reduces the response by at most 1-a^2. Thus the reduction is at most 19% above 0.9*Tn and 36% above 0.8*Tn. At T=12 s and Tn=14 s the factor is 36/49=0.734694 (26.5306% reduction); at T=25 s and Tn=50 s it is 1/4, giving pitch/slope=0.25/hypot(3,0.48)=0.082287 (91.77% below slope) for zeta=0.12. These bounds describe the screening envelope, not physical hull cancellation or a resolution of section 17. At T=Tn the gain is 1/(2*zeta); it tends to one only for T much larger than Tn. A 25 s wave need not be in that limit. The issue section-17 acceptance of pitch within 10% of the slope at 25 s is not met by four entries and remains open for maintainer disposition on issue #2748; these response-model regressions do not waive that acceptance. Natural periods, damping and heading scales remain unqualified screening assumptions; this analytic model does not establish design loads. |
| D1 / 14, 19 | Drag equation F=.5*rho*C*A*V² and moment M=r cross F. Package frame definition +x forward,+y port,+z up; direction FROM clockwise from bow, [0,360). | `compute_wind_current_drift` uses velocity direction (-cos heading,+sin heading), assumes yaw arm +.1*L along x. Beam wind20 m/s gives +588 kN sway and +14700 kN m yaw; current1 m/s gives +922.5 kN and +23062.5 kN m. Head wind gives -735 kN surge. Densities, areas and coefficients are unchanged assumptions. Imported mean-drift data must declare the same frame/convention after transformation. |
| Q1 / 16 | Pack section-5 criteria: balance1%, aspect3, edge lambda/6, waterline1 mm, draft1 cm, damping diagonals nonnegative, long-wave heave1±2%. | `qa_gates` exposes producing functions and explicit assertion outputs. Centroids use divergence first moments and exact quadratic triangle integration; E[xz]=(sum x*sum z+sum(xz))/12. C4 evaluates both raw added-mass and radiation-damping reciprocity with a 2% tolerance on abs(Mij-Mji)/max(sqrt(abs(Mii*Mjj)),abs(Mij),abs(Mji)); an all-zero pair has zero error. Diagonal normalization admits small opposite-sign noise, and the coupling fallback prevents a zero diagonal from hiding asymmetric nonzero couplings. The existing 1% pass and 3% rejection cases remain. Long-wave heave is taken from the maximum supplied period of a Heave RAOTable in m/m across every supplied heading; scalar assertions are rejected. C5 computes coverage against an explicit required-quantities set (the canonical offline_pack_report_v1 set is named in agent-pack.md): one of two missing gives 50%, and unrelated extra keys cannot inflate coverage. Native loading, irregular-frequency spike/lid evidence and native comparisons are NOT_RUN, with consumer requests raising. |

R4-P downstream pamphlet regression (Tp=6 s): the proxy now consumes the R4
rotational response, including its short-wave envelope. An independent scalar
calculation uses the tabulated periods 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,12,14,16,18,20,25 s;
linear interpolation of amplitudes in angular frequency with held endpoints;
and 200 equally spaced frequencies from 0.02 to 2 rad/s. The JONSWAP shape is
`w^-5 exp[-1.25(wp/w)^4] 3.3^exp[-(w-wp)^2/(2 sigma^2 wp^2)]`, with
`wp=2*pi/6`, sigma=0.07/0.09 below/above the peak, normalized by the trapezoidal
integral to m0=1/16 for Hs=1 m. Integrate amplitude squared times that spectrum.
The synthetic barge proxy uses heave/roll/pitch Tn=6/8/6.5 s and
zeta=0.10/0.06/0.10; the synthetic drillship proxy uses Tn=9/16/9.5 s and
zeta=0.08/0.04/0.08. Heading maxima are heave/roll at beam and pitch at head seas.
Pitch governs both: response m0=14.227770329103 and 0.269737076803 respectively,
so `Hs_limit=0.90*2.5/(2*sqrt(m0))` gives 0.298252504646 and 2.166118438166 m.
The pamphlet rounds to **0.30 m** and **2.17 m**; its existing absolute test
bands remain 0.02 m and 0.05 m. These replace prototype goldens 0.52 m and 1.84 m
because the R4 response changed, not because the tolerance changed. The drillship
Tp=6 s golden in `tests/installation/test_installation_pamphlet.py` is shared
with PR #2773's spectral-grid change. Whichever PR merges second must recompute
this value using the combined R4 response and spectral grid; neither isolated
branch value is a combined-change reference. Other pamphlet goldens are untouched.
