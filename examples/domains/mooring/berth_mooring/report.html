Analysis methodology

Steady load: F = rho Cd A |v| v / 2; Mz = x Fy - y Fx. NASA Drag Equation, public web edition retrieved 2026-10-20. The single supplied area and coefficient form an isotropic screen; directional wind/current coefficients are not inferred. Velocity points toward the force.

Passage: stagger(t) = initial_stagger + speed t. The existing closed-form passing-force implementation uses parabolic sectional areas and the finite-depth image sum documented in the repository's 1975-method note. Its numerical settings are explicit inputs. The passing track is +y and the quay is -y; yaw is right handed. Forces are summed in a fixed initial body frame. Bank/channel effects, current-passage interaction and moving-hull feedback are excluded.

Mooring links: T = EA (length - unstretched_length) / unstretched_length in tension, otherwise zero. Fenders: linear interpolation of supplied length/tension points, with negative tension indicating compression. Orcina Links: Data, public web edition retrieved 2026-10-20. Zero damping is an explicit exclusion.

Acceptance: sampled maximum tensile/compressive magnitude divided by a supplied allowable is utilisation. PASS requires peak <= allowable. Every allowable needs a source. Missing allowable or response is NOT_EVALUATED. No licensed-standard limit is inferred.

Passing-force symmetry and speed-squared scaling are checked; the inherited module retains its existing regression tests. Generated native review models pass repository structural YAML validation. This is not solver load attestation or physical validation of the example. Sampling and image-sum convergence for this example are NOT_EVALUATED. Stored results, when supplied, require the exact input digest and one solver version; this checks consistency, not authenticity or solver convergence.

Way forward

Supply sourced allowable line tensions, fender reactions and any displacement or compression limits. Qualify hydrodynamic inertia/damping and initial equilibrium. Wire the CSV loads to vessel applied loads, converting N to kN and N m to kN m, and retain the fixed global axes during rotations. Load and solve the twelve cases only under separate authorisation on a recorded solver version. Export nonnegative line tensile/fender compression magnitudes in kN for every case and object; the stored-result evaluator checks completeness and supplied limits. Record sampling, time-step and image-sum convergence before treating sampled peaks as governing.
