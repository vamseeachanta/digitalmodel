Resource intelligence and proposed implementation

The existing public geometry and its dimension provenance in README.md will remain the physical basis. The supplied historical builder and expected records are outside this checkout; new v2 records will not replace them. The existing 21-test suite will be retained. The execution will use one computation at a time and no licensed API.

  1. Dispersion and DF3 tests will precede the exact-depth implementation. The relation omega² = g k tanh(k h), lambda = 2 pi/k, and span = lambda/6 will set the limit. Six panels per wavelength is ASSUMED from the supplied study criterion, not a standard. Inputs will be g=9.80665 m/s², h=1500 m and omega=3.50 rad/s; the span will be 0.8383265196279277 m. Acceptance will use unrounded values.
  2. The unchanged solid definition will supply a coarse/fine hull and matching planar interior cap. Mesher targets will differ; a conforming midpoint refinement and exported-byte readback will enforce the span. Manifests will bind geometry, generator, inputs and outputs. Payloads exceeding the publication size limit will stay outside Git.
  3. Lid checks will require upward, nondegenerate SWL panels, the exact opposite hull waterline boundary, all interior edges paired, coverage equal to the hull waterplane, and all panel centroids inside the waterline. Hull panels will remain below SWL.
  4. A versioned builder will bind mass, hashes, 350 exact frequencies, nine headings, 1500 m depth and interior-panel generation. DF0 will reject any mismatch. Native-generated interior panels will require independent readback against the reference lid.
  5. An adapter-only export will retain real and imaginary motion and both load formulations, axes, units, origins and phase conventions. Every exported quantity and its metadata will be compared after a native save/reload; fake-adapter tests will require reload failure to block export.

Plan defect review

A mesher edge setting will not establish maximum vertex span; the post-check will use serialized coordinates. A lid bounding box will not establish interior coverage; boundary and oriented area checks will be required, including brace waterlines. A magnitude comparison will not establish phase equality; reload checks will include both load methods. A successful offline gate will not clear historical DF5.2 or DF5.3 failures. A time scaling will be labelled ESTIMATED and will not establish native capacity or a release time. Independent provider review will remain a required external gate; no additional assistant will be called by this preparation.

Retention and limitations

Each generated case will retain GDF bytes, SHA-256 manifests, input and expected v2 records, compact offline measurements and logs. A completed case will be reused only after every digest and generation-input identity matches. Native inputs, complex arrays and reload records will be retained by their existing result owner. Public geometry source identity will remain nrel-iea-reference-turbines; no private or vendor payload will be copied. The supplied platform mass basis will be unchanged; system mass suitability will require separate confirmation.

The native version panel limit, lid generation details, solve time and memory will be unmeasured. Dense-memory N² and direct-solve N³ scaling from the supplied same-hull benchmark will provide conditional estimates only. A one-frequency pilot will establish the next allocation.

Conditional resource allocation basis

These supplied observations will not establish performance for the new meshes. Reference-lid panels will be included in the effective count; the native automatic lid count will remain unmeasured.
