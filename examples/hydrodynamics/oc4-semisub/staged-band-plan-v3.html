Staged diffraction acceptance plan

Strength acceptance will be evaluated first on Stage S. Fatigue acceptance will then require Stage F, a quantitatively reviewed omitted-tail response bound and L2 convergence probes. Offline tests will establish preparation behaviour only; native results and independent review will remain release prerequisites.

The preparation will use the existing public solid definition, the supplied public fatigue table and the unchanged platform-only mass and inertia in expected-v2.json. No Froude scaling, occurrence renormalisation, production-system mass substitution or coupled mooring/riser feedback will be introduced. The original DF5.2 and DF5.3 criteria will have to be supplied before those failures can be cleared.

The exact retained L1 and L2 GDF files and their benchmark linkage will have to be provided before production measurements or packets can be issued. The supplied L1 count of 14,064 and approximate maximum span of 1.72 m will not establish the identity or measured band of a mesh. A newly generated mesh will not inherit that benchmark identity. Synthetic cube packets will qualify software behaviour only.

The supplied no-lid benchmark will provide the conditional estimate basis: 3044.0 s over 32 frequencies, 35.6 GB peak working set and chunk averages of 82.7 to 112.3 s per frequency. These supplied observations will not be treated as new measurements. Native panel limits, memory sharing, frequency dependence, heading comparability and automatic-lid panel count will require readback.

Implementation and test sequence

  1. Finite-depth inversion tests will precede band calculation. Serialized mesh coordinates will provide the greatest pairwise vertex span s. The supported upper frequency will follow k = 2 pi/(6 s), omega = sqrt(g k tanh(k h)), with h = 1500 m and g = 9.80665 m/s². Six panels per wavelength will remain ASSUMED from the study criterion. The declared-band DF3 check will compare every panel against the unrounded wavelength limit; the separate 3.50 rad/s FAIL will remain unchanged.
  2. The v3 recipe will retain nine headings (-180 to 180 degrees at 45-degree increments), depth, density, mass, inertia, origin and axis conventions. Stage S will contain 0.30 to 0.55 rad/s at 0.01 rad/s, plus the specified 0.418879 rad/s, for 27 frequencies. The specified decimal will be retained as the strength interpolation frequency; the fatigue peaks will use the unrounded 2 pi/Tp values.
  3. Stage F will contain every 0.01 rad/s grid point from 0.05 rad/s up to the measured L1 band, plus its unrounded endpoint and 2 pi/6.59, 2 pi/7.43 and 2 pi/8.19 rad/s. No frequency will exceed that band. The supplied 1.983 rad/s irregular-frequency estimate will trigger a separate conforming reference lid when the band reaches or crosses it. The lid generator will retain each hull waterline segment; check_mesh.py will validate orientation, SWL elevation, coverage, separation and boundary equality. Lid spans will not exceed the L1 maximum span. Native automatic interior panels will still require readback against this reference; enabling automatic panels will not establish equivalence.
  4. Packet tests will require DF0 to check both decks, source and file digests, mesh measurements, counts, mass, headings, depth, frequencies, lid state and the L2 probe deck. A completed packet will be reused only after the retained source and every bound byte match; a partial packet will be retained and rejected.
  5. Closed-form constant and decaying RAOs and an intentionally failing constant case will test response integrals and tail bounds through the reload-verified v2 complex export adapter. Tests will cover phase, rotational units, origins, point transformation, cancelled point motion, unsupported headings and malformed axes. No licensed solver will be started, imported or queried.

Numerical comparison criteria will be ASSUMED pending confirmation: complex L1/L2 differences will be at most 5.0% of each L2 component's maximum magnitude over the compared frequencies, with that scale also used for near-zero components; phase differences will be at most 5 degrees above 1.0% of that maximum. Frequency refinement will use 0.005 rad/s in resonance and energy-bearing intervals, and integrated response variance and peak changes will be at most 2.0%. Each criterion will be applied to motions and both load formulations; original hydrostatic criteria in the retained high-band plan will continue to apply.

Conditional omitted-tail bound

For each supplied gamma=1 sea, the integration will use m0 = Hs²/16 and the unmodified variance CDF F(w) = exp[-(5/4)(wp/w)^4]. The complex RAO will be interpolated only inside its exported band. Hang-off translation at (0,0,-10) m will use translation plus rotation cross the offset from the declared origin, with rotations in rad/m and no loss of complex phase.

The high-tail bound will multiply the omitted upper wave variance by the squared declared envelope: the maximum magnitude in the last 0.5 rad/s, including the interpolated window boundary, times a configurable multiplier of at least one. The low-tail bound will use the full-band maximum and the omitted lower wave variance. Point envelopes will use the triangle inequality on translation and rotation bounds; in-band cancellation will not reduce an out-of-band envelope. The default lower limit of 0.05 rad/s will omit a negligible low tail for these seas, but its logarithmic fraction will be reported even if floating-point underflow produces a stored zero.

These bounds will be conservative only conditional on the reviewed envelope holding outside the measured band. An unobserved resonance can violate that assumption. The last-window values will never be inserted as response ordinates; no zero/flat extrapolation or hidden spectral renormalisation will occur. Annual fractions 0.015, 0.010 and 0.013 will be retained individually, without renormalising their 3.8% total. This subset will not establish a global fatigue maximum.

Plan defect review and retention

The review will hunt for unsupported measured claims, endpoint rounding that admits oversized panels, missing stage frequencies, reference/native lid confusion, high-tail resonances, coordinate or phase loss, cancellation credited outside the band and strength release incorrectly waiting for fatigue. Current-artifact independent provider review will remain OPEN; no second assistant will be called by this preparation.

The retained packet will contain exact GDF bytes, source and output SHA-256 receipts, both stage decks, the L2 probe deck, resolved recipe, estimates and DF0 readback. Native results will retain complex arrays, coefficients, metadata and save/reload evidence through the existing adapter. Large regenerable payloads will remain outside Git with their manifests. A local save will not establish backup or publication. Public source identity and benchmark provenance will be verified before any engineering release.

Lesson: an approximate mesh span will support an illustrative estimate only; production bands and decks will require the exact retained coordinates.
