## R3 defect disposition

1. **Curvature reference:** fixed-z/PCHIP remains the immutable source surface.
   Fixture section-curvature acceptance now compares one-sided reference
   derivatives away from interpolation knots. Exact-circle deviation is a
   separately reported interpolation error, not an impossible source-fidelity
   gate. Independent exact controls remain. The proposed prismatic K bound is
   explicitly a qualification threshold, not a proven achievable result.
2. **Bottom seam:** opt-in construction will share the fitted side's exact keel
   edge with its planar bottom; it will not compare a chord polygon against a
   curved edge and call it watertight. Original default geometry remains unchanged.
3. **Patch continuity:** longitudinal patching is deferred. Only one whole-side
   candidate family is in scope; no ambiguous smooth-join exception remains.
4. **Hidden invalid area:** qualification now requires exact native statuses plus
   whole and per-record valid-area fractions >= 1 - 1e-6. Missing evidence fails.
5. **Diagnostic configuration:** direct native calls will prove parity with
   screen_step at unchanged production settings before higher-order diagnostics.
   Metre/millimetre reference-length and area conversion are explicit.
6. **RED route:** the exporter harness will reproduce current native failures
   first, then select the opt-in method. Default-route regressions stay unchanged.
7. **Convergence/budget:** I means I_D; component and class checks are explicit.
   Twenty native diagnostic calls are allocated by purpose, with fixed time caps.
8. **Open-surface measures:** candidate volume and first moment use parametric
   projection integrals, avoiding ill-conditioned near-keel graph inversion and
   invalid use of open-shell solid volume. Out-of-bounds geometry cannot be clipped.
9. **Historical xfail:** the exact existing test name is recorded and its default
   behavior remains untouched. A failed candidate may yield a diagnostic report
   and explicit strict xfails, but cannot qualify as an item-2 repair.
