Single invocation, ledger and crash boundaries

A new maximum 600-second invocation window will apply only to this continuation. It will not be described as unused original time; the original 21.219-second native duration will not determine remaining allowance. After static source/identity validation, an exclusive, flushed invocation-start record will be created before prefix replay and live preflight. Its filename will derive from the externally pinned parent-claim stem plus .ordinal-2.invocation.json. It will bind UTC start/deadline, monotonic clock identity, fixed parent/ordinal, config/review pins and output ownership. Monotonic elapsed time will govern the process; UTC will provide an additional refusal check. Rollback or disagreement will never extend the allowance.

A single cooperative lineage reservation will cover parent revalidation and exclusive flushed successor creation at <parent-stem>.ordinal-2.json. Existing or partially written invocation/successor records, changed parent, missing attempt-1, alternate conflicting ordinal-2 claim, redirected residence or ordinal skip will refuse. The fixed filenames will not derive from a new process, config or output identifier. Restart will not recreate a deadline. Before successor creation, any prefix/preflight failure will leave original native consumption at 1 and three native cases unattempted, but ordinal-2 admission under this amendment will be terminally closed. A separate exclusive <parent-stem>.ordinal-2.terminal.json will record that disposition without overwriting invocation-start. After successor creation every result, including a pre-spawn failure or crash, will consume ordinal 2; consumed count will be 2, while actual launches will be recorded separately. A crash without terminal metadata will be consumed-or-unknown and will refuse readmission.

At least 365 seconds will remain before claim and 360 seconds at actual launcher entry: 300 seconds native timeout, 5 seconds shared settlement and 55 seconds retention planning margin. The 55 seconds will not be represented as a measured worst-case bound or guarantee; retention will complete before any overrun disposition. The owned runner will retain its 300-second timeout and one shared five-second settlement allowance. Only cylinder_runner.launch_case and its owned supervisor will settle or terminate the owned child. A postclaim clock/deadline failure will not abandon a running process or terminate unrelated processes. Execution metadata and both streams will be retained before final deadline assessment; a 305-second timeout/settlement or later extraction overrun will yield INCOMPLETE while preserving evidence. Unsettled ownership will retain the supervisor handle and seat/reservation; no blanket lock release will occur.

Retention versus unverified pressure semantics

The adapter will expose retention_status separately from independent_check_status. Aggregate capture_status will remain INCOMPLETE, with explicit unverified_evidence for applied pressure/supports, station and reaction exports, stress recovery and equilibrium. Retained station/reaction files will be hashed without implying their grammar or physical values have been verified. PLANNED_SCOPE_STOP will mean only that requested retention and enumerated generic checks completed; it will not upgrade incomplete pressure evidence or grant numerical acceptance. Missing or invalid retained files and failed generic checks will remain distinct from unverified pressure semantics.
