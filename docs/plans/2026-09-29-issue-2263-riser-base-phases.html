API and calculation contract

The existing calculation type will accept inputs.riser_base_assessment.components[]. This issue-specific namespace will avoid collision with #2261's top-level inputs.components[] riser contract. Each component will carry a unique name, an enumerated type (riser_base, foundation, mudmat, or hatch_cover), a non-empty project-practice composition basis, and B401 zones. Zone names will become globally unique during flattening and will retain component identity in results. In this branch a family will protect exactly one component; cross-component protection will be rejected and will remain the responsibility of #2261's explicit continuity graph. This restriction will make an isolated hatch cover auditable without treating a boolean as continuity evidence.

The schema will allow all four component types to compose submerged, buried, and concrete zones; it will not invent mandatory standard taxonomies for those project-defined labels. Mudmat/foundation sediment-facing outer areas will cite §6.9.2 for 2005, 2010, and 2017 or §3.9.3 for 2021. Bare buried demand will cite §6.3.8 for 2005, 2010, and 2017 or §3.3.8 for 2021; the narrower 2005/2010 paragraph locators were re-verified against their licensed prints for this plan and will be added without weakening the existing generic Sec. 6.3 compatibility label. Concrete reinforcement will cite §6.3.12/§6.3.13/Table 10-3 for 2005 and 2010, §6.3.12/§6.3.13/Table A-3 for 2017, or §3.3.12/§3.3.13/Table 8-3 for 2021. The output will preserve the edition distinction: 2005/2010/2017 frame the values as reinforcement protection, while 2021 frames them as structure-current drain rather than CP of reinforcement. Ordinary submerged/coated zones will retain the edition-specific #2262 table citations.

inputs.riser_base_assessment.phases[] will carry a unique phase name/type/life and families[] rows. Each row will name one existing anode family and provide that family's phase mean demand plus a mandatory basis record. Standard-derived demand will identify the cited calculated result; project-practice demand or duration will use project_practice with a non-empty reason. Phase and existing-system assessment will require a physical installed_count supplied by the input; the route will reject a family whose count was auto-sized by the design calculation. An explicit design-sizing mode may calculate a proposed installation, but it will not be reported as an existing system. Family-specific capacity, utilisation, and installed mass will come from the referenced family. Sequential consumption will use m_c = I_m t 8760 / epsilon. The result will preserve signed raw physical and usable balances as evidence, report zero available mass plus an over-consumption amount when either balance is negative, and mark the assessment evidence-inconsistent/exhausted. Future sizing will use available_usable = max(raw_usable, 0) and shortfall = max(m_future - available_usable, 0), so a historical deficit will not be supplied retroactively; additional gross mass will be shortfall/u. Inclusion of active pre-operation time in design life will cite §6.2.1 for 2005, 2010, and 2017 or §3.2.1 for 2021. No edition defines a separate wet-storage algorithm; phase segmentation, phase names, durations, and demand allocation will be explicit project practice.

inputs.riser_base_assessment.retrofit will be family-specific. The existing-system record will carry installed/original count and mass, age, historical mean demand, missing-anode allowance, and either measured remaining mass or an explicit inference basis. Existing output will use the calculated depleted-family output per remaining anode unless a separately based existing-output assessment is supplied; measured mass will not be treated as measured resistance. The proposed-retrofit record will carry mass, utilisation, explicit output basis, future life, and future demand. The result will report existing physical and usable mass, historical consumption, future usable requirement, mass-governing new count, output-governing new count, and the combined existing-plus-new output check. B401:2021 assessments will also require the available potential/current history and steel, coating, calcareous-deposit, marine-growth, and remaining-life condition basis under §7.4.1 and §7.5.3; unavailable observations will be explicit limitations. Editions 2005, 2010, and 2017 will cite §6.2.1 guidance and §7.13.3 documentation support and will label the remaining-mass method as project practice because those editions prescribe no retrofit algorithm.

A short output result will remain FAIL. Each optional accepted_output_shortfall record will bind to one exact family, phase, criterion, calculated demand, calculated output, ratio, non-empty owner reason, and project-practice basis. Validation will reject a record when the referenced calculated failure does not exist or its echoed values differ. The separate disposition will never change the family or top-level engineering verdict.

Mass design will cite §7.7.1 Eq. (2) for 2005, 2010, and 2017 or §4.7.1 Eq. (4.2) for 2021. Individual current output will cite §7.8.2 Eq. (3), fresh/depleted resistance §7.9.2, adequacy §7.8.4 Eqs. (5)–(7), and count/geometry selection §7.8.1/§7.8.5 for 2005, 2010, and 2017. The 2021 labels will be §4.8.2 Eq. (4.3), §4.9.2, §4.8.4 Eqs. (4.5)–(4.7), and §4.8.1/§4.8.5. These rule citations will supplement the resistance, utilisation, capacity, potential, and driving-voltage records already emitted by #2262. Every standard rule will emit an edition-specific citation record from the route; the pure kernels will continue returning numbers only. Component labels/grouping, separate phase scheduling, storage duration, historical-demand assumptions, condition evidence, retrofit inference, and exception acceptance will be explicit inputs rather than attributed to B401.

TDD sequence

  1. RED tests will define component flattening, zone/family composition, isolated hatch-cover behavior, and per-component totals.
  2. RED ledger tests will hand-calculate per-family consumption, signed and available physical/usable balances, shortfall, additional mass, exact exhaustion, and over-consumption/evidence-inconsistency behavior by reusing existing kernels; future sizing will prove that irrecoverable historical deficits are not added to future demand.
  3. RED route tests will define installation, wet-storage, operating, and retrofit ledgers, including a six-month temporary example and a two-year wet-storage example with different family capacities/utilisation.
  4. RED validation tests will reject duplicate component/phase names, cross-component family allocation, missing input-sourced installed count for an existing-system assessment, missing project-practice reasons, acceptance without reason, acceptance without an exact calculated shortfall, and mismatched echoed acceptance values.
  5. RED retrofit tests will distinguish measured from inferred remaining condition, existing from new output, mass- from output-governing counts, 2021 condition/history limitations, and the narrower 2017 support.
  6. RED report tests will require component composition, phase ledger, retrofit adequacy, citations, and an explicit accepted-shortfall block.
  7. Production code will be added only after each focused RED group is observed.

Acceptance criteria

  • Riser base, foundation, mudmat, and hatch cover will each be tested as project-defined compositions of submerged, buried, and concrete B401 zones with named component-local anode families.
  • Installation and wet-storage consumption will reduce each family's usable operating mass sequentially, with no cross-family consumption and no double application of utilisation.
  • Retrofit output will state consumed mass, physical remaining mass, usable remaining mass, future requirement, additional mass/count, and the governing mass/output case.
  • An accepted shortfall will remain an engineering failure and will include the explicit owner reason and project-practice basis.
  • All expected numbers will have hand derivations in test comments from B401 citations or stated input algebra.
  • Scoped cathodic-protection and reporting tests, ruff, and mypy will pass on touched files.
  • The private comparison will be written only to the authorized benchmark follow-up location and read back; tracked leakage scans and a numerical-fingerprint review will find no restricted identifiers, absolute private paths, or distinctive source tuples.
