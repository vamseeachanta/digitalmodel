Review dispositions and acceptance

  • Future serialized numeric readback will be parsed as Decimal, normalized for trailing zeros and compared exactly against canonical intent. Mismatch will fail diagnostic verification without an invented tolerance. Offline fixtures will test this rule without authenticating native state.
  • The builder will verify the bottom panel at z=-1 and four sides spanning z=-1 to z=0, including normals. Retain/reverse sets will derive from verified roles; any index-role mismatch or unclassifiable panel will refuse production. A role-permuted fixture will test refusal. The ledger will record roles, transformations, Git revision, dirty-state observation and original-byte digests.

Missing source frequency units will remain unknown; prescribed angular frequency will explicitly use rad/s. Tests will require every divergence entry, without implying source frequency conversion.
