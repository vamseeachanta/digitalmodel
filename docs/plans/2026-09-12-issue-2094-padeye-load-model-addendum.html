1. Decision and deliverable

The next study will assess numerical sensitivity of a prescribed bearing-pressure idealization for the 50 kN vertical-load example. It will introduce a separately versioned load case, explicit discrete normalization, three prescribed meshes and independent traction integration. It will retain the existing equal-nodal-force diagnostic case and its failed assessment unchanged. The outcome will be a numerical screening study; it will not establish pin-contact validation, a lifting capacity or automatic golden/registry promotion.

Approval will cover the proposed law, discrete normalization, mesh matrix and numerical sensitivity criteria below. The parent's existing plan-approved label will continue to describe its earlier scope; this addendum will require an explicit scope decision. Implementation will remain stopped until that decision is recorded.

3. Proposed method and assumptions

The model will retain the 400 × 300 mm plate, 8.000 mm thickness, 80 mm hole centered at (200, 220) mm, fixed base at y = 0, linear elasticity E = 205,000 MPa and Poisson ratio 0.3. The example yield strength will remain 355 MPa and design factor 1.67, with allowable 355/1.67 MPa. These will remain inherited demonstration inputs, not a certified material or standards-derived design basis. Load direction will be restricted to +Y for this new case; nonzero sling angles will fail before dispatch.

With θ measured from +X and 0 ≤ θ ≤ π, the continuous idealization will prescribe traction outward from the hole:

t(θ) = p0 sin(θ) [cos(θ), sin(θ)]
p0 = 2F / (π R t) = 99.4718394324 MPa
F = 50,000 N; R = 40 mm; t = 8.000 mm
∫ traction × thickness × arc length = [0, F]
Moment about hole center = 0; global-origin Mz = xc F = 10,000,000 N mm

The pressure will be represented as a linear-Y gradient on verified upper-hole element faces, using native pressure loading. Straight face normals will approximate the circular radial direction. The actual discrete unit-gradient resultant will be integrated from exported face connectivity, endpoint pressures and thickness. A common positive factor α = 50,000/Fy,unscaled will normalize the discrete vertical resultant. The implementation will report α and both pre- and post-normalization forces and moments for every mesh. Load magnitude, dimensions, edge lengths, thickness and the unscaled vertical resultant will be finite and strictly positive. Nonfinite values and malformed connectivity will fail. Zero endpoint pressures, zero horizontal force and zero sling angle will remain valid. Normalization will preserve the discrete pressure shape; it will not establish physical validity of that shape or repair a failed horizontal-force/moment check.

For a regular upper polygon with N equal-angle edges and linearly interpolated endpoint pressures, α = π/[N sin(π/N)] will provide an independent analytic fixture: 1.00645454, 1.00160819 and 1.00040171 for N = 16, 32 and 64. Actual edge integration will determine the applied factor; the fixture will not be assumed to describe an arbitrary mesh.

A symmetric annular-sector mesh with consistent topology and global refinement will be prepared. The nominal outer sizes will be 10, 5 and 2.5 mm; upper-hole edge counts will be 16, 32 and 64. Radial and outer-block subdivisions will double between levels. Mesh coordinates, connectivity and actual edge-length distributions will establish the refinement relationship. If the proposed topology cannot satisfy shape checks, the study will stop for a documented mesh-plan revision before stress capture; the matrix will not be tuned using stress outcomes.

Pre-capture restraint and quantity gate: a reviewed analytical screen will identify the clamped/free base corners and loaded-arc terminations, the local boundary conditions and the risk of singular stress. The full stress campaign will not start unless a nonsingular governing reported quantity is established through this screen and its review. The global peak will remain a retained diagnostic even if it cannot serve as that quantity. This addendum will not authorize substituting an arbitrary stress region. If the screen cannot establish a nonsingular quantity under the unchanged restraint, a separate quantity/restraint proposal will be returned for review and approval; the three stress runs will remain blocked. A preparation-only pressure-transfer probe will be permitted without SOLVE. The prospective 1% global-peak metric below will not discharge this prerequisite.

Boundary membership will be proved by element-edge incidence and geometric ownership, including a complete single upper arc and no interior or lower-edge loads. A node-radius band alone will not establish membership. The native pre-solve load listing will be checked against the independently integrated mesh export. A small native transfer probe will precede the three-case study and will verify face orientation, gradient transfer, thickness scaling and force/moment delivery.

6. Risks and stop conditions

The assumed pressure law, fixed base and plane-stress treatment will limit physical interpretation. Contact clearance, friction, pin compliance, cheek plates, weld stress, instability and out-of-plane loading will remain outside this example. Three mesh results will not prove asymptotic convergence or estimate physical error. A persistent global stress sensitivity, shape warning, failed load transfer or missing source binding will stop promotion and trigger a recorded finding rather than a relaxed threshold.

The entire 50 kN / 8.000 mm case, including geometry, material and restraint, will be frozen before native work. A sizing failure will not authorize re-parameterization under this addendum; any such proposal will require separate review and approval. Fixed/free restraint transitions will remain an explicit sensitivity source, not a resolved property of the pressure law.
