Process piping implementation plan

Scope: pressure design and line sizing. The implementation will add no copied standards tables or PDFs.

Method and interfaces

  • A process_piping package will expose immutable results, explicit SI units, finite-value validation and resolved Citation sidecars. Small packaged public-reference records will support calculation-time validation without a private wiki checkout.
  • B31.3 equation (3a) will use required S, E (Ej), W and Y inputs. Corrosion and mechanical allowances will precede division by the remaining mill-tolerance fraction. Thick-wall and high-pressure cases will be rejected.
  • The hydrostatic minimum will use 1.5 P and the temperature-dependent St/S ratio. Elastic hoop and longitudinal yield screens will bound a reduced target; a weaker component rating will make the proposed test infeasible. Elevation will require a separate point-by-point assessment.
  • Bolt-up will separate Appendix 2 m/y design loads from PCC-1-informed assembly/operating gasket stress checks. RF spiral-wound and RTJ contact areas, material values and flange limits will be supplied by the caller. Symmetric cross patterns including twelve bolts will provide staged cross passes and a final circular pass.
  • Line sizing will reuse the existing pipe catalog and Swamee–Jain function. Liquid calculations will include fittings and elevation. Gas calculations will use a low-drop inlet-density screen or the integrated horizontal isothermal momentum equation, including acceleration and choking rejection. Two-phase results will be limited to a homogeneous velocity/erosion screen, with explicit US-customary C-factor provenance.

Verification

Tests will precede implementation and will check NPS 6 pressure/hydro/bolt-up examples, the 50 m³/h NPS 4 Sch 40 water example, independent gas equation residuals, unit scaling, invalid inputs, transitions, yield reduction and failing gasket/bolt/flange constraints. The related field-development, materials and wall-thickness tests will run. A runnable example and short user guide will document limits and public references.

Adversarial plan assessment

The principal defect risks will be unit confusion, treating m/y as a leak-tightness guarantee, ignoring axial stress or component hydrotest limits, applying an incompressible gas result past 10%, and treating API RP 14E as a sand-erosion model. Required inputs, separate criteria and explicit rejection paths will address these risks.

Verification will use file readback, SHA-256 manifests and recorded pytest output. The public explorer page will be a follow-up outside this repository.
