ANSYS analysis lifecycle application

13 September 2026 · Issue 2119 · Client: N/A · Lane: codex · Complexity: T2 bounded adapter · Execution: parallel-readonly planning, single-writer implementation.

The application will implement the shared analysis criteria for ANSYS evidence packaging and lookup. Implementation will await approval of the reviewed plan. This scope will invoke no solver and will not launch parametric native runs. Existing issue 2094 and issue 2117 review and solve gates will remain separate.

Resource intelligence and boundaries

Implementation discovery will use AnsysWorkflow._run_ansys in src/digitalmodel/ansys/workflow.py for execution-status handling; DesignPointReader.read in design_points.py for imported study metadata; ResultsExtractor.parse_result_digest and export_results in results_extractor.py for extraction; and batch_runner.py for batch enumeration. Their existing interfaces will be preserved. A parsed design point or completed process will not establish engineering readiness.

The evidence baseline will be commit 9493f7b27dfa05670773b311123297754183f8f7 and its tracked docs/plans/evidence/2026-09-13-solvers-current-review-coverage.md. Historical pressure-vessel, padeye and mudmat records will undergo file-by-file eligibility assessment. Unresolved broad review findings will prevent affected responses entering qualified lookup. Reproduction proofs will be inapplicable to this planning artifact: no new runtime failure or numerical result will be asserted.

Source reconciliation will preserve IDs from the sibling wiki data/data-source-catalog.yml and data/domain-database-index.yml, following the hub data-handling contract. The 13 September drive-index query “ANSYS verification parametric” will be treated as incomplete discovery: five configured indexes were unreachable and no matches were returned from the available search. Source absence will not be inferred. This adapter will introduce no standards-derived constants; component calculations will retain their existing source/Citation gates.

Frozen qualification and intake gates

The criteria target will be ENG-ANALYSIS-CRITERIA Revision 1 at commit 9493f7b27dfa05670773b311123297754183f8f7, canonical SHA-256 1cebe3d4c8577b9795e67024a05a58bda7514c5d2334bea7d39f281f96af5bd0. Manifests will bind this target; a changed criteria target will require matrix re-review.

Before import, the implementation owner will freeze a tracked intake ledger enumerating every candidate artifact referenced by the issue 2094 pressure-vessel, padeye and mudmat evidence records at the baseline commit. Each entry will identify capture/case, source revision, files, known invalidity and disposition: eligible for diagnostic retention, ineligible with reason, or deferred. The ledger will include original and corrected captures without overwriting either. Missing referenced files will remain ledger entries. Stage A acceptance will require at least one real eligible retained capture imported and read back; synthetic fixtures alone or an empty selection will not satisfy it. Families lacking eligible evidence will remain explicit coverage gaps.

The implementation owner will also freeze a finding-to-response ledger for inherited issue 2094 and 2117 review findings. Unmapped findings will conservatively block promotion of all potentially affected records until applicability is resolved. Source qualification will be a separate blocking task: the owner will document available catalog evidence and alternatives for unreachable indexes, and establish identity, retention rights and intended-use rights for every imported record. Unresolved retention rights will prohibit copying even into diagnostic output; metadata-only dispositions will preserve the gap. Unresolved qualification or use rights will prohibit engineering promotion. Legal checks will cover every new imported artifact and manifest, not only Python files.

A non-author checker assigned before qualification will independently compare every proposed promoted response against retained evidence and its verification register, recording the result; the implementing session will not serve as that checker. Zero promotion will require an explicit zero-qualified report and the reason no qualification check was applicable. Required evidence will be digest-verified at import, qualification and each engineering lookup; unreachable or changed evidence will refuse the affected engineering answer. Diagnostic retrieval will retain the reason.

A separate append-only disposition ledger will support withdrawal by the engineering owner or delegated approver, recording authority, reason, evidence, timestamp and affected revision/response. Withdrawal will not modify the immutable dataset. Lookup will require a current readable disposition ledger and will refuse withdrawn records; historical diagnostic access will preserve them subject to source rights. Publication will use validated temporary output and atomic promotion. Size checks will precede publication and apply to actual serialized bytes; excess size or failed readback will leave no published/queryable partial revision.

If no real capture is eligible, Stage A will remain blocked with a complete eligibility/rights disposition ledger; acceptance criteria will not be relaxed and synthetic fixtures will not replace the required real import. The engineering owner will resolve access or approve an explicit scope revision. The non-author engineering checker will be named with competence, delegated authority and date in the register; provider code review alone will not satisfy that calculation check. Definitions, locations, comparators, tolerances and reference requirements will be independently justified and frozen before promotion evaluation, with historical visibility disclosed rather than claimed blind. The manifest will bind the register digest; changed criteria will require a new revision and renewed checking before affected results return to engineering lookup.

Catalog reconciliation will be read-only; Stage A will write no sibling catalogs or shared harness. Local manifests will preserve resolved catalog IDs and record unresolved registration as a discovery gap. The pinned criteria digest will cover the raw Git blob bytes of docs/standards/analysis-parametric-lookup-criteria.html at the stated commit, obtainable with git show COMMIT:PATH, without working-tree newline normalization. Response hashing will use a versioned canonical UTF-8 JSON encoding with sorted keys, explicit types, decimal-string numeric identity and distinct null/absent/empty/zero values; ambiguity and nonfinite values will be rejected.

The disposition ledger will use validated increasing sequence numbers and chained entry digests, with a separately retained current head/count in the publication authority record. Lookup will compare against that current trusted head, not only a historical dataset manifest; unavailable or inconsistent authority, truncation, reordering or rollback will refuse engineering lookup. Tests will cover these failures and withdrawal after dataset publication. The trust boundary will exclude an attacker controlling both the ledger and its authority record; this local adapter will not claim independent protection against that compromise.

Each intended use will declare mandatory evidence and disqualifying limitations. Missing required native evidence, unresolved required verification or withdrawn rights will make the affected response unqualified, regardless of other complete fields. Diagnostic retrieval will retain the limitation. Criteria changes will follow the governing document's transition/impact rules; no universal retroactive invalidation will be inferred solely from a newer criteria revision. Component mappings and proposed sequence

Pressure-vessel records will identify geometry, pressure/thermal loading and the applicable stress definition; padeye records will identify plate/hole/connection geometry, load direction and the qualified demand response; mudmat records will identify footprint, thickness, soil/contact idealization and settlement/reaction responses. These will be applicability mappings, not assertions that any existing case passes. Other ANSYS components, assemblies and structures will use the same contract with separately reviewed response definitions. MAPDL evidence will not automatically qualify Mechanical/Workbench exports.

Stage A will add an offline evidence-package adapter and exact lookup for retained cases. It will assess all three example families and import only ledger-eligible diagnostic records, promoting a response only when the frozen verification register demonstrates every requirement for its intended use. A zero-qualified-result outcome will be reported as such and will not satisfy engineering lookup readiness. Stage A software acceptance may still demonstrate correct refusal using labelled synthetic fixtures.

Stage B will prepare a component-specific native campaign under a separate reviewed case manifest. It will freeze physical parameter ranges, numerical convergence cases, independent check coverage, tolerances, storage estimates and licensed execution budget before any solve approval. No arbitrary common sample count will be imposed across components. The analytical padeye study will remain distinct from the ANSYS campaign.

Artifact map and processing contract

Stage A will propose src/digitalmodel/ansys/analysis_evidence.py, src/digitalmodel/ansys/analysis_lookup.py, corresponding tests/ansys/ modules and a neutral schema/query example under examples/ansys/analysis-evidence/. Existing readers will be reused without weakening their checks. Inspection will determine whether existing atlas utilities meet the identity/readiness contract before reuse; shared schema changes will trigger T3 replanning.

Processing will follow: validate study and source references → enumerate exact cases → bind retained files and digests → parse typed responses → attach independent verification evidence → evaluate response readiness → write immutable manifest/records → read back and verify → expose qualified exact lookup and separate diagnostic retrieval.

The manifest will bind criteria revision, dataset ID/revision, code/method/source revisions, canonicalization and intended-use requirements. Each response will carry case identity, units, definition/location, value or typed null, calculation and verification status, criterion/comparator where applicable, evidence references and limitations. Row hashes will include the complete load/source/model descriptor and response records. Published revisions will be immutable; changed inputs, dependencies or verification evidence will create a new revision. Supersession will preserve historical audit access and identify affected consumers.

Repository manifests and generation entry points will remain with the engineering owner. Authorized native files will remain at the residence required by the shared contract, with digests and access references; restricted originals will not be copied to public examples. A stored path will not count as a backup. Stage A output will stop for placement review above 5 MB per revision or 25 MB cumulative; it will not truncate cases. No new database service will be installed.

TDD, review and acceptance

RED tests will cover mismatched or missing dependencies, source/unit/model identity conflicts, duplicate and missing cases, duplicate response labels, empty digests, missing required responses, malformed omitted design-point cells, failed/dry-run/stale evidence, malformed/nonfinite/null values, incompatible stress definitions, partial verification, unchanged hashes versus changed provenance, off-grid keys and revision isolation. An ANSYS-looking synthetic fixture will never qualify an engineering answer. GREEN implementation will make those tests pass without solver access. An independent non-author check will compare imported values with retained source evidence; synthetic tests will establish adapter behavior only.

Acceptance will require all selected retained cases accounted for, zero implicit promotions, deterministic roundtrips and refusal behavior, the documentation-routing suite at tests/docs/test_digitalmodel_routing_contract.py for the plan-index link plus relevant ANSYS regressions, scoped legal/security scan and report readback. Before engineering qualification, the response verification register will specify case-specific tolerances and evidence; missing criteria will block that promotion. Claude will independently review exact-current Codex plan/code/artifacts, and Codex will review Claude-authored material. T2 will apply because changes will remain a bounded single-package adapter without shared schema changes. Findings and unavailable providers will be recorded; malformed review output will block rather than reduce review coverage. Implementation approval will not authorize native solves.

Risks will include incomplete native archives, incompatible result definitions, inherited unresolved reviews, source rights and mixed software versions. They will be controlled through diagnostic-only import, response-specific readiness and explicit new-revision boundaries. The next approval will cover Stage A only; Stage B will remain a separately planned campaign.
