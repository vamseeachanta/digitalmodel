1. Proposed decision and scope

Approval will authorize an offline-tested response generator and independent assessor, followed by at most two fine-level preparation invocations and three linear-static response invocations, one per 10, 5 and 2.5 mm level. Each invocation will have a 900-second timeout and use one core and the existing exclusive licensed-seat procedure. A failed or interrupted invocation will consume its attempt and stop the campaign; no automatic retry, extra mesh or load adjustment will be authorized.

The frozen 400 × 300 mm plate, 8.000 mm thickness, E=205,000 MPa, ν=0.3 and full base clamp will remain unchanged. PLANE182 KEYOPT(3)=3 with real constant t=8.000 will represent total-through-thickness forces in N. The hole will be centred at (200,220) mm with R=40 mm. Only the UPPER arc, θ=0…π measured from +X, will carry p=p₀ sinθ radially outward from the hole centre, delivering total F=(0,+50,000) N. Positive σyy at the y=90 section will therefore denote tension. Both upper and lower semicircles share endpoints; their endpoints alone will not define load ownership. The existing preparation-only generator will remain free of solution commands. The approved coarse preparation will be reused only after byte and semantic revalidation. New fine preparation will precede any fine solve. Every response will import a checked native database into a fresh run directory; no solver will consume an unverified candidate database.

The proposed governing numerical-response quantity will be work-derived compliance. A recovered section-average axial stress will provide a separate equilibrium comparator. Neither quantity will replace global equivalent stress as a material-strength criterion. Global peaks, all competing maxima and complete listings will remain retained diagnostics. The original D2 sizing and golden/registry gates will remain unresolved regardless of a passing response study.

3. Analytical screen and restraint disposition

The screen will classify the clamped/free transitions at (0,0) and (400,0) as local singularity risks. It will not infer a nonsingular global stress maximum from a distributed pressure load or from three finite peaks. Loaded-arc endpoints (160,220) and (240,220) will be recorded: the prescribed pressure will reach zero there, but that fact alone will not validate the complete stress field. Polygonal-hole geometry will also change with refinement and will remain part of the numerical discretization study.

The reference section will run from (0,90) to (400,90) mm, halfway between the clamped base and the lowest hole point at y=180 mm. Its location will be fixed before stress capture. The existing frozen generator will compute a distinct discrete alpha for each mesh against the same continuum law and total target; no coarse alpha will be copied to a finer mesh. It will cross neither the hole nor a loaded boundary, and will remain 90 mm from the base and hole. Equilibrium in the unloaded full-width plate will give the analytical reference; the location will not be chosen from observed low-stress results.

Before native dispatch, offline tests will verify loaded-face ownership on the upper arc, total-force magnitude and sign, thickness representation, independently recomputed per-level normalization alpha, and mirrored nodes/pressures about x=200 mm within the frozen coordinate precision. Native force and moment checks will independently verify the imported state at every level.

The full UX=UY=0 base clamp will remain a stated idealization. A later UY-only base plus midpoint UX restraint would change lateral contraction and physical support behavior. This proposal will not authorize that change, spring tuning, a fillet, plasticity or load reduction. A physical restraint recommendation will require supporting-structure/weld stiffness evidence that is currently unestablished.

QuantityDefinition and proposed interpretation
Primary numerical response: C (mm/N)W = ∫loaded boundary t p n·u ds; Cᵢ = Wᵢ/Fy,ᵢ², with Fy,ᵢ the positive independently audited native applied Y resultant for that mesh, still required to meet the 50,000 N target gate. This normalization will separate residual load-amplitude differences from compliance sensitivity. An independent two-point Gauss rule on each straight native face will integrate its linearly varying pressure and linearly interpolated nodal displacement. 7. Review, risks and approval boundary

The review disposition and resource-intelligence receipt will accompany approval review. Historical provider verdicts will remain preserved: Codex MINOR, Claude r1 MAJOR and r2 MINOR, followed by inline corrections.

Claude and Codex will perform adversarial T2 plan and code/artifact review. Missing authentication will be UNAVAILABLE; a completed artifact without a parseable verdict will be INVALID_OUTPUT and block acceptance. Main-session corrections will retain original verdicts and identify what was corrected. Plan-stage review will not count as code-stage review.

The 50 N and 2,000 N mm native bounds will preserve the reviewed preparation/equilibrium contract; the section bounds will be separately proposed recovery targets with a 10% sampling allocation (5 N and 200 N mm). Neither will claim to be a guaranteed FE discretization bound. Actual residuals will be reported even when below a gate. The energy 0.1% and compliance 1% limits will remain proposed diagnostic targets, not derived solver error estimates. A recovery failure will be a useful failed diagnostic, not a reason to relax the test.

Global peak trends will be tabulated by mesh and physical region. Increasing peaks persisting at a clamped/free corner will support investigation of a local singularity risk, but three values will neither prove a singular exponent nor establish a finite limit. Stable or migrating peaks will not disprove the risk. The response-study pass will be independent of that unresolved physical classification.

A stopped campaign will be labelled INCOMPLETE or FAILED with its available rows retained; missing C3 will prohibit a three-level sensitivity verdict. No implicit budget restoration will follow an output-format failure. A later amendment would require explicit approval of any additional invocations. The operator session will own timeout disposition, check process exit for up to 60 seconds after the existing bounded stop procedure, and notify the user if the seat remains occupied; no lock release or unrelated process termination will be inferred.

T2 will apply because this will be one neutral example within the existing solver, operator and evidence architecture, with two-provider independent review. No new fleet scheduler, shared authorization framework or cross-provider deployment will be introduced. If implementation requires such shared changes, the scope will stop for a T3 proposal.

Native outputs will be generated by the owned neutral verification model; they will not be client-supplied or measured originals. Local retention will not be called backup. If client-supplied/measured sources are later introduced, their separate raw-source commit contract will apply in the owning private repository; this diagnostic will not invoke a vendor-standards exemption for solver outputs.

Code-stage cross-review will attempt the default third provider where the runtime supports it, recording an actual unavailability if needed; an existing artifact with invalid output will remain blocking. The two-provider T2 plan review will remain separate from that code-stage routing.

The principal risk will be confusing a numerically stable global response with a qualified local stress. The report and assessor will prohibit that promotion. Path recovery and FE discretization will be reported separately. Pressure normalization and energy consistency will not create an independent physical comparator. The present restraint will remain hypothetical until supporting-structure evidence is available.

Approval will authorize only this bounded diagnostic implementation and its five-invocation maximum. It will explicitly supersede the no-SOLVE restriction for these separately identified response captures, while preserving the original global-stress sizing block. Without approval, only planning and read-only evidence review will proceed. No label will be self-applied to imply approval.
