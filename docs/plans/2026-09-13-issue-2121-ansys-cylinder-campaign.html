Proposed ANSYS four-run response canary

Issue 2121 · T2 · parallel-readonly planning · Client: not applicable · B1 user approval pending; exact-current checkpoint receipts will govern review status.

The plan will prepare one fixed cylindrical-wall case at three meshes and one zero-pressure control. Separate B2 approval will authorize at most four native attempts. Numerical feasibility will be determined from those observations against fixed limits. Current checkpoint receipts will govern review status; no review verdict will authorize B1 implementation or B2 execution. The former 28-attempt campaign, lookup publication and capacity qualification will remain deferred.

Deliverable and approval boundary

B1 will produce four deterministic PLANE183 decks, a strict response extractor, an independently checked analytical reference, immutable criteria, an offline-tested four-attempt operator and a preparation report. It will invoke no ANSYS executable. B1 approval will adopt the geometry, method, response definitions and fixed limits in the machine-readable canary proposal. B2 approval will separately identify exact generated-deck, source, reference, extraction and criteria hashes, four-attempt order, operator/checker identities, executable/profile and storage/license preflight. Neither approval will authorize a fifth attempt, revised tolerances or the deferred parametric campaign.

The canary will answer whether this MAPDL formulation and recovery procedure meets the stated numerical limits at this one physical point. PASS will not establish physical validation, asymptotic convergence, material suitability, ASME vessel compliance or allowable capacity. FAIL or INCOMPLETE will be valid engineering outcomes requiring a later reviewed disposition, rather than justification for adjusting the limits to the observations.

The scope and source record will preserve pinned baseline hashes and review-finding dispositions. No current runtime defect or native result will be asserted by this planning exercise.

An adjacent B1 disposition record will retain the unqualified allowable/mesh-attribution limitations at the point of use. Historical raw captures and review receipts will remain immutable.

Fixed basis and response protocol

The proposed geometry will use a=750 mm, t=60.000 mm, b=810 mm and L=240 mm. Material inputs will be mathematical E=200000 MPa and ν=0.3; internal pressure will be 10 MPa for three meshes and zero for the control; outside pressure and temperature change will be zero. PLANE183 eight-node quadrilaterals will retain every midside node, with KEYOPT(1)=0, KEYOPT(3)=1, KEYOPT(6)=0 and NLGEOM OFF. X will be radial, Y axial, Z hoop, and tension positive. All y=0 nodes will have UY=0; radial displacement and the upper edge will remain free. No end-cap load will be applied.

Execution order will be the N=16 zero control first, then pressure cases with N=4,8,16 radial divisions and 6N axial divisions. The mapped meshes will contain 96/384/1536 elements and 345/1265/4833 nodes respectively; the control will reuse the finest topology. The primary stations will be r=a, a+t/2 and b at y=120 mm. Auxiliary stations will use the same radii at y=60 and 180 mm. All sampled nodes will be corner nodes on every mesh: inner/outer stations will share two elements and middle stations four. A different node type, coordinate or adjacency count will refuse extraction.

Seven quantities at each primary station will provide 21 primary values per attempt: σr, σθ, σz, τrz, σvm, ur and uz. The two auxiliary planes will add 42 diagnostic/check values. SX/SZ/SY/SXY will map to radial/hoop/axial/rz shear; UX/UY will supply displacements. With ERESX YES set before solution, RSYS=0, full graphics and all homogeneous elements selected, element-based extrapolated nodal components will be averaged arithmetically over all adjacent elements at each sampled corner. Acceptance values will use per-node/component *GET values (S queries with explicit ESOL) and *VWRITE with E24.16, one response per line within the 128-character line limit specified by *VWRITE. PRNSOL listings at the explicit high-precision format below will remain independent checks, not acceptance-value inputs. Each attempt will retain a numeric formatting witness and the requested/observed precision; overflow, truncation, missing precision or export half-quantum greater than the applicable dimensional floor divided by 100 will refuse evidence. Exact-decimal arithmetic will preserve the exported values. Element-node contributions and full nodal listings will independently support the declared mean; unexpected recovery behavior will fail rather than silently change the definition. Equivalent stress will be recomputed after averaging components. Inner/outer radial stress will assess traction recovery; applied-load evidence will separately verify the loaded inner faces, sign, pressure magnitude, outer unloaded faces and absence of axial loads. Global radial FSUM will not substitute for this load audit.

Independent analytical reference and adequacy

The producer will calculate A=pa²/(b²−a²), B=pa²b²/(b²−a²); σr=A−B/r², σθ=A+B/r² and σz=τrz=0. It will calculate ur=((1−ν)Ar+(1+ν)B/r)/E and uz=−2νAy/E, with σvm²=((σr−σθ)²+(σθ−σz)²+(σz−σr)²)/2+3τrz². Definition-based zero channels will include outer radial stress and all axial/shear stresses; floating-point cancellation will not decide the zero branch.

A distinct provider, in a fresh nonpersistent session, will receive a separately hashed checker brief containing only geometry, material, load, restraints, requested coordinates and primary vendor references. The brief will exclude this plan's comparator formulas, producer code and response-definition files. A fresh, nonpersistent checker session will inherit no conversation and will have all filesystem, shell and network tools disabled. Its sole input will be the hashed physics brief. The register will retain provider/CLI version, tools-disabled invocation, prompt hash, absence of tool calls and verbatim output hash. If that isolation cannot be enforced, the independent-reference gate will remain unestablished and B2 approval will remain blocked. Checker and producer derivations will be hashed and committed before comparison. The B1 register will record identities, source facts, derivation hashes, comparison and disagreements. An owner-appointed competent checker/adjudicator, independent of the producer, will sign the exact B2 evidence package before execution approval; an AI review verdict alone will not confer engineering authority.

The 1% response limit will test component acquisition to one-percent accuracy at the stated stations; it will reject a 5% gain error on a nonzero reference. The zero-stress limit of 0.010 MPa will reject a 0.100 MPa false residual. Axial contraction/radial displacement checks will discriminate the open-end branch from plane strain or closed-end alternatives at ν=0.3. B1 mutation tests will substantiate those specific detection claims, including both signs, reversed pressure, unit errors and component swaps. The 0.5% refinement limit will bound the last mesh change; it will not prove asymptotic convergence. The axial-sum criterion will screen unintended axial loading on an explicit pressure-area scale, not establish round-off or exclude cancelling nodal errors; the individual distribution will be retained diagnostically. The checker will verify support-set identity, listing/export consistency and the frozen aggregate rule, without adding a discretionary numerical threshold to PASS.

Native sigma_z and τrz will be definition-zero, so this canary will not independently validate their mappings with nonzero signals; that gap will carry forward to any later study. The control will screen unintended nonzero loading, thermal contamination or stale outputs only when they produce nonzero responses. It will not prove geometry, supports or addressing that still yield zero. In the analytical axial-stress cancellation, the two Hooke-law terms will be approximately +48.539 and −48.539 MPa; resolving 0.010 MPa will require about 0.000206 relative agreement of those terms. These will be analytical scales, not predicted native errors. A failed zero-channel limit will remain FAIL; later investigation will distinguish modelling, FE displacement and recovery/precision contributions under a new reviewed basis.

These limits will be approval-scope assumptions tied to the detection goals above, independent of future observed performance. No initial freeze or subsequent revision will loosen any value to fit a canary result. Any change to element, key options, mesh, station, extraction, criterion or budget will require amended review and matching user approval before preparation or execution proceeds.

Frozen retrieval and reference guards

The deck will set OUTRES ERASE, ALL/ALL and NAR/NONE, and will retain OUTRES STAT. Stress queries will use *GET,par,NODE,node,S,component,ESOL explicitly; the *GET reference will govern that selector. The single-step solve will use NSUBST=1 and AUTOTS OFF. After SET,1,1, the active result-file count, load step and substep will each equal one. Selected node/element counts will match the proposal. Each keyed native record will include case token, node number, actual X/Y, component and value; X/Y will match the requested positive station coordinates within 10⁻⁹ mm. Missing or invalid retrieval will be INCOMPLETE, never a default zero.

The proposal will fix a 96-column export format using A8 case/component tokens, F8.0 integer-valued node IDs and three E24.16 coordinate/value fields. The extractor will bind the token to the manifest case and map by key; it will reject missing, duplicated, unexpected, truncated or interleaved records while allowing reordered complete records. Each attempt will supply 54 native keyed component/DOF values at nine nodes and nine derived equivalent stresses. The independent stress and displacement listings will each contain those nine node rows with complete component labels. Neither an empty listing nor a formatting witness alone will establish result retrieval.

/FORMAT will be fixed at 8,E,24,16,100,240 and recorded with STAT. For native export versus listing and element-node mean, the fixed readback limit will be max(10⁻¹² × max absolute compared/contributing value, dimensional floor/100). Displacements will compare directly with the nodal listing. Every representation half-quantum will be at most readback-limit/100. This arithmetic-rounding allowance will not alter the separate engineering limits. B1 will test deliberately substituted recovery and low-precision listings for refusal.

The warning allowlist will be empty. Any native WARNING, ERROR or FATAL diagnostic, or unparsed diagnostic severity, will cause INCOMPLETE and stop the lane. Informational status lines will be retained without acting as numerical gates. A benign warning may stop this strict canary; any future allowlist will require amended review and approval and will not retroactively pass this result.

The isolated checker brief will inline factual inputs and source metadata only; URLs will provide attribution rather than instructions to retrieve inaccessible pages. No vendor prose or result table will be needed. Producer/checker agreement will require the same branch, frame and units, with absolute differences at most 10⁻¹⁰ MPa and 10⁻¹² mm across all nine stations for pressure and control. These differences will compare deterministic evaluations of the independently authored expressions; they will not require unaided twelve-digit arithmetic from the provider. A disagreement will block reference acceptance and B2 until the owner-appointed competent independent adjudicator resolves it on recorded evidence. The producer will not unilaterally select its own result. Changing the analytical basis or an already-approved reference hash will reopen review and matching approval; B1 will still permit test-driven fixes that implement the unchanged approved formulas before reference freeze.

The 0.010 MPa auxiliary limit will be an additional governing constraint, approximately 0.000077 of inner hoop stress and 0.000083 of outer hoop stress. The y=60 mm plane will lie one wall thickness from the restrained row. The limit will detect changes above that absolute magnitude, not every addressing error in a y-invariant field. Auxiliary FAIL will carry the same cause-investigation and no-relaxation rule as expected-zero FAIL. B1 will record each nonzero channel's relative scale from the independent reference; those scales will not redefine the fixed bands.

Checkpoint corrections for independent review

The checker brief will include exact input symbols a,b,p,E,nu,r,y; operators +,−,*,/, integer ** and sqrt; finite decimal literals; and a description of the required output schema. The checker will return the JSON object containing its seven response expressions and derivation; the brief will contain no response expressions. Unary plus and minus, integer literals and parenthesized grouping will be accepted. ASCII whitespace will be accepted around the response and between expression tokens; internal CR/LF will become spaces without joining tokens. The complete response will be one bare JSON object with exactly the specified keys; fences, prose, duplicate/extra keys and BOM will be refused. Numeric literals will accept exact decimal exponent notation. Power will bind before unary signs, then multiplication/division, then addition/subtraction; the latter binary operators will associate left. Signed integer exponents will accept r**-2 and r**(-2); ungrouped chained powers will be refused. The proposal will specify the complete syntax and required B1 identity/refusal tests. Provider syntax conformance will remain unestablished until invocation. Raw output will remain unchanged in the evidence record. B1 identity tests will cover these rules before invocation. Helper expressions will be inlined, and unknown syntax will be refused. Evaluator source, grammar, schema, tests and the complete brief will be hashed and committed before exactly one checker invocation. Every invocation, including failed launch, will be registered with provider/version, timestamp and prompt/verbatim-output hashes. Parse failure, missing registration or unusable output will leave the gate unestablished. No syntax retry, provider resampling or output-driven evaluator tuning will be permitted; another attempt will require adjudication and renewed approval.

/CONFIG,RESUPREC,0 will be applied before any result is written. At initial Begin level, RESUPREC=0 will be set before result writing. After /SOLU settings and the load audit, the sequence will be FINISH; /CONFIG,STAT; /SOLU; SOLVE; FINISH; /CONFIG,STAT; /POST1; SET,1,1. Both Begin-level captures will show zero; missing/conflicting status or later override will refuse evidence. FINISH will preserve database data, with no clear/reset between entries. B1 tests will require both correctly placed captures and refuse one-capture or nonzero fixtures. The pinned v242 definition will establish double-precision result storage. Exact binary32 representability will not be used to infer storage precision, because exact values and zeros can be stored in either precision and averaging can change representability. Original numerical limits will remain fixed; storage precision will not establish FE accuracy or exclude cancellation error.

The pre-solve load audit will use SFELIST,ALL,ALL, FLIST and DLIST under full selection. Element/face keys will map to connectivity and coordinates; only the intended inner pressure will be present, with correct sign and magnitude, no outer/top/bottom surface loading and no applied nodal force. The complete bottom-row UY set will be verified separately. SFELIST will describe the current database before the fixed status/re-entry sequence, not reconstructed POST1 history. After SFELIST/FLIST/DLIST, only FINISH; /CONFIG,STAT; /SOLU; SOLVE will be permitted before solution. No load, constraint, selection or solution-control mutation will occur in that window; B1 tests will reject an inserted mutation. B1 tests will refuse wrong faces, signs, magnitudes and incomplete records.

Process stderr and process stdout outside the native output file will be byte-empty under the strict profile. Nonempty streams will cause INCOMPLETE even with exit zero; an incompatible launcher will require reviewed amendment rather than an after-run waiver. Independent-reference agreement will be a preflight prerequisite before invocation; failure will consume no attempt. Every attempt will verify the approved reference hash, without repeating the checker.

The diagnostic characterization will provide exact distinct headings, source-body identities, offsets, class counts, counting method and Python version for B1 fixtures. All 217 observed headings will comprise 210 WARNING and seven NOTE occurrences, including duplicate output/error channels. Native ERROR/FATAL coverage will remain unestablished. Future warning reachability will remain conditional on generated-deck verification. The empty warning allowlist will be retained as a strict canary choice; B1 will test recorded identities and offsets as well as counts.

The zero-control deck will contain no SFE/SF surface-load records and no nodal-force records. Its complete SFELIST/FLIST evidence will show no loads; that empty-record state will be required. Missing or truncated listing artifacts will still refuse evidence. The pressure cases will require exactly the intended 10 MPa inner-face records. B1 fixtures will distinguish these branches and reject unexpected zero-valued SFE records in the control.

Neither pinned historical run will provide retained native process stdout/stderr evidence: inspected files and receipts will establish unavailable capture, not empty streams. B1 approval will adopt byte-empty streams as an explicit unverified launcher assumption. Incompatible B2 output will cause INCOMPLETE without waiver. The existing runner will currently supply decoded strings; B1 will add opt-in binary capture for the canary while preserving default callers. Raw bytes will be retained and hashed before strict decoding. RED tests will cover exact CRLF/non-UTF8 retention, empty versus missing streams, timeout partial bytes and the existing interface. No native launch will occur in B1.

Native execution, residence and closeout

B2 will be limited to four attempts, one core with SMP, 300 seconds per attempt, zero automatic retries and 30 minutes total wall time. An attempt will be consumed immediately before invoking the solver, including launch failures; a preflight refusal will consume none. Insufficient remaining time for the full next attempt will stop with INCOMPLETE. The operator will record the exact executable locator/digest, requested runtime profile, authoritative approval and checker identities, successful license query and exclusive seat ownership, existing processes, unused output directories, free storage and source-specific retention rights. Independent adjudication will verify the frozen numerical rules and evidence consistency; it will not invent thresholds or waive failed criteria. Actual product/version will be checked from native logs against the approved profile; executable naming will not establish it.

The preflight storage criterion will require available space above the approved capture allowance plus an explicit reserve; B1 will propose numerical values using retained comparable capture sizes, and the B2 preview will freeze them before approval. Unknown size/rights or insufficient storage will refuse execution. A timeout will terminate only the owned process tree; uncertain ownership or failed termination will stop the lane and retain the seat pending operator disposition. Unrelated processes will not be terminated.

Each attempt will retain input, stdout/stderr, exit/timeout/duration, native log/error/monitor/result files, mesh/material/load/support listings, station CSV, independent component/displacement listings, element-node recovery contributions and constrained RF/PRRSOL evidence. Required artifacts will carry case IDs, units, versions, SHA-256 hashes, timestamps and readback evidence. Missing files will remain missing, not zero. Failed attempts will be preserved. Private-original retention and licensed-source exclusions will follow the owning repository's data contract; public output will require explicit source-specific rights. Local saves, private Git retention, backup and publication will be reported separately.

The digitalmodel repository will own the canary interpretation. It will retain evidence outside production lookup; dataset registration and response qualification will remain deferred. A future parametric plan will consider the canary outcome and will independently approve its matrix and promotion rules. Plan and later code/artifacts will each receive exact-current independent Claude/Codex review. Relevant offline tests, engineering-register check, legal verification, issue publication and scoped cleanup audit will accompany B1 closeout. The current request will seek B1 preparation approval only, after the revised plan review clears its blocking findings.
