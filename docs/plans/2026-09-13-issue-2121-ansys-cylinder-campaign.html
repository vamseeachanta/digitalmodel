Proposed ANSYS open-cylinder verification campaign

Blocked draft. B1 implementation will remain held pending resolution of the recovery-feasibility method and independent error-budget rules. Final review status: Claude MAJOR; Codex MINOR. The technical proposal below will require revision and renewed exact-current review before an implementation approval request.

Issue 2121 · T2 · Proposed B1 offline implementation; B2 native execution will require separate approval.

The campaign will verify numerical responses of an open-ended elastic cylindrical wall. It will not qualify an ASME vessel, material allowable, end cap, nozzle, weld, instability limit or service capacity. No proposed case will represent an executed result.

Purpose, resources and selection

The implementation will extend the Stage A evidence lifecycle with a component-specific analytical comparator and a finite parametric example. It will use baseline 97a152c33e54c397f3d82802b860ef4a06c68c47 and the pinned source inventory in the resource-intelligence record. The separate padeye proposal will retain its own geometry/load-model approval gate. Mudmat qualification will require a separate soil-response comparator.

The verification method will be checked against three primary vendor references: ANSYS VM38, 2024 R2 for elastic/plastic modelling background; ANSYS VM25, 2024 R2 for pressure-only radial displacement and radial/hoop stress comparisons; and the PLANE183 reference, 2024 R2 for axisymmetric axes, formulation and force basis. These will support independently derived equations, not a claim of identical vendor fixtures. The campaign will omit VM25 rotation and VM38 plasticity. Vendor deck listings or licensed standards PDFs will not be copied into the repository.

Source discovery will remain bounded by the recorded catalog scope and unavailable drive indexes. B1 will resolve reuse/retention rights and the authoritative dataset owner before registering a source or dataset. Missing source or rights evidence will block affected publication and qualification; a zero search count will not establish absence across the ecosystem.

Prior-artifact disposition and ownership

The campaign will coexist with the retained pressure-vessel example under disjoint response definitions. It will preserve pressure_vessel.py, pv.inp, the historical golden provenance and its acceptance tests without promoting their claims. The retained t=60.000 mm, p=10 MPa example will be treated as overlapping geometry/pressure only: its uniform ΔT=175 °C, global peak stress and FSUM diagnostics will not substitute for the new ΔT=0 station responses. No historical station-response identity will be reused. The mesh-error attribution and allowable/capacity statements in that provenance will remain unqualified under Stage A.

The digitalmodel repository will own the proposed cylinder verification dataset and its versioned response definitions; the shared catalogs will hold references rather than duplicate datasets. The responsible owner authority and checker identities will be recorded before B2. Case IDs will use the ocv- namespace, qualified by dataset ID and revision. B1 will check for collisions in the existing examples, golden records and accessible catalogs, and will stop registration if ownership or identity remains unresolved. It will add an adjacent disposition record at the retained golden example and test that the unqualified capacity/mesh-attribution limitations and pointer to this separate verification scope remain visible at the point of use; the historical raw capture will remain immutable.

The case proposal will define nine physical cases at three nested meshes and one zero-pressure control (t=60.000 mm, N=16): 28 native attempts maximum. Structured quadratic quadrilaterals will include y=120 mm and all three radial sampling stations. Element aspect ratios will range from 1.125 to 2.25. The first four attempts will be t=60.000 mm, p=10 MPa at N=4,8,16, then the zero control. The initial four-attempt gate will require execution/evidence checks on every attempt, finest-mesh accuracy including expected-zero components, refinement checks and zero-control acceptance. Coarse-mesh accuracy and zero-component discrepancies alone will remain diagnostic. Failure of a mandatory check will stop the campaign before the remaining 24 attempts.

Independent comparator and response definitions

The implementation will derive the open-ended Lamé solution using a=inner radius, b=a+t, r=station radius, y=axial coordinate and p=internal pressure. Tension will be positive. It will calculate A=pa²/(b²−a²), B=pa²b²/(b²−a²), σr=A−B/r², σθ=A+B/r², σz=τrz=0, ur=((1−ν)Ar+(1+ν)B/r)/E and uz=−2νAy/E. Equivalent stress will be calculated from the same recovered tensor components, with σvm²=((σr−σθ)²+(σθ−σz)²+(σz−σr)²)/2+3τrz².

Each case will produce 21 named responses: σr, σθ, σz, τrz, σvm, ur and uz at r=a, a+t/2 and b, all at y=120 mm. Names will encode the station; definitions will include coordinates, axes, units, nodal averaging and extraction method. Global SX/SY/SZ will map to radial/axial/hoop stress respectively; SXY will map to τrz. Displacements will use nodal UX/UY. Stresses will use extrapolated nodal components averaged over selected adjacent elements in the full homogeneous mesh; the stored equivalent stress will be recomputed from those components. No maximum sorted stress will substitute for a station response or membrane classification.

An independent checker will derive or implement the reference outside the deck generator and extractor. B1 tests will independently recompute the VM25 pressure-only fixture from its stated inputs (a=4 in, b=8 in, E=30×10⁶ psi, ν=0.3 and p=30000 psi), and will corroborate it against the cited targets at their stated precision. The fixture will preserve bottom UY=0 and an axially coupled but otherwise free top, with no end-cap load. It will not supply an axial-displacement target; the checker will establish that branch by an independent constitutive derivation. Any stored vendor target will require an explicit permitted-use disposition; otherwise only independently recomputed numbers and source references will be stored. Before B2, the checker will sign the comparator version, tolerances, response definitions and competence/authority record. A self-generated fixture alone will not establish engineering validation.

B1 will add axial-condition discrimination tests at ν=0.3: the open-ended reference will satisfy εz=−2νA/E, while plane strain (εz=0, σz=2νA) and closed ends (σz=A, εz=(1−2ν)A/E) will fail the intended axial-response criteria. Mutation tests will replace each axial branch and require failure. The independent checker will derive the axial strain from three-dimensional Hooke law and the zero axial-traction boundary, separately from the producer. B1 will also test radial equilibrium by independent quadrature of ∫aᵇ σθ dr = pa and the identity σr+σθ=2A.

Auxiliary stations at y=60 and 180 mm will retain diagnostic stresses and displacements at the same three radii; these 42 additional quantities per case will stay outside the 21-response lookup set. At N=16, stress differences from the midplane will satisfy the expected-zero stress band; ur differences and uz deviations from linearity in y will satisfy max(0.01×|the applicable analytic displacement|,10⁻⁹ mm). Failure will block qualification and require review of boundary effects. Coarse auxiliary records will remain diagnostic.

Offline feasibility hold before execution criteria are frozen

B1 will begin with an independent formulation/recovery feasibility register. PLANE183 will be the proposed element because its quadratic displacement field will reduce interpolation error at the surface; neither interpolation estimates nor the published VM25 fixture will establish a native pass for this geometry. The register will distinguish exact-field interpolation estimates from FE displacement error, B-bar alternatives, nodal extrapolation, averaging and axial variation. Table 2 will remain candidate outer limits until that register supports their feasibility. If feasibility remains unestablished, B1 will stop at a reviewed feasibility report; no approved B2 deck set or frozen execution criteria will be claimed. Changing element, mesh, response location or tolerance after that checkpoint will require an amended reviewed basis and matching user approval.

The independent checker register will open at B1. A distinct provider/session will receive only the physics statement, boundary conditions and source references, without the producer implementation. Producer and checker derivations/code will be hashed and committed separately before comparison; identities, competence/authority, source versions, hashes and disagreements will be recorded. B2 will additionally require the owner-authorized checker signature. Provider review alone will not confer engineering checker authority.

The version-pinned source fact register will support the cited input facts; vendor targets will retain their separate storage-rights gate. The case proposal will provide candidate response mappings, formulation, units and criteria for generator/checker consumption. Any discrepancy with this plan will stop implementation until reconciled.

Execution, retention and lookup controls

B2 will require explicit approval for up to 28 native attempts, 300 seconds per attempt, zero automatic retries and 180 minutes total wall time. Failures will count toward the cap. A timeout, missing evidence or any mandatory response, control, refinement or equilibrium failure will stop further execution; per-key qualification will describe retained evidence after that stop and will not authorize continued attempts. A transient failure will consume an attempt and require renewed approval for completion; resumption, replacement cases or mesh changes will require disposition and renewed approval. ANSYS version, license availability, operator, checker independence, available storage and output paths will be recorded before the first attempt. Changed solver versions or response definitions will create a new dataset revision.

The proposed dataset identifier ansys-open-cylinder-verification will be registered only after catalog collision and ownership checks. Immutable physical inputs, generated decks, logs, result files and extracted responses will carry stable case IDs, SHA-256 digests, units, provenance, source versions, freshness and intended-use readiness. Raw measured/client data will belong in the owning private repository; vendor-licensed binaries/documents will remain at licensed storage with permitted references. Rights-compatible neutral outputs will be published only after the rights review. Local saves, repository publication and backups will be reported separately.

Only the finest-mesh records for the nine physical cases will enter the proposed production lookup revision: at most 189 separately qualified response entries. All 28 attempts will remain in an execution/refinement ledger, including failed and zero-control attempts. The exact physical identity will include geometry, pressure, elastic constants, thermal condition and formulation/boundary version; the response identity will include station and definition. Identity quantities will reuse Stage A canonical exact-decimal strings in explicit mm/MPa units: finite strings will normalize trailing zeros without float conversion; numeric JSON values and mismatched units will be refused. For example, string inputs 60, 60.0 and 60.000 will normalize to the same decimal key, while 61 or 7.51 MPa will not be rounded onto the grid. Tests will verify these equality and refusal rules. N=16 will be fixed in the production revision provenance. A request for another mesh or off-grid physical input will be refused, not rounded or interpolated.

Qualification will require live evidence digests, independent row/reference checks, resolved inherited findings, source/rights readiness and explicit owner authority. Numerical pass alone will not promote a row. The implementation will retain unqualified results with their failure reasons. Lookup will remain restricted to verification use and will not return vessel design allowables or capacity conclusions.

Review, approval and closure

The exact current plan and case proposal will receive independent Claude and Codex adversarial review. Blocking findings will be resolved before an approval request. Review records will identify revisions, hashes, verdicts, findings and dispositions. Code/artifact review will form a separate B1 gate; plan review will not substitute for implementation review.

The immediate approval request will cover B1 offline implementation of this plan only. B2 execution and subsequent evidence-based qualification will retain their explicit gates. Closure will require the relevant reviewed stage artifacts, test/scan evidence, issue publication and scoped cleanup audit; no planning or offline completion statement will imply a native solve or qualified database.
