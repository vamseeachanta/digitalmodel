Technical safety screening plan

Scope will cover ship-impact energy and fire heat-flux selection for the requested feature. Blowdown, flare, move-off and pump redundancy will remain outside this change.

  1. Tests will establish hand-calculated energy, explicit mass and speed units, edition selection, citation rejection, historical-use refusal, fire selection, water-credit conditions and the QRA boundary before implementation.
  2. A strict edition-keyed YAML loader will reuse the foam Citation sidecar. Only the historical collision limit and jet peak will be bundled; they will be marked superseded, unverified and usable only by explicit test opt-in. Pool and average/reduced fluxes will require cited caller criteria.
  3. Energy will use E = (m + a) v² / 2, with a = C_a m and no default C_a. Shared Pint units will provide conversions. Results will retain inputs and criteria. QRA will retain optional energy context without estimating damage or fatalities.
  4. Fire selection will use inventory phase, release rate, pressurised release, pool potential and ventilation. Mixed releases will retain both credible types. Water credit will require complete evidence and a cited reduced average flux; peak flux will remain unreduced. Unsupported scenarios will be refused.
  5. Focused and related pytest suites will run. Documentation and the pull request will record results, limitations and follow-ups.

Adversarial plan checks

Review will seek uncited or mismatched editions, non-finite arithmetic, unit ambiguity, mixed-fire under-selection, unjustified water credit and any inference from energy to fatality. Structural citation validation will not be represented as source verification. No original standard text will be copied.

Independent review of the change will be recorded on the pull request.

Basis and verification limits

Public method sources will be the RISP JIP report, version 2 (October 2023, published by Offshore Norge), part ST-16725-2, section 3.4; Fire and Explosion Guidance issue 2 (March 2018), sections 2.10.1–2.10.2, 3.4.2.2 and 8.7.4; and NIST SP 811 (2008), appendix B.8. Licensed current standard values will remain required inputs.
