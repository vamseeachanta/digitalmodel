Under the originating job authorization, the change will close remaining findings 5, 7, 9 and 10 from R02, tracked by issue 2239 and the merged PR 2314. One draft PR will be published; no merge will occur.

Resource intelligence and scope

The geometry contract, analytic box/Wigley fixtures, immutable-array regressions and existing friction/citation tests will be reused. The mesh adapter will be divided into geometry primitives, validated mesh/topology, clipping, indexed sections, immutable output schema and computation orchestration. Original public imports and serialized input identity will remain compatible. No engineering qualification, private data, wiki publication or Holtrop-method correction will be inferred.

Implementation and tests

  1. Failing regressions will demonstrate generated physical triangles below the existing area threshold, repeated section evaluation, full-vertex copies, and file/function limits. Artificial signed cap fans will remain exempt from physical-face degeneracy checks.
  2. Source meshes will retain the 1e-12 diagonal-squared area floor. Generated physical faces will instead be checked for repeated indices, non-finite coordinates and area at or below machine epsilon times their own longest-edge squared. This local numerical-resolution floor will permit small resolved cut triangles; applying the source threshold would reject valid near-vertex cuts. Refusal will remain explicit; artificial signed fans will be exempt. Existing near-plane, concave, endpoint and trim tests will guard the accepted domain.
  3. A calculation-local section index will precompute face x ranges once, compact selected vertices before clipping, and cache stations across midship, features and grid requests. Cache lifetime will be one calculation; no unbounded global cache will be added.
  4. Citation regressions will cover configured, missing and unconfigured dependencies, both transfer directions and explicit opt-out. A registry method will identify the ITTC transfer procedure; citation-enabled transfer will fail closed if required references cannot resolve.
  5. Helpers will keep touched modules at most 400 lines and functions at most 50 lines. Focused and complete naval-architecture suites will verify numerical and immutability compatibility.
  6. A reproducible analytic million-face case will record face/station counts, wall time, process peak memory and box/Wigley properties against their analytic comparator. A small script will reproduce current Holtrop fixture discrepancies and common-Froude comparisons without treating approximate fixture references as an oracle.

index = SectionIndex(clipped, midship, tolerance)
for requested station:
    if station absent from local cache:
        candidates = precomputed_min <= station <= precomputed_max
        compact = remap candidate faces onto only referenced vertices
        cache[station] = section(compact)
    consume cached area and physical segments
after waterline clipping:
    refuse unresolved physical area <= machine_epsilon * local_longest_edge**2
    cap boundary using signed fan; verify closure and independent volumes
