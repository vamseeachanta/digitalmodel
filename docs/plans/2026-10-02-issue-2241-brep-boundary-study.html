Proposed BRep boundary-preserving study

Issue 2241, item 2 · T2 · Lane: codex · Client: N/A · Owner approval will be required; no implementation will begin under the previous experiment's authorization.

Evidence to consult

The study will use the rejected arc-length experiment and its JSON as archived-run/source-reference comparators. It will reproduce the reported interior depth and keel counterexamples before developing another fit. It will preserve the original r3 plan's immutable reference, numerical thresholds, native-validity contract and exclusions. It will not interpret native valid status as geometric acceptance.

Proposed bounded scope

  1. The lane will first write failing tests for exact keel/waterline/end bounds and single-valued x-z projection on both fixtures, using independent source-reference evaluation.
  2. It will evaluate one diagnostic-only tensor-product graph representation S(u,v)=(L·u, y(u,v), −T+T·v). Fixed affine x/z coordinates will make the depth and longitudinal boundary contract explicit. Only y will be fitted to the existing PCHIP reference. It will inspect and document the spline degree, knots and control-net construction before fitting, and it will not alter the reference or recover generator targets from labels.
  3. Before any native assessment, all three grids from the previous plan will have to pass its full geometry, source-relative curvature, topology, independently integrated area, volume/moment and boundary checks (the native-versus-independent area comparison will follow during native assessment). The lane will verify those checks with closed-form controls and independently integrated quantities. A failed check will stop the candidate; no smoothing parameter search, surface deletion, threshold relaxation or second representation will follow.
  4. Only a geometry-qualified candidate will enter the unchanged native status, full-coverage and three-grid convergence checks. The previous plan's order/depth comparison and metre/millimetre parity contracts will remain mandatory. Native calls will be limited to sixteen: two current-default comparisons, six candidate grid assessments, four fixed-geometry refinement assessments and four control/parity/unit assessments. Each will have a 120-second timeout and the total diagnostic run will have 45 minutes.
  5. The lane will produce a report stating which gates were exercised and which were not. An unsuccessful candidate will remain a diagnostic with strict expected failures. A successful experiment will still require a separate proposal for production integration or migration.

Artifact and review route

Implementation will live in isolated diagnostic helpers under scripts/hull_library/ with focused tests. The public exporter defaults, existing experimental route, curvature_screen.py, HullProd dependency, generators and catalog will remain unchanged. New modules will stay below 400 lines and functions below 50 lines. The lane will run Black 24.x/Ruff, the full hull-library and diffraction quality-gate suite, and verified-target legal scanning. A second provider will adversarially review code/artifacts before closeout; unavailable providers will be named. This proposal will receive plan review before owner approval is requested.

Risk and stop rule

A boundary-preserving graph may still fail native integration because the immutable PCHIP reference is only piecewise smooth. Fixed x/z coordinates will not prove curvature accuracy or convergence. The study will not introduce longitudinal patches, revise physical acceptance targets or close item 2 merely because finite metrics become available. If the representation cannot satisfy the inherited contract, the report will identify the failing gate and return for a new decision.
