Result against review criteria

  • B1: connected patches of touched bottom panels are triangulated with a maximum new-triangle longest-edge/altitude aspect ratio of 50. Source boundary segments are retained; source side quads and remote refinement transitions remain unchanged in regression tests. Conformity uses only cut-boundary candidates and overlapping keel faces. Reports include minimum panel area, maximum triangle aspect ratio, triangle count and quad count.
  • B2: clearance_tolerance defaults to 1e-4, relative to each nearby bottom panel's shortest edge. Positive near-corner/edge distances are rejected with an explicit diagnostic. Exact alignment and transverse crossings away from corners remain supported. Near an existing opening is also tested.
  • R1: integrated removed volume is compared with polygon area times draft at 1e-9 relative; nominal-circle agreement within 0.5% is a separate criterion.
  • R2: circular polygons have equal nominal area and accept eight or more segments. The default remains 64; automatic selection of segments and wall layers is deferred because it requires a separate meshing policy.
  • R3: moonpool_index identifies each wall panel's cumulative cutout index, with -1 for other panels. Repeated cuts retain earlier IDs. Legacy flagged meshes lacking IDs are explicitly rejected.
  • R4/R5: the untyped report dictionary and mesh metadata retain cumulative cutouts, including nominal area, footprint area, draft and removed volume. Non-zero waterline and off-centre expanded-half-model cases are tested.
  • Review-discovered correction: empty or incomplete touched-panel coverage is rejected within the 1e-9 relative area criterion. A sub-resolution footprint cannot silently return unchanged geometry with a claimed volume loss.
