Low-frequency surge and Naess extreme

Scope: an offline analytical increment toward the second-order motions workflow.

Resource intelligence and proposed work

The implementation will retain the existing unordered-pair variance convention in drift_forces.py and the full-bin Newman spectrum convention tested by test_drift_force_variance.py. It will add a validated full-QTF force spectrum, a damped scalar receptance, separate static offset, and a centered Hermitian quadratic-response kernel. Computation will use kN, m, s and mass supplied in kg.

The Naess expression will be implemented with explicit eigenvalue normalization, duration and observed upcrossing rate. Both response tails will be available. The light-damping and large-duration assumptions will be recorded. No solver, conditioned waves, aerodynamic model, multi-DOF model or Weibull fit will be added.

TDD and acceptance

Closed-form bichromatic resonance, static offset, finite-bin endpoint weights, complex Hermitian QTFs and invalid inputs will be tested before implementation. Kernel moments will be checked against independently summed response variance. Seeded random-phase ensembles will check force and surge variance and 3-hour maxima. Approximation discrepancies will be reported against the requested 5% standard-deviation and 10% extreme criteria, without changing those criteria.

Published numerical values will only be claimed reproduced when their input data can be verified. Otherwise the missing inputs and unreproduced comparisons will be stated. The module pack will link a short method note; the implementation map will register the new family and the existing QTF data model.

Adversarial plan review

Defect checks will target pair double-counting, diagonal mean contamination, Hz/rad/s conversion, mass units, response amplitude versus height, random-phase versus Gaussian quadrature normalization, asymptotic validity, incomplete QTFs, and accidental interpolation outside source support. These checks will govern the implementation. The final artifact review will repeat them against tests.
