Objective and resource intelligence

The correction will address the two supported blocking mechanisms in exact review b03933c8e960, against source ca49d9eac76b99493d5dbe867be67649a73afda1. A fourth review of unchanged code will not be requested. The corrected revision will receive a new defect-focused review with inline disposition. No numerical acceptance criterion or geometry will change.

  • The implementation will use cylinder_pressure_resume._claim_and_launch, whose phase2 checks will remain after the claim but will also be measured before it for the intermediate case.
  • The resource collector will replace only its call to cylinder_parent_map.enumerate_windows_v2; the active-family enumerator and its callers will remain unchanged.
  • The collector will reuse read_windows_parent_map, the existing _seed predicate, creation identities, protected-name query and strict absence verifier. Unknown identity will remain a refusal.
  • The existing intermediate execution and resource-absence plans will supply fixed scope, ownership and stop criteria. No external code or standard will be introduced; this is software admission correction.

Measured pre-claim guard

Only the third, intermediate-mesh attempt will run the exact production phase2 recheck and cumulative storage callback before journal.claim. The wrapper will record actual monotonic elapsed time and retain the probe observations. It will require 2 times measured duration plus 1 second reserved overhead to fit within the unchanged 5-second post-claim limit: measured duration shall be no more than 2 seconds. This will be a conservative scheduling heuristic, not a deterministic upper bound or an engineering constant. The actual final 5-second check will remain mandatory.

The wrapper will require all four retained observation ages to meet the existing 20-second pre-claim limit before and after the probe, and the existing 365-second invocation allowance before the claim. It will repeat the full checks after consumption with the unchanged 30-second ages, 5-second elapsed limit, 360-second launcher allowance, and 300-second native timeout. Probe exceptions, negative time or over-budget duration will refuse before the consumed claim. The result will retain observed duration, allowance, overhead, check/storage results and refusal reason under preclaim_probe. The resource callback will use a distinct preclaim-probe phase label and preserve phase2 for the post-claim callback. Tests will establish that the probe never changes _ready_at or the licence-observation timestamp and never acquires or renews a vendor licence. The one-second write allowance will be an explicit unmeasured scheduling reserve, not a measured bound; slow claim writes will still trip the final hard stop before native launch. For the intermediate case, replay, preflight, input-copy preparation and the measured probe will precede journal.start as well as journal.claim. After final accounting and reservation release, a failed preparation will write preparation-refusal.json from the frozen final result containing error, last_preflight_evidence, preclaim_probe when available, final_storage_before_receipts, consumed_count, native_launch_count, no_owned_processes_established and reservation_released. This will be a separate branch before the existing journal.started-only terminal/outcome branch; receipt failure will add PREPARATION_RETENTION_INCOMPLETE to the returned record. The newly created output directory will retain this receipt and its evidence, without any ordinal-3 ledger record. A later corrected, independently reviewed configuration may use a fresh output identity against the still-unconsumed ordinal; existing directories will never be overwritten. Once journal.start is written, existing one-shot/no-readmission rules will remain unchanged. The total invocation clock will still start at output creation; a slow preparation will not reset or extend it. Coarse-case ordering and behavior will remain unchanged.

Relevant process stability

An absence-only enumerator will retain initial parent map A, map B after initial identity reads, and final map C after at most one protected-name query. Identity will be reread for every surviving process, including processes with unchanged parent identifiers, to detect identifier reuse. Creation-time changes will refuse. Name/executable changes or unreadable identities will refuse. Original maps, identities, protected-name query and change events will be retained on success and refusal.

Relevant sets will include existing solver and message-passing seeds, their descendants, and interFoam processes. Initial and final parent graphs will both be checked. A new or parent-changed process will be retained as unrelated only with bounded nonblank name, nonblank executable, exact creation identity, and absence from both relevant closures. An exited initial process will be evidence-only only if its complete retained name/executable identifies it as unrelated and outside the initial relevant closure. A new process that disappears before identification, a blank identity after the single query, inaccessible or reused identity, relevant process or relevant parent transition will refuse. Existing unchanged protected rows may retain the existing missing-executable semantics; new/changed rows will not receive that exception. No process identifier or image-name exception list will be introduced.

The final snapshot will include the final fully identified population and its recomputed coverage/selection. The initial population and all churn observations will remain separately retained. The root-owned absence verifier will additionally validate every retained normalized A/B inventory and reject any observed solver seed or interFoam identity, including one absent from the final inventory. Thus a vanished relevant process will independently refuse rather than relying only on collector churn disposition. The collector will publish observed_inventories with normalized A/B/C rows; each inventory will be shape-validated using the existing initial-row validator before the historical seed checks. The existing verifier will still require an empty selected set; observation will remain point-in-time, without a guarantee about subsequent starts. No process will be terminated or any unrelated execution control changed.

Plan-review disposition

Review c819288b8146 will be addressed by moving preparation before both invocation and consumed claim; adding the exact preflight and driver sources to review evidence; separating phase labels; testing unchanged observation timestamps; and disclosing the write allowance as an unmeasured reserve. The suggestion to admit unidentified new processes solely from parent linkage will be rejected: a new root solver with unreadable identity need not descend from any observed seed. Assertions that protected churn is frequent will remain unestablished until live observations support them. Unknown or competing processes appearing after a claim may still cause a consumed refusal under the unchanged stop rules.

Acceptance and limits

The candidate will require focused and integrated passing tests, unchanged historical recovery-source digests, a legal scan, and exact independent review. A read-only live observation will measure the corrected collector. Before any future native admission, an exact reviewed configuration will also require a non-consuming full-probe timing measurement; the collector-only measurement will not substantiate the 2-second full-probe limit. A systematic budget failure will require evidence-backed remediation and will not relax the hard limit silently; no native run, licence checkout, seat acquisition or attempt claim will be part of this implementation checkpoint. Runtime bundle, current-source replay, exact configuration review and fresh live admission will follow only when the source review is nonblocking. The authoritative result matrix will remain unchanged until real execution evidence can be admitted through permitted tooling. Matrix-intake write denials will not be bypassed.
