Welded-padeye parametric demand study

Proposed implementation plan · 13 September 2026 · Issue 2117 · Engineering owner: digitalmodel.

Implementation will await explicit user approval after independent review. Reference selection will not authorize this numerical campaign. The study will follow the reusable analysis criteria. It will generate demands only, without solver execution, capacity lookup, utilization lookup or physical qualification.

1. Sources and stop conditions

The source chain will identify Ricker, Design and Construction of Lifting Beams, 1991, example pages 156–158 and Fig.16A, its 2004 erratum, and BV NR595, August 2014, Appendix 1 for a missing-check register. Historical procedures and BV requirements will not be combined into a compliance claim.

Visual verification will resolve drawing fractions, baseline weld leg, dimensions, load-sharing interpretation and errata. The adopted procedure reading will be compared with conflicting source statements. Unresolved source, applicability or rights questions will block dependent calculations and publication. Licensed originals will remain outside Git; authorized derivatives will retain source IDs, editions, pages, digests and Citation sidecars.

Resource reconciliation will consult sibling wiki catalogs, the hub data-handling contract, and existing atlases/lifting_lug/937764fdc168/manifest.yaml, src/digitalmodel/lifting_lug/workflow.py, src/digitalmodel/parametric/atlas.py, src/digitalmodel/parametrics/atlas_bridge.py, src/digitalmodel/workflows/parametric_run.py and src/digitalmodel/ansys/padeye_pressure.py. Reuse will follow inspection; previous circular-lug and rectangular-pressure cases will retain separate identities. Partial drive-index discovery will not establish source absence.

The exact baseline will use thickness 1.250 in, scale 1.00, weld multiplier 1.00 and angle 45 degrees, conditional on source verification. A disagreement will stop generation for review rather than substitute a nearby row.

Scaling will move every exterior vertex, exterior arc centre/radius and weld endpoint uniformly about the hole centre, then translate the weld-group centroid to the origin. Hole, pin and supporting beam dimensions will remain fixed; thickness will vary independently. The profile definition will expose coupled ligament, footprint and eccentricity changes. Geometry checks will cover positive dimensions, containment, interference, connection fit and shackle clearance. Unresolved clearance will not imply fit. Geometry validity will remain separate from weld capacity limits. Fabrication-size availability will be recorded as a non-gating advisory. Weld leg will equal the multiplier times the fixed absolute source baseline leg, not a thickness-derived value.

3. Loads and demand method

Every row and response will carry a load-basis descriptor: gross weight, embedded historical factor, lug-sharing denominator, source clause and already-factored flag. Pending source qualification, the adopted chain will be Fy=Wgross×factor/n=16×1.8/2=14.4 kip. Positive Y will be upward; X will be horizontal in-plane; positive Z moment will follow the right-hand rule. Directional loading will use Fx=±Fy tan(theta), not a separate hardcoded force. The factor will not be reapplied.

Each source-qualified, geometrically valid scheduled direction will produce nine finite demands. The load and weld responses will be: signed Fx (kip), signed Fy (kip), signed Mz (kip-in), resultant force (kip), maximum weld-line force per length qmax (kip/in), and nominal throat stress (ksi). With the hole-centre lever arm from the weld centroid, Mz=rx Fy−ry Fx will define eccentric loading.

The elastic method will represent two equal straight fillet lines projected in-plane; curved segments will fail the method-applicability gate. It will evaluate total line length L=Σ∫dl, centroidal J=Σ∫(x²+y²)dl, and q(x,y)=F/L+(Mz/J)(−y,x). It will evaluate the vector magnitude at every straight-line endpoint to obtain qmax and divide by a/√2 for nominal throat stress. Nonpositive L, J or throat will classify geometry as invalid before calculation. Plastic redistribution, instantaneous-centre methods and end returns will be excluded unless separately source-verified and reviewed.

Three additional nominal plate-base-section responses will make thickness sensitivity explicit: sigma_left=Fy/(B*t)−6Mz/(t*B²), sigma_right=Fy/(B*t)+6Mz/(t*B²), and tau_average=Fx/(B*t), in ksi. Source geometry will confirm a rectangular cut of positive width B at the weld line and positive thickness t before these calculations. Left/right will denote negative/positive X about that section centroid. These signed nominal elastic section-transmission stresses will assume plane sections and transferred loads; they will not represent local peaks, hole stress, weld FEA stress or qualification. Load components, weld-line forces and fixed-leg throat stress will be thickness-invariant by construction; the plate stresses will vary with thickness.

4. Directional coverage and verification

Each of the 225 geometry-angle rows will contain positive and negative direction subrecords. Zero angle will schedule positive only and mark negative not applicable: 450 cells will contain 375 scheduled attempts and 75 not-applicable cells. Invalid geometry will retain null demands and reasons. A missing required demand on a valid source-qualified direction will fail usable coverage; a withheld label will not evade this requirement without reviewed scope revision.

Independent verification will cover at least 54 cases and 90 scheduled directions: thickness {0.750,1.250,1.750}, scale {0.80,1.00,1.20}, weld {1.00,1.50}, all three angles. A non-author checker will independently derive all nine responses. Before calculation, the register will freeze response-specific tolerances and precision, baseline reference comparison and sign/asymmetry checks; unresolved tolerances will block calculation. Additional invalid-boundary and failure tests will supplement this minimum.

Each response envelope will select maximum absolute magnitude while retaining signed directional values. Exact ties will select positive deterministically. Fy, resultant and absolute Fx will carry direction-invariant-by-construction flags; selection will not imply physical governance. Mz, qmax, throat stress and each plate stress will retain response-specific envelopes and governing directions. Any missing applicable direction will make its response envelope null with a partial-coverage reason. The negative not-applicable cell at zero angle will not suppress its valid positive-direction envelope.

5. Dataset and report contract

Each immutable revision will contain the complete 225-key grid, including invalid rows, with method/code/source revisions, load descriptors, canonical decimal parameter keys, units, typed values, per-response calculation/verification status, evidence references and reasons. Lookup will return per-response envelopes and retrievable directional subrecords. Row hashes will bind canonical inputs, method, both subrecords and envelopes; revisions will not mix implicitly. Exact lookup will require a dataset revision and intended use, reject off-grid inputs, and provide no interpolation or extrapolation. Invalid/unresolved responses will be available only diagnostically.

CSV decoding will follow a typed schema: numeric null sentinels will not transform free-text literal “NULL”; reason codes and text will roundtrip distinctly. JSON and CSV will undergo digest/readback verification. Storage will stop for placement review above 5 MB per revision or 25 MB cumulative; bulk relocation will preserve manifests and digests without silent truncation.

The HTML report will show demand traces, fixed slices, invalid gaps, coverage and independent comparisons. No capacity or utilization field will enter lookup. An optional baseline-only historical capacity appendix will remain outside the dataset and unqualified. It will neither supply varied-profile capacities nor establish assembly acceptance.

6. Implementation and acceptance gates

Implementation will begin with RED tests for load propagation, geometry transforms, all counts, nine-response withholding, thickness-dependent plate stresses, expected load/weld invariance, curved-segment rejection, nonpositive geometry, signs, ties, zero-angle applicability, partial envelopes, exact keys, revision isolation, typed-null roundtrips and missing-Citation refusal. GREEN work will reuse audited components in an isolated worktree. Acceptance will require complete case accounting, all required valid demands, the independent register, report checks, legal/security scans and saved-artifact readback.

T2 will be proposed for this bounded adapter and confirmed with plan approval; shared schema or citation-gate changes will trigger T3 reassessment. Codex-authored content will receive independent Claude exact-current review and Claude-authored content independent Codex review, with hashes, verdicts and dispositions. Publication and cleanup records will identify retained evidence and unresolved checks. Neither this plan nor successful tests will authorize a solver run.
