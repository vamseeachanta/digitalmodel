Analytic case and mixed cuts

The source Wigley mesh has L=100 m, B=10 m and design draft T=6 m, sampled on uniform nx=1000,nz=250 grids. The condition draft is t=5.973 m, between z rows 5.952 and 5.976 m. Ten transverse stations −44.95…45.05 m lie between the 0.1 m x columns; the midship section is at x=0. Grid waterlines are 1,3,5.973 m. All inputs are analytic synthetic geometry; no client or measured data is consumed.

With y=(B/2)(1−(2x/L)²)(1−((z−T)/T)²), the partial-draft midship area is A_M=B(t²/T−t³/(3T²))=39.7300018225 m²; V=(2L/3)A_M=2648.66678817 m³. The design-draft comparator 2BT/3=40 m² is not substituted for the partial-draft value. Wetted area is independently integrated over x∈[−L/2,L/2], z∈[0,t] using the analytic surface derivatives; the reference is 1439.14696964 m². Computed A_M relative discrepancy is 4.01038233333e-06; no new A_M accuracy threshold is introduced.

The cut leaves 998000 physical faces. Their minimum area is 0.000131256564908 m², below the source minimum 0.0012000015268 m²; their mean is 0.00144203033129 m² versus source mean 0.00268834633288 m². Thus the scale case exercises newly clipped physical triangles rather than only complete source faces. It does not by itself prove behavior below the old source-area threshold; targeted near-vertex unit regressions cover that finding.

Both implementations execute eleven _section calls at eleven unique stations. The benchmark does not evidence cache reuse. Repeated-station caching is established only by test_repeated_stations_compute_once; compact mixed cuts are exercised here through the off-column section grid and compared through the complete result digest.
