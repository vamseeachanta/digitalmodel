## Defects

### MAJOR-1 — Timeout discards every piece of evidence the slice exists to preserve
`src/digitalmodel/ansys/runner.py:140-142`, `_execute_solver`

On `subprocess.TimeoutExpired`, `return_code` stays `None`, `stdout`/`stderr` stay `""` although `exc.stdout` and `exc.stderr` carry the captured streams, and no post-run snapshot is taken — `result_files` stays `[]` and `log_file` `None`. A solve killed at the 7200 s cap publishes nothing, including the partial `.out` MAPDL wrote. This is the path where "preserve completed subprocess metadata" has the highest value and it is unimplemented. No test covers it: a grep for `TimeoutExpired` across `tests/ansys/` returns nothing, while both runners this module's docstring claims to mirror have one (`tests/hydrodynamics/diffraction/test_aqwa_runner.py:316`, `test_orcawave_runner.py:372`). `aqwa_runner.py:651-652` at least records `-1` rather than `None`.

### MAJOR-2 — The bounded-scan test pins neither boundedness nor the overlap sizing
`tests/ansys/test_runner_evidence_failures.py:98-102`

The fixture splits the 13-character `*** ERROR ***` with 6 characters past the 65536 boundary, so any `overlap >= 6` passes. Regressing `runner.py:201` from `max(map(len, _ERROR_MARKERS)) - 1` (21) to `min(...) - 1` (12), or to a literal, keeps the suite green. The worst case is untested: 21 of the 22 characters of `"SOLUTION NOT CONVERGED"` before the boundary, one after. Separately, `monkeypatch.setattr(Path, "read_text", ...)` does not establish boundedness — changing `stream.read(65536)` to `stream.read()` at `runner.py:205` also passes. Two concrete missing cases.

### MAJOR-3 — Relative `output_dir` makes the new freshness gate reject every run
`src/digitalmodel/ansys/runner.py:150-158`, `:232-237`

`argv` passes `-i <input_file>` and `-o <output_dir>/<stem>.out` exactly as supplied while setting `cwd=str(output_dir)`. With the module's own documented defaults — `ANSYSRunConfig.output_dir = Path("output")` (`:47`) and `run_ansys(output_dir="output")` (`:242`) — MAPDL resolves `-o` against `cwd` and writes `output/output/model.out`; `_capture_result_files` does not recurse, so the log is never attributed, `fresh_log` is `None`, and the run returns FAILED `"No fresh MAPDL log was produced by this run"` whatever the solver did. `-i` fails the same way for a relative script. Before the freshness gate this path failed differently; `04d1012c..HEAD` converts it into a deterministic false-negative on the licensed host. `AnsysWorkflow._resolve_output_dir` / `_resolve_script_path` (`workflow.py:79-102`) resolve to absolute, which is why the router tests miss it, and every runner test uses absolute `tmp_path`. Fix: resolve `script` and `output_dir` to absolute in `prepare()`.

### MINOR-1 — `prepare()` IO failure escapes as an exception, not a FAILED result
`runner.py:105-108`. `output_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)` runs before `self._result` exists and outside any handler. A permission failure, or an `output_dir` that already exists as a file, raises a raw `OSError` out of `run_ansys` and out of `AnsysWorkflow._run_ansys` (`workflow.py:48`). The advertised invariant holds only post-prepare. Fail-closed, but the result-object contract does not hold. Untested.

### MINOR-2 — Evidence surface is self-inconsistent when the fresh log is unreadable
`result_files` is assigned at `runner.py:163-166`, including the fresh `.out`, before `_detect_error` attempts to open it. When that open raises, `log_file` is `None` while `settings["outputs"]["result_files"]` (`workflow.py:62`) still lists the `.out` as captured evidence. `test_unreadable_fresh_log_returns_record` asserts `log_file is None` but makes no assertion on `result_files`, so the inconsistency is unpinned.

### MINOR-3 — Reported marker is arbitrary when several are present
`runner.py:206-209` overwrites `log_error` on every match, so the published message is the last marker in `_ERROR_MARKERS` order found in the last matching chunk, not the first failure in the log. A log with `SOLUTION NOT CONVERGED` at line 40 and `*** FATAL ***` at line 12000 reports whichever chunk matched last. No test pins the selection.

### MINOR-4 — `duration_seconds` assertion cannot fail
Both DRY_RUN exits (`runner.py:126-128`, `:130-136`) return before the `try/finally` at `:138-144`, leaving `duration_seconds` at its 0.0 default. `test_runner_evidence_failures.py:60` asserts `duration_seconds >= 0`, which 0.0 satisfies — the assertion does not establish that the `finally` ran on the failure path it is placed in.

### MINOR-5 — `timeout_seconds` is unvalidated
`ANSYSRunConfig` (`runner.py:45-51`) is a plain dataclass and `workflow.py:52` forwards `int(settings.get("timeout_seconds", 7200))` unchecked. A config value of `0` or negative makes every solve die immediately at `subprocess.run(timeout=...)`. The module docstring claims it "mirrors the OrcaWave/AQWA runner contract", where `RunConfig(timeout_seconds=0)` and `AQWARunConfig(timeout_seconds=0)` raise (`test_orcawave_runner.py:177`, `test_aqwa_runner.py:106`).

### MINOR-6 — Golden values are duplicated across CSV and PROVENANCE with no cross-assertion
`tests/ansys/test_example_goldens.py::_golden` binds the deck to `provenance["input"]["sha256"]` but never binds the digest CSV to provenance, and no test asserts the values repeated in both files agree: `acceptance.unity_check` 0.18212 vs `mudmat_result.csv` `uc`; `acceptance.allowable_mpa` 212.5749 vs `allowable_mpa`; `comparator.observed_reaction_fz_n` 0.03 vs `reaction_fz_n`; pressure-vessel `comparator.observed_von_mises_mpa` 134.7185 vs `max_seqv_mpa`. They agree today (checked); nothing keeps them agreeing. `_golden:58` also takes `sorted(gdir.glob("*_result.csv"))[0]` — a second digest in the directory is silently ignored rather than rejected.
