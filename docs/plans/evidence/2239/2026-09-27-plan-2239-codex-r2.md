VERDICT: MAJOR

1. **MAJOR — The geometry convergence check can pass without improving geometry accuracy.**  
   **Evidence:** Plan lines 89–92, 126–129, 139, 164.  
   Subdividing planar triangles preserves the same polyhedral surface. Consequently, `refine(hull)` can produce identical hydrostatics and satisfy the 0.1% check even when the original mesh poorly approximates the hull. Two agreeing resolutions do not establish accuracy. Relative tolerances also fail for zero-valued properties such as a symmetric hull’s LCB or absent bulb area.  
   **Fix:** Require independently generated tessellations of the underlying geometry, or explicitly distinguish numerical integration verification from geometric accuracy when only one mesh exists. Define per-property absolute/relative tolerances and reference scales. Specify the Wigley equation, dimensions, exact volume reference and independently evaluated wetted-area reference; do not require monotonic convergence without justification.

2. **MAJOR — W0 lacks geometry definitions needed by downstream methods.**  
   **Evidence:** Plan lines 89–101, 126–128; `holtrop_mennen.py:174`; `hull_form.py:363–369`.  
   The capped mesh is passed to hydrostatics without excluding artificial waterplane caps from wetted area. Bulb area and transom area require identified sections/features; neither is uniquely specified by “compute A_BT and A_T.” The contract omits draft datum, trim pivot/sign, LCB origin/sign/normalization, and how `tf`, `hb` and `cstern` will be supplied. W3 requires submerged half-breadths and their longitudinal variation, not merely hydrostatic coefficients or an intersection with the centreplane.  
   **Fix:** Define physical versus artificial faces, feature metadata, section locations, coordinate conventions and unsupported-geometry rejection. Specify the W0 output consumed by W2/W3 and distinguish computed quantities from required user inputs.

3. **MAJOR — W1 does not define a reviewable extrapolation procedure.**  
   **Evidence:** Plan lines 56–57, 93–95, 130–131, 142–146.  
   Reynolds numbers and `k` alone cannot establish a valid model–ship comparison. Froude similarity, geometry similarity, coefficient normalization, allowance conventions and the assumption of invariant form factor must be explicit. “ITTC-78 2D/3D” does not identify a procedure revision or distinguish a simplified resistance transfer from the complete procedure. Prohaska needs a defined fitting range and conditioning checks, not just recovery from ideal synthetic data.  
   **Fix:** Cite the exact procedure and equations. For a deliberately simplified transfer, explicitly define the conserved residual, for example  
   `C_R = C_T,m − (1+k)C_F,m − C_A,m`,  
   and reconstruct the target with its separately specified allowances. Define all omitted corrections and validity conditions. Specify Prohaska’s regression variables, admissible data, minimum sample count, conditioning and uncertainty/rejection criteria.

4. **MAJOR — Several tests can pass materially incorrect implementations.**  
   **Evidence:** Plan lines 138–151.  
   Round-trip and `k=0` agreement tests can both pass an implementation that returns the input unchanged. A component-sum test can reproduce the same erroneous arithmetic as the total. The friction test repeats the implementation formula rather than supplying independent values. The trimmed-box volume test cannot distinguish opposite trim signs in a symmetric, partially immersed box. The `1e-9` and `1e-12` tolerances do not specify absolute versus relative error.  
   **Fix:** Add independent forward extrapolation cases with nonzero `k` and differing allowances, independently sourced component values, and explicit friction reference values (`0.0046875` at `Re=10^6`; `0.075/49` at `Re=10^9`). Add signed fore/aft draft or LCB assertions. Test declared metre/mm equivalence, axis transformations, reversed normals, open/nonmanifold/degenerate meshes, nonfinite inputs, dry hulls, bulb/transom waterline crossings, and invalid Reynolds/viscosity inputs. Retain identities as supplementary tests with stated numerical tolerances.

5. **MAJOR — W3’s numerical problem and validation oracle remain underspecified.**  
   **Evidence:** Plan lines 59, 99–101, 148–149, 187–188.  
   No Michell integral convention, coefficient normalization, integration limits, endpoint treatment or tail-error control is supplied. Doubling quadrature points can leave the same truncation error unchanged. The 5% test lacks a pinned curve, hull parameters, reference values and digitization uncertainty; relative error also needs treatment near small wave-resistance values.  
   **Fix:** Specify the equation and coordinate conventions, independently control quadrature and infinite-tail errors, and pin a legally usable linear-theory reference before W3 authorization. Define pointwise or aggregate acceptance, absolute floors and reference uncertainty. Keep numerical verification against linear theory separate from experimental validation.

6. **MAJOR — Full-bodied applicability and component comparability are not enforced.**  
   **Evidence:** Plan lines 59, 102–105, 150, 180–186.  
   Naming KVLCC2 as an applicability test provides no expected disposition or rejection rule. Towing-tank `C_T` does not independently measure Michell wave resistance, and an inferred residuary coefficient is not automatically the same component. Likewise, RANS pressure resistance cannot simply be treated as wave resistance. A numerical agreement on one full-bodied hull would not establish thin-ship validity.  
   **Fix:** Define method-specific applicability metadata and the expected warning/refusal for unsupported hulls. Specify each comparison’s observable, normalization and decomposition assumptions. Test unsupported-method requests and invalid component pairings. Treat exploratory KVLCC2 Michell results as outside validated applicability regardless of accidental agreement.

7. **MAJOR — Leakage and extrapolation controls are promises rather than complete mechanisms.**  
   **Evidence:** Plan lines 49, 104–114, 150–151.  
   One same-family rejection test does not prevent calibration on evaluation data, duplicate hull revisions under different identifiers, preprocessing fitted on held-out data, or tuning after inspecting test results. No out-of-domain rejection test or domain definition is included. Regime matching lacks a required metadata schema and missing-data behavior.  
   **Fix:** Define immutable hull/family/source identifiers and training, calibration, validation and locked-test roles. Track target derivation and fitted-parameter provenance. Reject overlapping lineage, missing conditions and unsupported domain inputs. Add explicit negative tests for circular calibration, renamed duplicates, missing Reynolds/Froude metadata, coefficient normalization mismatch and extrapolation.

11. **MINOR — Public-data licence verification lacks an auditable acceptance record.**  
    **Evidence:** Plan lines 76, 102–105, 165, 167–170.  
    “Public workshop data” does not establish redistribution rights. The plan does not specify a source manifest, permitted uses, attribution, raw-source preservation, extraction provenance or uncertainty. Analytical Wigley geometry also does not establish rights to published experimental or theoretical curves.  
    **Fix:** Require per-source rights evidence, version, URL, digest, attribution, units, conditions and extraction method. Preserve redistributable raw evidence beside derived values; reference licensed standards at their permitted location. Make absent redistribution permission block committing the affected data.

12. **MAJOR — W5 has a time box but no concrete falsification criteria.**  
    **Evidence:** Plan lines 106–114, 148, 161–163.  
    “Limiting cases,” “agreement” and “residual where one exists” provide no numerical pass/fail rules. W3’s stated validation range, `Fn=0.2–0.4`, does not establish a low-Froude asymptotic oracle. No public series, held-out families, baseline or surrogate error threshold is selected. Stopping before any candidate passes a limiting check is ambiguous because initial candidates are expected to fail.  
    **Fix:** Predeclare candidate limits, the three-day cutoff, low-Froude evaluation grid and oracle accuracy, asymptotic error/order criteria, surrogate baseline and held-out error thresholds. Record every candidate’s equations, provenance, tests and rejection reason. At cutoff, report a negative result unless the predefined criteria are met.
