Padeye two-mesh diagnostic criteria

This experiment will implement the approved re-parameterisation and mesh-evidence scope of issue 2094. It will retain screening status and will not establish a physical lug rating, promote a golden or mark registry readiness complete.

Both decks will use the 400 by 300 mm plate, 80 mm hole centred at (200,220) mm, thickness 8 mm, load 50 kN in global +Y, yield 355 MPa, design factor 1.67, PLANE182 plane stress with thickness and one-core SMP direct solution. The two nominal meshes will be 10 and 5 mm. These are frozen example inputs, not standards-derived sizing criteria. This will be a different demonstration case from the former 500 kN lug. No inference of pin/contact, weld, stability or three-dimensional validity will follow from t/D = 0.1.

Before capture, generated input bytes and the reviewed source revision will be hashed. Both runs will use fresh directories and the shared seat lock, with a fresh zero-use license query and process preflight before each solve. The pinned executable and existing recorded runtime profile will be retained. A 120 s timeout will bound each small static run; any remaining process will prevent lock release pending disposition.

Execution evidence will require completion, zero exit status, fresh readable log, RST/database, and a complete finite digest. Native node, element and selected-load-node counts will be positive integers; fine mesh node and element counts will exceed coarse counts. Full-graphics and AVPRIN=0 will make nodal averaging explicit. The nodal stress and reaction listings will be retained for independent extraction and FSUM sign checks.

Python will verify the exported load against FX=0 N and FY=50000 N, independently recompute the signed reaction-plus-applied resultant, and enforce the approved 0.1% bound (50 N). It will compare the reported residual against the recomputation using the recorded output precision. Allowable will be recomputed as 355/1.67 MPa; peak and unity output rounding intervals will remain below the allowable, yield and UC=1 limits.

The reported quantity will remain the global nodal-averaged equivalent-stress peak, with node and global coordinates retained on both meshes. No selected region will replace it. Growth greater than the combined F12.4 print-resolution intervals (0.0001 MPa), or coordinate movement beyond the combined E20.12 print-resolution intervals, will produce unqualified/investigation-required status. These are output-resolution tests, not convergence tolerances. A changed node ID alone will not trigger movement. Growth or movement will not be described as proof of mathematical singularity. Two meshes will provide a sensitivity observation only, even if all diagnostic checks pass.

Independent review will compare native listings with digests and verify artifact hashes before reporting evidence. A qualification failure will preserve all diagnostics and will stop golden promotion. A subsequent change of reported quantity or model fidelity will require a separately reviewable proposal; tolerance changes will not be used to make these results pass.

Source guidance: Ansys stress-singularity guidance. This source will support the interpretation limits; it will not supply the numerical acceptance thresholds above.
