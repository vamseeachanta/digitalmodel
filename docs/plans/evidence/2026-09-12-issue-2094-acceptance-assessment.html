The selected maximum switches between symmetric hole-edge points; the 80 mm coordinate difference does not establish physical migration of a hot spot. Two meshes do not demonstrate convergence, and growth alone does not prove a mathematical singularity.

The fine run reports one element out of 3,992 violating shape warning limits, with the same warning appearing twice. The coarse run has no observed warnings. Both logs have zero solver errors. The shape warning remains a qualification limitation.

Native defect found and corrected

The initial pair exposed a sign error that offline tests and both pre-capture reviews missed. FSUM returned +50,000 N, while the independent PRRSOL reaction listing totalled −50,000 N. Labelling the element-force sum as a support reaction produced a false 100,000 N residual. The correction sums restrained-node NODE,RF,FX/FY explicitly and retains FSUM in separate fields. This follows the distinction in the Ansys *GET reference.

Two fresh diagnostic re-solves verified the correction. Their reported residuals, approximately 10⁻⁹ and 10⁻¹⁰ N, agree with independent rounded-CSV recomputation within output precision and are below the 50 N criterion. The global stress values and all inputs before POST1 are unchanged from the initial captures. The original failed-extraction evidence remains preserved and explicitly marked invalid for reaction use.
