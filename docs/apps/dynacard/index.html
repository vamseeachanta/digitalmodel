How to read the result

The surface card is evaluated at the polished rod. The pump card is calculated at the plunger after the wave equation removes rod weight, stretch and dynamics. Fillage is effective travel divided by gross plunger travel. Fluid load is the upstroke mean load minus the downstroke mean load.

    What this does not check

    • Tubing anchoring: anchored tubing is assumed; unanchored tubing is not modelled.
    • Deviation and side load: a vertical well is assumed. A deviation survey is not solved by this application.
    • Load-cell calibration and timing: calibration is unverified; uniformly timed samples are assumed. Sampling timestamps are not checked.
    • Damping and friction calibration: the reference defaults are used. Measured viscosity, friction and damping calibration are missing.
    • Fluid properties: the supplied density is not validated against fluid sampling. CPIP uses the separate stated mixture basis.
    • Rod materials: only steel rods are supported. Material certification is not checked.
    • Pump depth versus rod length: mismatch is not reconciled. The wave calculation uses total rod length; CPIP uses pump depth.
    • Valve and plunger slip: leakage and slippage are not calculated.
    • Production: displacement is not production. A well test, runtime, leakage and volume-factor evidence are needed to establish production.
    • Classifier field accuracy: NOT_EVALUATED; training uses synthetic generators. Shape-only plate score: 5 of 8 literal.

    The buckling flag is the reference's minimum pump-load threshold of −500 lbf. A critical buckling load, neutral-point depth and mechanical capacity are not established by this flag.
