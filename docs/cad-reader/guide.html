CAD reader package guide

Draft for client review

1. Summary

The CAD reader package is a geometry ingestion interface. Analysis is performed to determine whether coordinates have an explicit unit basis; key results are summarized below. Computer-aided design is abbreviated as CAD.

  • Geometry ingestion is available for downstream analysis.
    • Straight drawing primitives and mesh faces are returned in metres.
    • STEP and IGES shapes provide display triangles in metres and exact kernel properties.
  • Unit evidence is required before coordinates are accepted.
    • Missing source units are supplied explicitly; contradictory declarations are refused.

2. Scope

Geometry is read from Drawing Exchange Format (DXF) modelspace, stereolithography (STL) meshes, Wavefront object (OBJ) meshes, STEP and IGES files. Drawing (DWG) input is accepted through an external DXF converter interface.

ISO 10303-21 exchange files are referred to as STEP; Initial Graphics Exchange Specification files are referred to as IGES. These two readers use the optional OpenCASCADE Technology (OCCT) geometry kernel, imported as OCP from cadquery-ocp. The DXF, mesh and DWG readers import no geometry kernel.

Native SolidWorks (.sldprt, .sldasm) and Inventor (.ipt, .iam) files are refused. Export STEP or IGES from the authoring system before ingestion.

3. Basis and criteria

Unit consistency is evaluated before a geometry result is returned. File declarations and caller declarations are required to agree because contradictions change the physical interpretation of a coordinate.

The relation x_m = x_s × f is used, where x_m is a coordinate in metres, x_s is a source coordinate and f is metres per source unit. Metre identity, SI milli and centi prefixes, international inches and international feet are supported. The corresponding symbols are m, mm, cm, in and ft. Kernel surface areas and volumes are scaled by f² and f³ respectively.

STEP and IGES units are decoded from file text before kernel transfer.

FormatDeclarationAccepted units
STEPLength units in each GLOBAL_UNIT_ASSIGNED_CONTEXTSI_UNIT metre with no prefix, MILLI or CENTI; CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT INCH or FOOT with factors 0.0254 m or 0.3048 m respectively.
IGESGlobal parameter 14 (units flag) and 15 (units name)Flags 1 (in), 2 (mm), 4 (ft), 6 (m), 10 (cm); flag 3 with IN, INCH, MM, FT, M or CM.

Missing declarations require caller units. A blank IGES flag and name do not silently default to inches. Conflicting STEP contexts, malformed or mismatched conversion factors, conflicting IGES flags and names, unsupported units and an IGES model space scale (Global parameter 13) other than 1 are refused. STEP units are cross-checked against the kernel report. The kernel output unit is set to the resolved source unit, so the conversion to metres is applied once.

No geometric acceptance limit is assigned. Size plausibility, closure, structural suitability and converter fidelity are NOT_EVALUATED.

4. Geometry model

World coordinates are returned as indexed point, line, polyline, triangle or polygon entities. Coordinate-system transforms precede length conversion. A closed polyline repeats its first index.

DXF layer and handle metadata are retained. Mesh appearance, normals, materials and groups are omitted. The source-unit decision records whether the basis is the file, the caller or both. The meshio STL decoder deduplicates exact-coordinate corners into shared point indices before unit conversion; source corner numbering is not preserved. Face vertex order and winding are retained. No tolerance-based welding, triangulation or healing is performed by the DXF and mesh readers. Two-facet ASCII and binary regressions check that six corners with one repeated position produce five points and one shared index.

STEP and IGES results also contain a BrepSummary in geometry.brep. B-rep means boundary representation. These properties come from the kernel shape, independently of the display triangles.

FieldMeaning
solids, shells, facesCounts of distinct topology entities.
bounding_boxMinimum and maximum corners of exact geometry in metres.
volume_m3Sum over solids in cubic metres; None when no solid exists.
surface_area_m2Area in square metres over face occurrences, including faces outside solids. A face shared between solids can contribute more than once although the face count is distinct.
is_validOCCT topology and geometry validity result; invalid shapes are returned with false and are not repaired.
linear_deflection_mChord deviation allowed for display triangles, in metres.

The display tessellation uses a chosen linear deflection of 10⁻³ of the bounding-box diagonal (one part in a thousand), and angular deflection of 0.5 rad (the OCCT default). This display setting is not an engineering acceptance limit. Each face is tessellated separately; source_id identifies the face. Points along shared edges repeat per face, and reversed faces have their triangle winding reordered. Exact area, volume and bounding box are not derived from this mesh. No analysis mesh quality or closure criterion is established.

5. Supported formats

Supported primitives are bounded explicitly. Unlisted DXF modelspace entities cause the read to fail without returning a partial drawing.

  • DXF: points, lines, straight lightweight and legacy polylines, and triangular or quadrilateral faces.
  • DXF exclusions: arcs, bulges, width, thickness, fitted curves, polygon meshes, polyfaces, blocks, annotations and other unlisted entities. Paperspace is outside scope.
  • STL: ASCII and binary triangle geometry. Header names do not establish units.
  • OBJ: polygon faces with positive or relative negative position indices. Appearance is discarded. Line, point, freeform, continuation and unlisted records are refused.
  • STEP (.step, .stp) and IGES (.iges, .igs): all transferable roots combined into one shape with faces. Zero or incomplete root transfers and wireframe-only shapes are refused.
  • STEP and IGES exclusions: assembly structure, per-part units, names, colours, layers and product and manufacturing information are not returned. Input text must be UTF-8; raw legacy 8-bit names are refused.
  • DWG: explicit converter configuration and the ordinary DXF unit gate.

6. Use

Optional reader dependencies are loaded when their formats are requested. The drawings extra supplies ezdxf; mesh supplies meshio; cad-occ supplies cadquery-ocp for STEP and IGES. The repository constraint numpy<2 applies. Importing the package requires no reader dependency.

From the repository root, install the optional reader:

python -m pip install ".[cad-occ]"
from digitalmodel.cad import read_cad

drawing = read_cad("geometry.dxf")
mesh = read_cad("surface.stl", units="mm")
assert mesh.units == "m"

part = read_cad("part.step")  # Units come from the file declaration.
print(part.brep.volume_m3, part.brep.is_valid)

OCCT translator settings are process-wide. This package serialises its reads and restores settings it changes. Other code changing these settings concurrently in the same process is not coordinated.

The converter is configured with separate literal arguments containing {input} and {output}, a caller-selected positive timeout, and an existing writable scratch directory. Each attempt retains converted.dxf and converter.log in a fresh subdirectory. The converter runs without a shell and is trusted to respect its input. A DXF output that is missing, a symlink or a directory is refused.

7. Validation

Expected coordinates are established by independent hand calculations in synthetic fixture docstrings. Face indices and world-coordinate transforms are checked against the fixture definitions.

Unit failures, malformed input, non-finite coordinates, optional dependency failures and converter errors are covered. A fake converter copies a synthetic DXF. No real drawing or real converter output is represented as validated.

STEP and IGES unit parsing uses hand-written file fragments. Kernel round trips build a box and cylinder with build123d and write them with the same OCCT kernel used to read them. Independent hand expectations are 0.006 m³ and 0.22 m² for a 100 × 200 × 300 mm box, and 0.001π m³ and 0.045π m² for a cylinder of radius 50 mm and height 400 mm exported in inches. Volumes and areas are compared at relative 10⁻⁶, and bounding boxes at 10⁻⁶ m.

Tests cover missing and conflicting units, malformed conversion factors, zero and partial root transfers, malformed input and UTF-8 refusal. The contracts gate requires kernel imports and pins cadquery-ocp 7.9.3.1.1 and build123d 0.7.0 with numpy 1.26.4. Local kernel fixtures can skip if optional packages are absent; CAD_REQUIRE_KERNEL=1 makes missing kernel imports fail in that gate. Dedicated assertions check shell counts for solids and a closed shell without solid topology, and confirm that shared-face area is counted per solid occurrence. No externally authored STEP or IGES fixture is exercised; production-file fidelity and cross-platform behavior require further evidence.

8. Failure handling

Input failures are reported with CadReadError, and unit failures with its UnitsError subtype. Missing optional dependencies raise ImportError naming the required extra. STEP and IGES read failures, zero or incomplete root transfers, missing faces and tessellation failures raise CadReadError. Kernel validity is reported separately and does not establish transfer completeness or design suitability.

An absent unit basis is obtained from authoritative drawing information and supplied explicitly. Conflicting declarations are corrected at source. Unsupported geometry is exported to a supported representation with a documented conversion basis. Converter diagnostics are inspected after a failed conversion. Independent fidelity review remains necessary after a successful conversion.

9. Appendix

The source basis and quality evidence are recorded below. No physical drawing unit is inferred from an object name, comment or bounding box.

9.1 Data register and pre-flight evidence

All geometry fixtures are synthetic. No physical dimension is assumed. Geometry quality, source authenticity, design suitability and conversion fidelity are NOT_EVALUATED. The retained unit basis and explicit format exclusions define the scope of each result.

  • Unit definitions are sourced; expected coordinate products are independently calculated.
  • Unsupported geometry and conflicting units are refused.
  • No real drawing, converter or solver is used in the synthetic examples; STEP and IGES fixtures use the optional geometry kernel.
  • Kernel format exclusions and downstream geometric criteria are stated.

    Curved drawing entities, block expansion, assembly metadata and converter fidelity checks remain separate extensions. Analysis-specific geometric criteria are supplied by downstream consumers.
