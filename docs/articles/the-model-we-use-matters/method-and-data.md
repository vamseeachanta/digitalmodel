# Method and data

Article: "The model we use matters", The Deckhand Chronicles, revision 5 of 19 Nov 2026. This page holds in full what Appendix A of the article summarises.

The worked example uses the first-mode natural frequency of a two-dimensional rectangular tank from linear potential-flow theory [2]. Its inputs are the article's illustrative tank: length 1.0 m, fill depths 0.15 m and 0.70 m, and gravity 9.81 m/s^2. The deep-liquid shortcut replaces tanh(k h) with 1.

Public data set: AceEngineer publishes the illustrative tank inputs and the two schematics on the article page, 4 Nov 2026. These are illustrations rather than measured data; no CSV is required. The page states no separate data licence. The adapted data set is your own problem set.
