Engineering analysis criteria

Parametric studies, verification and reusable lookup datasets · Document ID: ENG-ANALYSIS-CRITERIA · Revision 1 · 13 September 2026 · Engineering owner: digitalmodel · Scope: ecosystem criteria requested by the user; T3 documentation review.

This document defines the requested reusable analysis acceptance criteria. The user explicitly requested these criteria for the repository ecosystem on 13 September 2026. They apply to planning and acceptance of new or materially revised engineering analyses across the repository ecosystem, including hand calculations and solver studies. It does not certify existing analyses, install cross-repository enforcement, authorize solver execution or replace issue-specific approval. These requirements take effect for new or materially revised analyses from Revision 1 under that user direction. Compliance coverage shall be recorded by repository and analysis; missing rollout records do not waive the requirements, and document availability does not demonstrate compliance.

Traceability: parametric benchmark go-by. Governing shared sources remain the agent data-handling contract, shared operating contract and calculation citation contract. This guide defines engineering deliverable criteria; shared workflow and source-ownership rules retain their authority.

1. Acceptance criteria

Review independence is assessed at the person or agent-session level: the authoring session's self-check shall not count as independent review. A separate non-author reviewer may use the same provider as the author, while the shared provider-diversity and reciprocal requirements still apply. For example, a non-author Codex reviewer plus an independent Claude reviewer supplies two independent review lanes; the authoring Codex session does not supply either lane merely by performing its own checks.

The user-direction record preserves the request and clarification as an agent-recorded issue comment. It does not constitute an owner-applied approval label. For human-authored work, the analysis owner shall assign an appropriately qualified non-author reviewer; for joint work, the reviewer shall have contributed to none of the authorship streams being reviewed. Unavailability of that required independent reviewer shall block acceptance unless the user approves a recorded substitute under the same exception rule.

Applicable recorded standing approvals and delegated authority may satisfy the exception requirement without a new approval transaction. A new decision shall be requested only where existing authority does not cover the exact exception. Shared workflow/authorization contracts retain precedence; these criteria do not create duplicate approval requests for already-authorized unchanged scope.

Review terminates when the reviewed artifact content is unchanged and all findings have a recorded disposition. Residual provider disagreement shall be surfaced to the user with the minority position and supporting evidence, rather than concealed as consensus or automatically cycled. The 13 September 2026 reciprocal-review directive requires fresh exact-content review after corrections; an older inline-correction shortcut shall not substitute for that explicit requirement.

Independent numerical verification requires a separately derived calculation or external reference not generated by the production method, and a checker who did not author the calculation being checked. Repeating the same implementation or changing only its mesh establishes neither independent method verification nor physical validation. Independent review requires a reviewer who did not author the reviewed content; it need not recompute every value unless its assigned verification scope requires that. Physical validation shall additionally compare against relevant measured or experimentally established behavior, with uncertainty and applicability stated.

Every exception to these deliverable criteria shall be approved by the user/engineering owner or a delegated approver other than the analysis author. The basis and dataset manifest shall record the approver, date, exact scope, technical rationale and affected intended use. An exception shall not authorize departure from a governing design requirement or source-rights constraint. The parametric-study exception in AC-04 shall follow this same approval and recording rule.

AC-09 — output lookup datasetEach accepted parametric study shall deliver a reusable machine-readable dataset and a documented query example. AC-10 — identity and revisionsParameter keys, row identity and dataset revision shall be distinct. Canonical serialization, precision and null representation shall be specified. Parameter keys shall use exactly comparable decimal strings, scaled integers or declared grid indices; binary floating-point values shall not serve as implicit exact-key identity. The manifest shall declare quantization without silently rounding off-grid requests into matches, and refusals shall distinguish malformed keys from valid keys absent from the declared design. Every query shall resolve to one explicit dataset/method revision.Hash/readback checks and roundtrip tests shall guard provenance. New revisions shall publish a complete declared dataset, or explicit verified inheritance; implicit mixed revisions shall be rejected. Superseded data shall be excluded from ordinary lookup and clearly labelled in historical audit mode.
AC-11 — bounded lookupExact-case lookup shall be the default. Requests shall declare parameter units and intended use. Query keys shall use the same canonicalization as stored keys without precision loss or rounding an off-grid value into a match. Nonidentical keys shall be refused; nearest-neighbor, tolerance-window and snapping behavior shall not masquerade as exact lookup. Unit conversion shall either be refused or explicitly specified, validated and recorded in the response. Ordinary lookup shall require declared intended use and response-specific readiness; invalid or unresolved responses shall return no engineering answer. Diagnostic/audit access shall retain their status and reasons. A valid computed exceedance of a design limit shall remain reportable as a calculated failure, distinct from an invalid calculation. Off-grid inputs shall be rejected unless a separately validated interpolation method is enabled. Extrapolation shall be refused unless explicitly scoped, justified and validated.Interpolation shall have independent off-grid validation, an error bound meaningful to the engineering decision and applicability checks. Missing validation shall not default to zero error. Discontinuities, regime changes and invalid/unqualified cells shall block interpolation. Individual responses shall be validated before forming governing envelopes.
AC-12 — report and reviewThe engineering report shall include basis, parameter ranges, method, governing cases, complete dataset link, coverage, validation and limitations. Every analysis, including human-authored and jointly authored work, shall receive review by a party that did not author the reviewed content, with provider count governed by the shared review scale. Codex-authored plans and code/artifacts shall receive independent Claude review of the exact current content; Claude-authored work shall receive independent Codex review of the exact current content. The shared T1/T2/T3 review scale shall additionally govern provider count; reciprocal review does not waive its requirements.Review evidence shall bind immutable revision or content hashes, verdict, findings and disposition. Default review posture shall be non-APPROVE and defect-hunting. Material changes shall require reciprocal review of the changed current content; historical transcripts shall not substitute. Provider identities and count follow the shared scale: T1 one provider, T2 two, T3 Claude, Codex and Agy/Gemini. An unavailable third Agy/Gemini lane may reduce T3 to T2 under the standing shared fallback rule, with UNAVAILABLE recorded. Unavailability of the required reciprocal Claude or Codex reviewer shall block acceptance unless the user explicitly approves a recorded exception under section 1, identifying the substitute independent reviewer. Invalid or verdict-ambiguous returned review output shall block acceptance rather than reduce the tier. At least one reviewer independent of the author shall remain. Blocking findings shall remain explicit.

An approved exception shall never silently waive a governing safety/design criterion.The handoff shall list accepted outputs, open checks, review coverage, retained evidence and next actions. Cleanup shall distinguish clean, expected residue and unexpected residue. Existing user approvals and source-rights boundaries shall remain in force.

2. Minimum deliverable contract

Published dataset revisions shall be immutable. Each response shall additionally carry generated_at and verification_evidence_ref; the manifest shall define the permitted intended_use values and their readiness requirements. The verification reference shall resolve to the case/response verification register and its evidence. Repository-resident manifests and generation entry points shall reference bulk case data at the location required by the existing residence contract.

Interpolation or extrapolation enablement shall require explicit approval under the section 1 exception rule before it can return engineering answers. The approval shall identify method revision, validation evidence, parameter domain and intended use. The dataset manifest shall record the enabled modes; each non-exact response shall disclose its mode, resolved method revision, validation reference and applicable error bound. A disabled mode shall refuse the request.

Consuming analyses shall record the dataset ID and revision used. Supersession or withdrawal shall identify recorded consumers and require a documented impact disposition for each, plus an errata entry naming affected responses and intended uses. Consumers outside the recorded registry shall be an explicit coverage gap; unknown consumption shall not be reported as no impact. Correction notification and publication shall follow the existing communication-authorization rules.

The deliverable package shall contain: an approved analysis basis and study configuration; source/citation metadata; deterministic inputs and case ledger; machine-readable responses and per-response status; independent verification and numerical-quality evidence as applicable; a revision manifest with digests; a lookup example with expected behavior for valid, off-grid and unqualified requests; and an HTML engineering report with review dispositions. Approved exceptions shall identify the omitted deliverable, technical reason, approver and affected intended use.

A minimum response record shall contain criteria_document_id, criteria_revision, dataset_id, dataset_revision, parameter_key, row_hash, input_values, units, load_case, response_name, value, criterion_id, method_revision, calculation_status, verification_status, intended_use, limitations, source_refs, input_digest, output_digest. Native records shall additionally identify solver/build, model and mesh/time-step revision, convergence measures and retained raw-output references. Directional or time-series subresults shall use explicit component keys; envelope values shall identify the governing component/case. The manifest shall bind code revision and declared canonicalization rules.

Dataset revisions shall be reproducible from retained authorized evidence. Client/measured originals and licensed-standard exclusions shall follow the shared data-handling contract. Public availability shall not be treated as blanket permission to copy, transform or publish source content. Restricted evidence may support a private calculation only where its access and transformation rights permit that use; a public summary shall not claim reproducibility for readers who cannot access required restricted inputs.

3. Range selection and reporting criteria

The parameter study shall distinguish design variables from uncertainty, environmental/load combinations and numerical controls. Nominal, limiting and interaction cases shall be justified by the governing problem. The number of points shall follow response behavior and the intended lookup accuracy, rather than an ecosystem-wide fixed count. Exploratory results may drive adaptive refinement only under a recorded rule that preserves initial samples and makes coverage changes auditable.

Plots shall declare fixed slice values, axes and units. Missing or rejected samples shall break traces or be explicitly marked; curves shall not visually bridge unqualified regions. The complete dataset shall remain accessible even when charts show representative slices. Numerical precision shall reflect evidence and reporting rules rather than suggest unsupported accuracy. Captions shall follow the associated table or figure.
