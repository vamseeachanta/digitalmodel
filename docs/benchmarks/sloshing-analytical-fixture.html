Derivation

  1. Eigenmode basis: NASA SP-106, The Dynamic Behavior of Liquids in Moving Containers, with Applications to Space Vehicle Technology (1966 edition), section 2.2 'Rectangular Tank', equation (2.13b); https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19670006555.
  2. Incompressible irrotational flow gives Laplace(phi)=0 in a rigid rectangular tank.
  3. For x in [0,L], z in [-h,0], impermeable boundaries admit phi_n proportional to cos(k_n*x)*cosh(k_n*(z+h)), k_n=n*pi/L.
  4. Linear kinematic and dynamic free-surface conditions give omega_n^2=g*k_n*tanh(k_n*h), for positive integer n.
  5. Odd and even modes are eigenmodes; their forcing coupling is not calculated.
  6. Harmonic kinematics basis: OpenStax, University Physics Volume 1 (2016 edition), section 15.1 'Simple Harmonic Motion'; https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-1-simple-harmonic-motion.
  7. T1=2*pi/omega1; T=period_ratio*T1; omega=omega1/period_ratio; acceleration amplitude=displacement*omega^2.
  8. Hydrostatic slope is derived from pressure-force balance: OpenStax, University Physics Volume 1 (2016 edition), sections 5.3 'Newton's Second Law' and 14.1 'Fluids, Density, and Pressure'; https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/5-3-newtons-second-law; https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-1-fluids-density-and-pressure.
  9. Resolving pressure forces gives d(eta)/dx=-acceleration/g and the spatial end-to-end magnitude L*displacement*omega^2/g; this is not temporal peak-to-peak elevation.
