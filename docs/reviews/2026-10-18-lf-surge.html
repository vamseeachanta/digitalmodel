Unresolved findings

MAJOR: the three-hour maximum criterion in test_slow_drift_monte_carlo.py is unmet. The estimate is 6.8 m versus 8.5 m from 256 synthetic random-phase records, 19.6% low against a 10% criterion. A strict expected failure preserves the criterion. The asymptotic approximation and its quadrature normalization require further primary-source review before design use.

The published Table 4 extremes are not reproduced: the complete complex QTF and calibrated model inputs are unverified for reuse. Table 1 damping and frequency pairs are tested as inputs to synthetic resonance cases only.

Checks and resolved findings

The test-first resonance check covers mass conversion, damping and mean offset. Pair counting, DC exclusion and full endpoint weights are checked against closed-form complex two-wave forcing and the existing Newman calculation. Signed receptance and phase invariance are checked independently. Kernel variance agrees with the force-filtered spectrum in an identity check. Malformed imaginary arrays previously broadcast silently during development; both matrix parts now have explicit shape checks, verified by a refusal test.

Drift tests: 69 passed and 1 strict expected failure initially; 73 passed and 1 strict expected failure after the API correction and added refusal cases. Structure and implementation-map tests: 94 passed before and after. Repository structure check and engineering register: PASS. Ruff: PASS for all four changed Python files. Bandit: no findings in the two calculation modules.

The catalogue freshness test fails at test_model_catalogue.py:365. The test, renderer and catalogue inputs are unchanged by these corrections. This existing failure is separate from the drift calculations.

The extreme is no longer re-exported from drift_forces. A regression test pins this boundary; experimental use requires importing slow_drift_response. Direct force-spectrum refusal cases cover nonuniform grids, malformed imaginary arrays and negative spectra. Identity checks use a relative tolerance of 1e-10. The implementation map labels QTFData as a related model that these functions do not accept; their input is QTFMatrix.
