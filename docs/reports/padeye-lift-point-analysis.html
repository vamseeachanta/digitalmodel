This calculation compares nominal single-plate and direct-shear weld demands with explicit supplied allowables. A passing comparison establishes only that these assumed demands do not exceed those allowables. The direct-shear calculation requires an effective throat of at least 9.0 mm using the supplied illustrative allowable and total effective length. This minimum is rounded upward for display. Method and assumptions

Let P = static sling tension × dynamic amplification × skew factor. These factors are required load-case inputs, without automatic rule minima. The sling tension already includes its angle and load share. For plate thickness t, pin diameter d, hole diameter D, width W and hole-centre distance e to the loaded free edge:

  • Projected bearing area A_b = d t; nominal bearing stress = P / A_b.
  • Net section A_n = (W − D) t; nominal tensile stress = P / A_n.
  • Two clear shear planes A_s = 2 (e − D/2) t; nominal tear-out stress = P / A_s.
  • Effective weld area A_w = L a; direct shear = P / A_w.
  • Required effective throat a_required = P / (L τ_allow).
  • Utilization = demand / supplied allowable; nominal PASS requires utilization ≤ 1.

Normal stress uses MIT, Stress-Strain Curves (2001), equation (1). Direct shear uses MIT, Shear and Torsion (2000), page 2. The stated areas follow the assumed geometry and equilibrium; these sources provide nominal stress definitions, not ABS lug resistance equations. A centred, in-plane axial load and a concentric direct-shear weld group are required.

Limits and way forward

  • abs_compliance: NOT_EVALUATED. ABS lifting-appliance rules: applicable edition and clause evidence required. Accessible ABS equation and allowable evidence is unavailable. Supply the applicable edition, clauses and applicability assessment.
  • lifting_qualification: NOT_EVALUATED. NASA-STD-8719.9C (2024), section 13.3.2.4. Analysis alone does not establish lifting qualification. This clause conditions substitution of analysis for testing of permanently attached interfaces on documented rationale and approval by the designated authority; its applicability and approval are not established here.

Establish the applicable ABS edition, equation clauses, resistance limits and lifting-standard applicability. Review actual weld orientation and load transfer; where moments or normal throat stresses occur, use a full weld-group analysis. Then check pin bending and shear, contact and hoop stresses, interaction, cheek-plate load sharing, supporting structure, fatigue, fabrication, inspection and testing as applicable. None is established by these nominal comparisons.

The effective throat requirement is not a fillet-leg size, a fabrication minimum or a complete weld design. Geometry may later be supplied by a drawing reader; this calculation does not infer dimensions from drawings or a solver.
