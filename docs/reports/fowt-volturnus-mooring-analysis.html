1 Executive Summary

The governing intact design offset is (29.25, -9.781e-16) m, with line utilisation 0.4863 at 0° in the extreme sea; The governing damaged design offset is (803.4, 14.13) m, with line utilisation 0.2903 at 30° in the extreme sea. Coupled dynamics and mean wave drift are not included. No allowable excursion or anchor holding criterion is established; tension strength alone does not establish stationkeeping acceptance.

No evaluated utilisation exceeds 1.0 under the stated factors and assumptions.

2 Introduction

This analysis quantifies first-order motions, constrained static offsets and quasi-static mooring demand of the public IEA 15 MW reference turbine on the VolturnUS-S semi-submersible. It supports selection of subsequent coupled assessments; it does not establish a site-specific design.

3 Design Data

3.1 Site data

Water depth is 200 m. The environment is a public reference envelope without a geographical site.

3.2 Structure data: turbine

The rated reference turbine is 15 MW. Rated horizontal thrust is 2.457 MN at hub height 150 m above still water. Aggregated mass is 20.27 million kg; centre of gravity is (-0.3416, -0.004657, -1.575) m.

3.5 Criteria

Utilisation is tension multiplied by a factor and divided by minimum breaking load; the limit is 1.0. Intact factor 2 and damaged factor 1.43 are assumed, based on previous experience. No factor is attributed to a standard. No offset limit is established; tension utilisation alone does not establish stationkeeping acceptance.

3.6 Method

The method and assumptions are defined in Section 4.

3.7 Load cases

Three headings × two sea states × two configurations give 12 cases. Headings 0°, 30° and 60° span half the 120° sector of the three-line geometry. RNA orientation remains on the reference axis while the applied horizontal load and wave headings vary. An all-heading response envelope of the asymmetric turbine–platform system is not established. The damaged configuration removes the most loaded intact line for each sea and heading.

4 Analysis Methodology

First-order added mass, radiation damping and excitation are calculated with Capytaine 3.0.0. Elastic catenary equilibrium, tangent stiffness and line tensions are calculated with MoorPy 1.3.0. Only horizontal mean equilibrium is solved. Mean heave and attitude remain fixed at the reference draft; reaction forces and moments are reported in Table 10.

The submerged boundary is the union of circular columns and rectangular radial pontoons with an irregular-frequency lid. Two panel resolutions are evaluated. Natural periods solve det[K−ω²(M+A(ω))]=0 with six-degree coupling and zero-offset mooring stiffness for the intact system. Radiation damping is supplemented in surge, heave and pitch by 5 % critical damping, assumed, based on previous experience; 2 % and 10 % sensitivities are carried. The additional coefficient is Bextraᵢ(ω)=2ζ√[(Mᵢᵢ+Aᵢᵢ(ω))Kᵢᵢ]; its frequency dependence is retained, and the response remains linear in motion amplitude.

The response is obtained from [K−ω²(M+A)−iω(B+Bextra)]q=F over 0.05–2 rad/s, consistent with the exp(−iωt) excitation convention. The intact system uses zero-offset stiffness; the damaged system uses stiffness at its constrained mean equilibrium. Aligned load and wave headings are assumed. The xz plane is a reflection plane; lateral mass-centre offset and inertia cross-products that break this reflection are projected to zero, an assumed symmetry approximation.

For response spectral density |RAO|²S, m₀ and m₂ are frequency moments. Significant single amplitude is 2√m₀; the Rayleigh three-hour maximum is √[2m₀ ln N], where N=T/(2π√(m₀/m₂)), following Longuet-Higgins (1952). Each horizontal maximum is added independently with both signs to the static mean, and the largest tension of four combinations is retained. This envelope is an assumption about concurrent horizontal excursions; the two maxima need not occur simultaneously. Elastic catenary relations follow Irvine (1981). Seabed friction is assumed zero; anchor holding capacity is not established by line-strength utilisation.

The published RNA tensors do not satisfy Iref=ICG+m[(r·r)I−rrᵀ]. Cells F24:K24 and L24:Q24 in the public tabular data give diagonal discrepancies of 27.2 %, 34.2 % and 35.9 %. Treating F24:K24 as about the RNA centre of gravity or about tower top produces the two analysed readings. The tower-top reading is adopted as assumed; exchanging the tensor labels reduces the full-tensor relative residual from 31.7 % to 23.1 %. Retaining F24:K24 as the trusted endpoint is a further assumption; neither reading resolves the published inconsistency. Published RNA mass and centre of gravity are retained separately.

6 Key Findings

6.1 Governing line demand

For 0° extreme intact, line 1 governs at utilisation 0.4863 against the limit 1.0 under the factors in Section 3.5.

For 30° extreme damaged (line 1 removed), line 3 governs at utilisation 0.2903 against the limit 1.0 under the factors in Section 3.5.

No offset limit is established; tension utilisation alone does not establish stationkeeping acceptance.

6.2 Validation and applicability

Hydrostatic and natural-period comparisons in Tables 5–6 are the validation of this model; the stated differences are retained rather than treated as equivalence. Static offsets in Allen et al. Section 3.1 are not tabulated and are not compared.

6.3 Published inertia inconsistency

The public RNA tensor cells F24:K24 and L24:Q24 disagree with the parallel-axis identity by 27.2 %, 34.2 % and 35.9 % on the three diagonals. The tower-top reading is adopted as assumed because it gives the smaller identity residual; the inconsistency remains unresolved. Appendix A quantifies the effects of both readings and of additional damping.

6.4 Symmetry approximation

The 0° sway, roll and yaw response ratio is 1.257e-15 relative to peak response under reflection projection. This ratio concerns the assumed symmetric model; it does not establish exact symmetry of the published mass tensor.

6.5 Large damaged excursion

The greatest damaged design excursion is 814.3 m for 0° extreme damaged (line 1 removed), compared with 39.26 m for the greatest intact excursion, a ratio of 20.7. The calculated two-line equilibrium lies at a large displacement. A mooring-layout and allowable-excursion assessment is required before stationkeeping acceptance; no allowable-excursion criterion is established.

No evaluated utilisation exceeds 1.0 under the stated factors and assumptions.

7 Limitations

The calculation is quasi-static, at one draft, for a public reference platform with no site. Mean drift, low-frequency response, line dynamics, coupled aero-hydro-servo dynamics, aerodynamic damping and control are absent. Additional linear damping, symmetry projection, aligned headings, independently combined horizontal maxima and fixed mean attitude are assumed. The required constraint reactions are external to the free-floating system. The extreme sea combined with rated thrust is an envelope, not a parked load case. Inertia ambiguity and period-validation differences limit quantitative confidence; line acceptance remains conditional on that basis. No offset limit is established; tension utilisation alone does not establish stationkeeping acceptance. Linearisation and the evaluated frequency band cannot represent every response outside this model.

8 Recommendations

The next campaign should include coupled time-domain statics, six free decays, and operating and parked cases using a licensed coupled solver. It requires the controller source, a load-case table with source attribution and an execution manifest. Mean drift should be obtained from a second-order solution and evaluated with low-frequency response. A site-specific environment, resolved RNA inertia origin, free mean attitude, line dynamics and a physical pretension/design assessment are required before design acceptance.
