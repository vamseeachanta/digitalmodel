Dimension bases and reference limits

For manual curation, displacement_lt denotes total ship weight at the source-stated design condition. The source must identify standard, lightship or full-load explicitly, with the included payload and consumables. Deadweight is not displacement. Mean molded design draft and waterline length must share that loading condition; molded depth is taken amidships to the uppermost continuous deck at side. The molded datum and deck are curation conventions, recorded in source with water density and trim. An unknown basis leaves the value blank; comparisons require matched bases. Equality between full_load_lt and displacement_lt is permitted for a full-load basis, so the validator's inequality does not prove that the two fields have different loading conditions.

The public USNA EN400 references define the underlying geometry and total-weight concepts; they do not prescribe the curation conventions or verify stored entries. Chapter 2, sections 2.3.2 and 2.5.1 covers waterlines, depth and draft; Chapter 3, section 3.1.1.1 defines displacement as total ship weight. Both PDFs were read text-first on 14 November 2026, with HTTP 200 responses. Their revision dates are not established by the retrieved documents. These citations support definitions, not computational input qualification.

The generator schema and identifier sentence checks are documentation checks. The numbered migration example separately exercises the existing validator. The added dimension-basis check also checks documentation, without adding runtime validation. The focused suite contains 27 tests: 26 passed at the starting revision; the new documentation check failed before the header changed; all 27 then passed with no failures or skips. Stored dimension bases are not validated by that result.
