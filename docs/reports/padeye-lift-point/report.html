1. Summary

The lift point is a single plate with a centred pin hole and a balanced welded base. Analysis is performed to determine nominal plate stresses and direct weld throat demand; key results are summarized below.

  • The supplied synthetic plate targets are met at the illustrative load.
    • The calculated bearing, net tension and tear-out demands are 180.0, 90.0 and 64.3 MPa.
  • The illustrative weld target is exceeded.
    • The calculated demand is 75.0 MPa against a synthetic 60.0 MPa target. An effective throat of 10.000 mm is required for that target at the same load and length.
  • A lifting capacity remains unassigned.
    • American Bureau of Shipping (ABS) acceptance is NOT_EVALUATED because applicable rule criteria and the complete connection basis are absent.

2. Analysis basis

A centred axial load is applied in the plane of a uniform plate. Direct force is transferred through a balanced base weld; connection moments are excluded by this idealisation.

ASSUMED - to be confirmed: all geometry, loads, factors and comparison targets are synthetic inputs. The targets demonstrate comparisons and do not represent ABS design allowables.

Force divided by area is used for nominal stress. International Maritime Organization (IMO) lifting guidance requires a design basis acceptable to the relevant Administration; nominal stress calculations alone do not establish that basis. ABS Publication 152 is identified as a candidate rule source, but its applicable edition and design clauses remain unresolved.

Each supplied target is used only with its matching stress definition. A result at or below its supplied target is marked PASS for that comparison; a missing criterion is marked NOT_EVALUATED. No material strength, code coefficient or acceptance limit is inferred.

3. Physical system and idealisation

A rectangular plate head with a straight loaded edge is represented. Plate width is measured across the load, and edge distance is measured along the load from the hole centre.

4. Calculation method

Nominal areas are formed from the entered effective dimensions. The following symbols are defined for the independent force and area calculation.

P is the factored sling force; S is the static tension in the selected sling leg; D is the dynamic amplification input; K is the skew input. The force is P = S × D × K. Here P = 100.0 × 1.2 × 1.5 = 180.0 kN.

For thickness t, plate width w, pin diameter d, hole diameter h and centre-to-edge distance e, the projected bearing area is d × t, the net tensile area is (w − h) × t, and the nominal net-ligament shear area is 2 × (e − h/2) × t. This convention follows NASA/TM-20230000313 (2023), page 58, equation 7.17, whose hole diameter d is mapped to h here. Only the area convention is adopted; its material limits and clevis load sharing are excluded. It is not a derivation of physical tangent-plane area or an AISC D5 check. Empirical lug interaction is excluded.

The nominal stress is P divided by the corresponding area, with force in newtons and area in square millimetres giving megapascals (MPa). For effective throat a and total effective weld length L, the weld area is a × L. The weld quantity is a nominal resultant stress on the throat, rather than resolved shear stress.

For a supplied weld resultant target q, the required effective throat is a = P/(L × q). Its conversion to a weld leg, minimum weld size, effective length and directional strength remain outside this calculation.

5. Plate results

The three plate demands are calculated at a factored sling load of 180.0 kN. They are below the supplied synthetic targets, while class acceptance remains NOT_EVALUATED.

The projected bearing area is 1,000.0 mm², the net tensile area is 2,000.0 mm², and the nominal shear area is 2,800.0 mm². These areas establish average stresses only; local contact, hoop stress and combined failure mechanisms remain unresolved.

6. Weld results

A nominal resultant stress of 75.0 MPa is calculated for the entered throat and effective length. The synthetic 60.0 MPa comparison target is exceeded.

At the illustrated load, an effective throat of 10.000 mm is required to meet the synthetic target. The nominal resultant comparison does not evaluate weld directional interaction.

7. Way forward

The illustrative weld shortfall is addressed by a calculated effective throat requirement. The calculation is conditional on the same direct load idealisation and synthetic target.

Increase the effective throat to at least 10.000 mm for the illustrative comparison, or limit the factored force to 144.0 kN for the entered throat and synthetic target. These alternatives follow from force divided by throat area; neither assigns a working load.

Before assigning a lifting capacity, establish the applicable class edition and clauses, material certificates, connection geometry, directional weld criteria, pin and support checks, and the required examination and load-test basis. The cited lifting guidance supplies the rationale for obtaining an applicable design and examination basis.

8. Validation

The calculated stresses are compared with independent hand calculations using the entered dimensions. Arithmetic agreement is established for this example; physical qualification is not established.

The independent areas are 50 × 20 = 1,000 mm², (160 − 60) × 20 = 2,000 mm², 2 × (100 − 30) × 20 = 2,800 mm² and 8 × 300 = 2,400 mm². With 180,000 N, the independent demands are 180.0, 90.0, 64.3 and 75.0 MPa. The independent throat calculation is 180,000/(300 × 60) = 10.000 mm.
