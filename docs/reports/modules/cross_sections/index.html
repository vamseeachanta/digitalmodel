Cross-section module analysis

Summary

The module calculates uniform concentric layer area, dry mass, submerged weight and linear elastic section properties with explicit SI inputs. The worked steel, coating and insulation example gives 370.3 kg/m dry mass and 1,739.7 N/m sealed submerged weight. These are arithmetic results under declared assumptions; no design acceptance is established.

Inputs

All calculation geometry is in metres, density in kg/m³, gravity in m/s² and modulus in Pa. The module uses explicit unit strings and does not use an implicit unit registry. The caller must convert other units before calculation; millimetre and inch inputs are rejected. The source fixtures retain their native units for IO and schematic reporting.

The example geometry and non-steel properties are assumed, based on previous experience, and chosen for transparent hand arithmetic; they are not a proposed pipe design. Steel density is from Engineering ToolBox (2004, updated 2025), Metals and Alloys - Densities, steel row; steel E is from OpenStax University Physics Volume 1 (2016), §12.3, Table 12.1 and stress-strain relation. Neither value qualifies a specific grade, temperature or product. Geometry is assumed for all layers.

Method

  1. A = π(OD − ID)(OD + ID)/4 and m/L = ρA: OpenStax Prealgebra 2e (2020), §9.5, circle area; OpenStax University Physics Volume 1 (2016), §14.1, Eq. 14.1 and Table 14.1.
  2. m_total/L = ΣρᵢAᵢ. Outer diameter builds from the first ID plus twice the summed thicknesses.
  3. W_sub/L = (m_total/L − ρ_water A_displaced)g: OpenStax University Physics Volume 1 (2016), §14.4, Archimedes' principle. A_displaced = πOD_outer²/4 for a sealed empty bore, or ΣAᵢ for a freely flooded bore.
  4. I = π(OD⁴ − ID⁴)/64 about any centroidal diameter: subtract the two circle results in Engineering Statics: Open and Interactive, §10.2, Eq. 10.2.10 (undated public edition, accessed 2026-10-22). The implementation factors the fourth-power difference to reduce cancellation.
  5. Z = I/(OD_outer/2). With σ = Eε and ε = ε₀ + κy, integration gives N = ε₀ΣEᵢAᵢ and M = κΣEᵢIᵢ; hence EA = ΣEᵢAᵢ and EI = ΣEᵢIᵢ: OpenStax University Physics Volume 1 (2016), §12.3, Table 12.1 and stress-strain relation. All layers share strain and curvature; Z alone does not establish a composite stress capacity.

Worked example

For the three layers, A/π = 0.0125, 0.0175, 0.0225 m². Therefore m/(Lπ) = 98.125 + 17.5 + 2.25 = 117.875 kg/m. Sealed displacement/π is 0.0625 m²; W/(Lπ) = (117.875 − 62.5) × 10 = 553.75 N/m. These expressions are independent hand arithmetic from the input register, recorded at the precision needed to reproduce them.

Validation evidence

The tests in tests/subsea/cross_sections/ compare calculated values to independent hand arithmetic, including solid circles, annuli, thin walls, bonded layers, unit round trips, non-finite inputs and every validation-report branch. Thin-wall OD-based area overestimates by t/(OD − t); the test bounds its own chosen geometry. Floating-point tolerances are arithmetic allowances and do not imply engineering acceptance.

The report test regenerates both artifacts twice, compares bytes, checks displayed results against live calculations and detects stale output. It also changes a production result to ensure the report follows the computation.

PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/reporting/build_cross_sections_module_report.py
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/reporting/build_cross_sections_module_report.py --check

Limits of use

Concentric, homogeneous, contiguous layers only. The calculation API requires exact shared boundaries in metres; the fixture schema's existing input-consistency tolerance is not a physical gap allowance. Radial geometry must satisfy 0 ≤ ID < OD. Every mass layer needs a density; every stiffness layer needs E. No material property is inferred from its name.

The bonded elastic model excludes slip, ovalisation, plasticity and temperature dependence. Sealed buoyancy excludes bore contents mass; flooded buoyancy assumes free water access. Negative submerged weight means upward force. Geometric section modulus does not establish stress distribution between dissimilar materials.

NOT_EVALUATED

  • Design-code compliance, strength, collapse, buckling, fatigue and corrosion allowance.
  • Seawater conditions, contents mass, trapped pressure, water absorption and end effects.
  • Packed cable and umbilical mechanical properties, helical lay and manufacturing packing.
  • Fixture mass and stiffness where complete material inputs are missing.
  • Thermal/electrical ratings and licensed solver comparison.

Way forward

Supply documented product dimensions, densities, moduli, contents and environmental values before using these quantities in a design. Establish layer bonding and bore condition first. Perform the applicable strength and stability assessment separately; a numerical section property is not evidence of system acceptance.

  • Correct empty report selection and preserve the open bore in radial figures.
  • Avoid comparing packed-component diameter magnitudes in incompatible units.
