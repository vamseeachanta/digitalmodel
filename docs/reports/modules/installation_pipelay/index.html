4. Analysis Methodology

Small-curvature outer-fibre strain is OD/(2R), and elastic bending stress is E times that strain. Axial stress is force divided by steel area; the demonstrated tensile outer-fibre stress is their sum. The supplied fraction times supplied strength is the arithmetic threshold. Utilisation is total stress divided by that threshold; percent strength is total divided by strength times the percent conversion [S1, S2, S4]. These geometric and ratio identities introduce no standard-derived criteria. The route calculation balances F/R = μw [S1, S3, S4].

6. Key Findings

The combined-stress result meets its supplied arithmetic criterion (220.0 compared with 300.0 N/mm²) [S1, S2]. The independent force-equilibrium example checks radius in m, as required by N divided by N/m [S1, S3, S4]. These results do not establish installation suitability.

The stress module's inconsistent force/area unit example is corrected to distinguish N/mm² from N/m². The executable examples are covered by the independent arithmetic test [S2, S4].

7. Limitations and Not Covered

Weld-repair acceptance is NOT_EVALUATED: the fractional-cap/minimum-length interpretation is unresolved, and the existing expected-failure test is retained [S3, S5]. Coating response is NOT_EVALUATED because strain transfer, material limits and the governing model are unverified. No default criterion or standard-derived value is reproduced or changed.

The stress combination describes a tensile outer fibre; signed compression, the opposite fibre, equivalent stress, ovalisation, buckling, plasticity and load interaction require separate assessment. Physical sign and geometry validation are incomplete. The integrity helpers do not systematically reject nonfinite values. The demonstrated inputs are finite and positive; this report does not qualify those input domains [S2, S3]. Dynamic lay behavior, stinger contact, soil variability and native solver comparisons are not calculated.

8. Recommendations

Before engineering use, supply the load case, effective versus wall tension definition, material certificate, qualified geometry and an approved installation criterion. Supply measured pipe-soil friction and submerged weight for route equilibrium. Resolve the repair-length conflict against the governing edition and repair clause before adopting either helper's result. Obtain coating-section evidence and strain limits before using a coating acceptance flag. Independently check the buoyancy and catenary methods with qualified boundary conditions. These missing inputs are required evidence, rather than zeroes or inferred criteria [S2–S5].
