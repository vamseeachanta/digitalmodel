2. Introduction

The scope is the public Python functions and methods declared in src/digitalmodel/subsea/pipeline/. Each is located by static source parsing; private helpers, generated dataclass methods and imported aliases are outside the declaration inventory. The executed examples use only geometry, unrestrained thermal expansion and factor multiplication. Other workflows are inspected without execution.

The inputs are deliberately synthetic and separately defined for each calculation. The annulus is not a pressure-design example. No material grade, soil, loading spectrum or operating acceptance criterion is inferred from these arithmetic inputs.

4. Analysis Methodology

A — independent annulus integration. In polar coordinates, dA = r dr dθ. Integration over radii from one to two metres and a complete revolution gives wall area 3π m². The bore and outside disks have areas π and 4π m². With y = r sin θ, integration of y² dA gives the centroidal second moment 15π/4 m⁴. Radial separation gives thickness one metre. These identities supply the independent test oracles.

B — independent strain integration. Uniform strain ε = α ΔT is integrated over the reference length: ΔL = ∫ ε dx. The supplied coefficient and heating/cooling changes give strains +1/5000 and −1/5000 and growth +1/5 m and −1/5 m. Reported strains are recovered from public-function growth divided by reference length.

C — independent supplied arithmetic. The arbitrary capacity is multiplied separately by each arbitrary caller factor. Exact rational multiplication supplies the independent expected pressures. A computed burst pressure is never used as an oracle. The input parameter name burst_pressure in the existing API does not qualify the synthetic capacity supplied here.

The hand-value tests use relative tolerance 1e−12 for binary floating-point rounding only. That computational tolerance is not an engineering acceptance criterion. Source comments, tests of proportionality and implementation self-comparisons do not establish a standard equation or empirical factor.

6. Key Findings

The available-dimension geometry routes recover the declared annulus. Heating and cooling retain their signed free-growth response. The factor API maps the supplied design, incidental and hydrostatic-test factors to their corresponding output products. Normal public imports now resolve the local calculation modules.

The inventory does not transfer these results to engineering restraint, free-span or pressure-capacity methods. In particular, free thermal growth does not predict buckling or walking, and factor multiplication does not qualify its supplied capacity.

7. Limitations and Not Covered

Buckling restraint, axial feed-in, imperfection response, soil friction, upheaval resistance and mode constants require separate independent cases. The legacy thermal-buckling route references helpers absent from its class; the legacy upheaval route contains an unfinished eigenvalue branch. Those workflows remain NOT_EVALUATED and are not executed or repaired here.

Span frequency boundary, sag and seabed-proximity corrections, onset thresholds, VIV response, fatigue curves and allowable lengths need independent equation and factor evidence. Passing existing span regressions does not establish these sources. Collapse, burst, combined loading, derating and pressure-sizing routes require equation-specific and edition-specific evidence plus qualified material and corrosion inputs. No licensed solver is imported or run.

Geometry validation is checked for the consistent example, not certified for every invalid input. Composite geometry, reliability, installation and workflow declarations are inventoried without qualification. This report supplies neither a production design nor approval for service.

8. Recommendations

The way forward is to obtain edition-specific equations and applicability limits for each engineering method, then independently worked cases for restraint, lateral and upheaval response, span frequency and empirical corrections, and pressure capacities. Material properties, corrosion basis, soil inputs, boundary conditions and acceptance criteria shall be supplied before a design conclusion can be calculated.

Each unqualified route shall retain NOT_EVALUATED until its own evidence is reviewed. The tracking issue remains open while the requested engineering methods are unqualified. The present arithmetic examples can be rebuilt with the command in the accompanying README.
