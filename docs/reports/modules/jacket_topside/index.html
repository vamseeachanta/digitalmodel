Jacket and topside module analysis

Structural tubular geometry, software limits and evidence required for capacity assessment.

1. Executive Summary

Independent annulus integrals and direct joint ratios support the geometric results. Zero-demand checks establish demand arithmetic at finite positive returned capacity only. Nonzero member capacity, joint capacity and damage acceptance remain NOT_EVALUATED. No design compliance is certified [1–4, 9].

2. Introduction

The module exposes tubular section properties, member stress checks, joint ratios and classification, and a simplified punching shear check [3, 4, 15]. A workflow adapts these outputs to JSON and CSV [5]. Adjacent dent and damage screens expose separate applicability and escalation contracts [6, 7]. The review is bounded to these module files; it does not qualify a structural system or a standard.

Plan: retain synthetic inputs, derive geometry independently, exercise zero demand through public functions, inventory unresolved methods, then rebuild the complete delivery and compare its bytes [9, 11, 12]. No source-data or standard-derived coefficient changes are made.

3. Design Data and Basis

Every input is a declared synthetic assumption, not a measurement, material grade or design criterion. Positive material strength and modulus avoid zero-capacity degeneracy; effective length is supplied explicitly. Member loads are stresses in ksi; joint loads are actions in kip and kip-in. Ratios use unit 1. The brace angle is measured from the chord axis. Side and gap inputs are unused for this single-brace case [3, 4, 9, 10].

5. Detailed Results

Independent annulus calculation

Let R = 6 in and a = 11/2 in [1]. With dA = rho d(rho) d(theta), integration around a full turn gives A = integral integral rho d(rho) d(theta) = pi(R²-a²) = 23*pi/4 in². The centroid lies at the common centre by symmetry. Integrating y² dA gives I = integral integral rho³ sin²(theta) d(rho) d(theta) = pi(R⁴-a⁴)/4 = 6095*pi/64 in⁴. Then S = I/R = 6095*pi/384 in³ and r = sqrt(I/A) = sqrt(265)/4 in [1, 2, 9]. These expected expressions are derived independently; displayed actual results below come from public properties [3, 12].

6. Key Findings

Annulus properties and single-brace ratios have a transparent independent basis. A zero result at zero demand demonstrates arithmetic only; it cannot distinguish a correct allowable from any other positive allowable [1, 9].

Review found no local correctness defect supported by the present independent cases. Production equations are preserved. Method limitations are recorded rather than converted into a standards-equation rewrite [3–7].

7. Limitations and Not Covered

Simplified bending treatment: the bending helper returns a fixed fraction of supplied yield strength without applying a diameter-to-wall branch. Its docstring discusses compactness but that discussion does not establish the implemented range. Nonzero bending capacity and interactions remain NOT_EVALUATED [3].

The combined check calculates a compression-based interaction regardless of the tension flag; the flag selects only the standalone axial check in the orchestrator. Nonzero tension interaction therefore needs an independent basis and explicit intended-behaviour review [3].

Joint classification is geometric and simplified. The chord-load factor description and implemented reduction differ in their stated dependencies. The applied shear routine and geometry-factor comments also need a reconciled angle convention. The perpendicular zero-demand case cannot resolve these questions [4]. No numeric standard coefficients are reproduced or changed here.

The workflow can report PASS for an empty collection, and its PASS status reports an implementation threshold rather than engineering qualification [5]. Direct dataclass constructors lack comprehensive checks for non-finite inputs, invalid dimensions and inconsistent brace-array lengths [3, 4]. Robust input validation, multi-brace loading, fatigue, pressure, weld design and system reserve strength are outside this calculation.

Screening source citations and existing hand tests are inventoried, but their external study was not reopened for this report. Continuous applicability, experimental qualification, combined damage and engineering acceptance remain NOT_EVALUATED [6, 7, 13, 14]. No licensed solver is imported, queried or executed by this builder.

8. Recommendations

Before a nonzero capacity assessment, obtain an openly accessible authoritative worked example with an identifiable edition, clause title, complete dimensions, material basis, load conventions and applicability. If such evidence cannot be obtained, retain NOT_EVALUATED and arrange a qualified standards review. Reconcile bending compactness, axial interaction, chord-load dependence and angle conventions against that basis. Add independent nonzero and boundary regressions before changing any equation [3, 4].

Review empty-input workflow semantics and constructor validation in a bounded follow-up. For damage screening, retrieve and independently check the cited study and experimental evidence, then confirm support, bow, pressure and combined-damage limits before considering acceptance [5–7]. Keep the broader issue open.

10. Conclusions

Geometry and zero-demand arithmetic are reproducible within the stated synthetic case. Nonzero member and joint capacity, damage acceptance and standards compliance remain NOT_EVALUATED. The way forward is independent source qualification followed by targeted nonzero regression cases. No design compliance is certified.
