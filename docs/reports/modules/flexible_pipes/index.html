4. Method and equation sources

Elementary circle geometry is used as the independent basis: r_inner = D_inner / 2; r_outer = r_inner + t; A_wall = π(r_outer² − r_inner²); m_layer = ρ A_wall. The diameter factor is definitional [G].

The production mass expression uses mean diameter: m_layer = ρ π D_mean t. The comparator instead subtracts squared radii. Total dry mass is the sum of layer masses. Displaced mass is external density times the entire outer-envelope circle; content mass is bore density times the bore circle [G, C].

No standard factors or empirical coefficients are adopted. The source register identifies repository methods and the synthetic basis; it does not establish compliance with an external standard.

7. Correctness findings and known limitations

No geometry or full-wall mass defect is established by this case. The following interface findings limit broader use and are separate from the arithmetic comparison.

  • Layer mass uses the full annular wall even when metallic_area is supplied; structural_area uses the metallic override. Wound-wire mass and void fractions therefore require another basis [C].
  • Missing minimum bend radius gives zero curvature utilisation in verify_flexible_pipe, although the result docstring describes an infinite value when absent. An omitted check cannot establish bend-radius acceptance [D].
  • Non-finite values are not explicitly rejected by the layer input comparisons. The present case supplies finite positive dimensions and densities [C, G].

The concentric full walls omit interlocked layers, wire helices, gaps, friction, ovalisation and manufacturing tolerances. Stiffness estimates and structural screening interfaces are not qualified by a mass calculation [C, D, B].

10. Way forward

The synthetic geometry and mass comparison can support software regression for this case. Before applying the methods to a manufactured pipe, obtain the reference basis listed in Section 8, independently verify the corresponding method and define acceptance criteria for the governing case. Structural and API qualification remain open under issue 3343. No structural acceptance follows from the arithmetic disposition or deterministic rebuild.
