1. Executive Summary

The synthetic rectangular beam and ideal pin-ended column are calculated through the public module functions. The beam stress is compared with section equilibrium and integrated section properties; the column load is compared with the sine-mode eigenvalue [A–C]. The calculated values and units are presented in the Detailed Results table.

The beam stress is 16.0 MPa at (+y, +z) and 4.0 MPa at (−y, −z). The separate ideal pin-ended critical load is 493480.2 N [A–C, E, F]. These values reproduce the independent mechanics expressions under the stated roundoff criterion [I]; design acceptance remains NOT_EVALUATED.

IDEAL_MECHANICS_CHECKED applies only to the two worked expressions and their stated cases. All source-unqualified code capacities are NOT_EVALUATED for design use. No standard buckling curve, reduction factor or combined-capacity rule is qualified by these examples [E–H].

The beam width lies along y and its height along z. Coordinates are centroidal principal axes. Positive axial force and the supplied positive moment contributions produce positive normal stress under the implemented algebraic convention. The opposite corner reverses both bending terms without reversing the axial term [B, E]. This is distinct from the compression-positive convention of the buckling and combined-loading APIs.

The column is straight, prismatic, elastic and ideally pin ended, without initial eccentricity or imperfection. Effective length is prescribed rather than calculated from a frame. Its synthetic section is separate from the beam rectangle [A, C].

4. Analysis Methodology

Independent beam basis

For width b and height h, integration over −b/2 ≤ y ≤ b/2 and −h/2 ≤ z ≤ h/2 gives the area and second moments [B]:

A = ∫∫ dA = bh; I_y = ∫∫ z² dA = bh³/12; I_z = ∫∫ y² dA = hb³/12 [B].

The denominator follows from ∫z² dz = z³/3 evaluated at the symmetric bounds, with the remaining width integration. Normal-force and moment equilibrium then give the stress field in the module's signed convention [B, E]:

σ(y,z) = N/A + M_y z/I_y + M_z y/I_z [B].

SI geometry in metres with forces in newtons and moments in newton metres produces pascals, converted to MPa by division by 10⁶ [D]. An independent millimetre calculation uses N mm, mm² and mm⁴ and gives N/mm² = MPa. One metre equals 10³ millimetres; area and second moments therefore convert with the second and fourth powers, respectively [D]. Each term is isolated through a public beam call before the two corner calls [E, I].

Independent column basis

For a constant flexural rigidity EI, equilibrium gives the fourth-order displacement equation. A sine mode satisfies both zero displacement and zero bending moment at the pin ends [C]:

EI v'''' + P v'' = 0; v = sin(πx/L); (EI π⁴/L⁴ − P π²/L²)v = 0; P = π² EI/L² [C].

Substitution of the synthetic column inputs reduces EI/L² to 50000 N, hence P = 50000π² N. Doubling L multiplies its squared denominator by four and reduces P to one quarter [A, C]. The public column API uses elastic modulus in N/mm², second moment in mm⁴ and effective length in mm; it returns N [F]. This is an ideal elastic eigenvalue, not a design resistance.

The independent assertions allow 32 binary64 machine epsilons in relative error [I]. This allowance bounds roundoff in the small number of products, divisions and π evaluations; it is not an allowance for model error, geometry uncertainty or design-code deviation. Relative error is used for these nonzero references. No numerical tolerance is established for design qualification.

6. Key Findings

The worked functions reproduce the independent mechanics values within the roundoff-only criterion for the synthetic cases [I]. This establishes unit conversion and sign behavior at the stated points, plus inverse-square effective-length scaling. It does not establish general member design conformity.

Review found that transverse plate stress sigma_y was accepted but omitted from utilization. The method now raises ValueError for nonzero sigma_y, and the regression demonstrates rejection before any utilization is returned [F, I]. This rejection also propagates through panel, grillage and parametric-sweep callers that pass nonzero transverse stress. The former biaxial grillage and default-sweep tests are changed to require rejection because their returned results omitted transverse plate interaction. The default sweep contains such unsupported cases and now raises; a supported load set or a qualified biaxial model is required before that sweep can complete. Existing zero-transverse-stress behavior is retained.

7. Limitations and Not Covered

All code-derived plate resistance, column reduction and member capacities remain NOT_EVALUATED. Source editions, clause applicability, factors, section class, imperfections, residual stress, effective lengths and independent published capacity examples are absent. No numerical value from a licensed publication is reproduced or inferred.

The plate elastic aspect-ratio expression is approximate and does not search longitudinal mode numbers. The combined plate calculation supports no nonzero transverse stress. The column reduction routine accepts an unknown curve by silently selecting its default. The existing simplified linear combined-capacity formula can cancel signed moment contributions and omits axial tension from interaction [F, G]. These unresolved methods are inventoried, not qualified or silently corrected.

Domain validation is incomplete: nonpositive geometry, nonfinite inputs and invalid material or factor values are not comprehensively rejected. Zero or negative denominators may raise arithmetic errors or produce meaningless software dispositions [E–H]. The worked inputs avoid these domains; design acceptance cannot rely on their current handling.

8. Recommendations

The two worked cases may be used as reproducible software regression examples under the stated ideal assumptions. Before design use, the intended standard edition, clause titles, material certificates, section geometry, restraint/effective-length basis, load combinations and factor selection should be supplied and independently reviewed.

Plate modal behavior and transverse interaction require a defined supported model before implementation. Unknown column curves and signed member interactions require explicit API contracts and independent failing examples before correction. Capacity comparisons should then use an independently published worked case with its governing criteria. The tracking issue remains open while these requested methods remain unqualified.
