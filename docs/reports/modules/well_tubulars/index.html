Well tubulars module analysis

Casing, tubing, stress envelopes and rod strings: source inventory and a synthetic thin-wall calculation.

Arithmetic evidence only. Design acceptance and physical qualification are NOT_EVALUATED.

Calculation JSON · Byte manifest · Change history · Rebuild instructions

1. Summary

The documented thin-wall stress helpers are executed for internal pressure, reversed differential pressure and pure shear. Hoop stresses are 10000.0 psi and -10000.0 psi for the pressure cases; the equivalent stresses are 9380.8 psi, 14422.2 psi and 2598.1 psi, respectively [A, B]. Independent equilibrium and principal-stress checks cover these assumed cases only [C].

2. Context and scope

The package exposes product lookups, pressure profiles, axial loads, design-factor comparisons, stress-envelope coordinates and rod-string cycle checks. Their presence is an inventory fact, not qualification. Only compute_hoop_stress and compute_vme_stress are executed for engineering results in this report.

4. Method

The hoop helper replaces both pressure surfaces by the mean diameter and balances a longitudinal half-cylinder. The equivalent-stress helper assumes plane stress with zero radial normal stress and includes in-plane shear [A, B].

D = (OD + ID)/2; t = (OD − ID)/2
Δp D L = 2 σh t L
T = [[a, s, 0], [s, h, 0], [0, 0, 0]]
m = (a + h)/2; r = sqrt(((a − h)/2)² + s²)
Principal stresses: λ = (m + r, m − r, 0)
q = sqrt(((λ₁ − λ₂)² + (λ₂ − λ₃)² + (λ₃ − λ₁)²)/2)

The factors of two arise from cut-wall count, mean geometry and invariant normalization. The independent test uses equilibrium and tensor eigenvalues, rather than the expanded production expression [A, C].

5. Worked calculation

The hand derivation uses an arbitrary one-inch length, mean diameter 10.0 in and wall thickness 0.200 in. The positive-pressure resultant is 400.0 × 10.0 × 1.0 = 4000.0 lbf. The two cut strips have combined area 2 × 0.200 × 1.0 = 0.4 in², giving 10000.0 psi [A, B].

For the in-plane tensor [[6000.0, 2000.0], [2000.0, 10000.0]] psi, the characteristic equation gives 8000.0 ± sqrt(8000000.0) psi and zero. Principal differences give sqrt(88000000.0) psi. Reversing pressure alone gives −10000.0 psi hoop stress and principal values −2000.0 ± sqrt(68000000.0) psi and zero; the equivalent stress becomes sqrt(208000000.0) psi. Pure shear of 1500.0 psi has principal values (+1500.0, −1500.0, 0.0) psi and gives 1500.0 × sqrt(3) psi [A]. These are hand-derived expectations, not separate production results.

6. Checks

The independent regression compares signed hoop stress and equivalent stress against the force balance and principal differences above. The retained relative tolerance is 1e-12 and absolute tolerance is 1e-8 psi [A, C]. This rounding budget covers decimal geometry subtraction and square-root arithmetic; it is not model uncertainty or a physical accuracy bound.

Separate checks rebuild into fresh external directories, compare all delivered bytes, replay the public helpers, read every manifest digest independently, check local links and exercise tampered results. Negative controls change a value or a hoop sign and regenerate mutually consistent HTML, JSON and hashes; the independent numerical assertion still rejects them [C]. Replaying the same calculation is a freshness check only.

8. Limits / NOT_EVALUATED

The stress example assumes a straight circular uniform thin wall, small deformation, uniform loads and plane stress away from discontinuities. The mean-surface substitution omits the distinct inner/outer pressure-surface correction; absolute pressures and radial stress can matter. No universal thin-wall accuracy threshold is selected [A].

  • Collapse regimes, regime transitions and imperfection sensitivity: NOT_EVALUATED. Independent method and applicability evidence are absent.
  • Catalogue dimensions/grades, connections, anisotropy and sour-service criteria: NOT_EVALUATED. Qualified source editions, clauses and material/environment evidence are absent.
  • Design acceptance and default factors: NOT_EVALUATED. No design basis or acceptance policy is adopted.
  • Rod fatigue/service factors, wear, corrosion, ovality, stability, thermal loads and operating qualification: NOT_EVALUATED. This calculation supplies no evidence for them.

Open source-review findings

  • check_production_casing chooses the governing burst result but passes the tubing-leak profile to check_triaxial even when injection governs burst. Cross-case triaxial adequacy is not established [D].
  • The isotropic and anisotropic VME implementations switch quadratic roots halfway through a single axial sweep. Full envelope branch coverage is not established by this report. No envelope is plotted or used as an acceptance boundary [D].
  • The function named check_triaxial uses a plane-stress membrane invariant; it does not evaluate radial stress through the wall [D].

These findings require separate method review. No algorithm or acceptance policy is replaced here.

9. Way forward

The maintainers should obtain independent review of the stress derivation and resolve the source-review findings before selecting design load combinations. Qualification of a design requires a stated standard edition and applicable clauses, qualified product/material data, load and environment evidence, explicit acceptance factors and independent checks for every governing failure mode. Unqualified methods remain open; this report does not close their acceptance decisions.
