1. Summary

Normal public APIs evaluate the existing bare and coated synthetic example [E, C]. The submerged mass equivalents are 15.0 kg/m and 15.6 kg/m, respectively, rounded only for presentation. Independent polar integrals check the unrounded quantities [T]. These results describe fully immersed, uniform sections with a sealed dry bore and omitted closure/end masses; they do not select a design or establish stability.

4. Method

For a homogeneous annulus, integrating the polar area element s ds dθ gives the following chain [E]. Factors of two follow from diameter being twice radius; the radial square follows from integration, not fitting.

A(r,R) = ∫₀²π ∫ᵣᴿ s ds dθ = π(R² − r²); m′ = ρA; m′_air = ΣⱼρⱼAⱼ.
A_displaced = ∫₀²π ∫₀ᴿ s ds dθ = πR_outer²; m′_water = ρ_water A_displaced;
m′_sub = m′_air − m′_water.

Area is in m²; volume per axial length is in m³/m and has the same numerical value. Density in kg/m³ times area gives kg/m. Subtracting displaced water mass is the mass-equivalent form of the buoyancy principle explained in [E]. A positive result means material mass exceeds displaced water mass under this basis, not that installation is acceptable.

The full exterior disk includes the sealed void. Internal contents and internal fluid density are stored inputs but do not enter the current material layer sum or change displacement [C]. A flooded bore requires a separately reviewed mass/displacement convention. Flooding behavior is NOT_EVALUATED; changing a label does not model it.

Legacy names containing weight or force with kg/m units mean mass per length or mass equivalent. Force per length is separately reported in kN/m using the existing implementation factor 9.81 m/s² and 1000 N/kN [M, C]. This audits that conversion only; local gravity selection is NOT_EVALUATED.

6. Checks

[T] derives areas from exact rational radii and synthetic densities independently of production summaries. Live area checks use relative tolerance 1e-12 and absolute floor 1e-15 m²; mass checks use relative tolerance 1e-12 and floor 1e-10 kg/m, matching the executable example [E]. These allowances cover binary roundoff in this short arithmetic chain and are far below presentation increments; they are not engineering acceptance tolerances. Force tolerance uses the mass floor multiplied by the implementation conversion [C, T].

The executable document and a separate public-API replay check freshness. Two fresh external builds must match each other and all delivered bytes. The independent manifest readback binds the delivered files and source evidence. Negative controls change a result magnitude or sign; independent arithmetic must reject both even when HTML, JSON, builder and hashes agree with the changed value [T].

Areas are displayed in scientific notation, masses to one decimal place and force equivalents to three decimal places. Inputs retain their stated precision. Unrounded JSON drives assertions; the rounded document table is never a numerical oracle [E, T]. The evidence is available in results.json and manifest.json.

8. Limits / NOT_EVALUATED

  • Physical material densities/defaults, coating grades, porosity, absorption, dimensional tolerances, corrosion, eccentricity and noncircular sections: NOT_EVALUATED.
  • Sealed dry bore only. Closure/end masses and end effects are omitted. Internal-fluid mass and open/flooded-bore behavior: NOT_EVALUATED. The interface accepts contents fields without adding their mass, and PipeCrossSection.to_dict omits internal_fluid_density from its configuration export. These are open method/export findings beyond the present basis [C, E].
  • Internal/external pressure, stress capacity, stiffness or inertia qualification, buckling, code compliance, installation acceptance, seabed stability, hydrodynamics and operational limits: NOT_EVALUATED.
  • Legacy summary wording may imply floating/sinking or standards compatibility. Such claims are not adopted here. Invalid-input policy and alternative coating construction beyond the supported constructor: NOT_EVALUATED [C, M].
  • Optional Plotly/Matplotlib image and legacy HTML exports, CLI/YAML runtime behavior and physical gravity choice: reviewed interfaces only; execution/qualification NOT_EVALUATED. The delivered page requires no remote scripts or assets [V, L, B].

9. Way forward

Retain the synthetic cases as arithmetic regressions. Before physical use, obtain a traceable geometry/material/water basis, define bore sealing and flooding states with closure and contents masses, and have the governing mass/displacement convention reviewed. Establish acceptance criteria and validation evidence separately. Until then, unqualified parts of the wider module remain open; this example provides no release decision.
