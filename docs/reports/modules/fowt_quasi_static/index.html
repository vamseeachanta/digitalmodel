Quasi-static floating wind module analysis

Synthetic arithmetic qualification · Revision A

1. Executive Summary

The quasi-static floating-wind module links rigid-body properties, submerged geometry, mooring equilibrium, hydrodynamic coefficients and linear response. This analysis qualifies arithmetic for synthetic modal roots and a complex impedance only [S1, H1, H2, C1].

Exact sampled roots and range endpoints are retained. Campaign convergence, mooring design acceptance and native BEM results are NOT_EVALUATED. No physical design input or operating limit is inferred.

4. Analysis Methodology

Natural roots solve det[K−ω²(M+A(ω))]=0. The implementation linearly interpolates added mass, sorts eigenbranches, retains the lowest positive root on each branch, and assigns modes by dominant mass-weighted participation. Sampled residuals within 32 binary64 epsilons of the eigenvalue/frequency-squared scale are treated as roots; adjacent intervals are excluded to prevent duplicate candidates [C1].

Modal crossings, multiple roots on a frequency-dependent branch and nearly degenerate mode labels remain unresolved. The search does not establish roots beyond the sampled band. Coupled-system coverage is retained in the existing analytical eigenvalue test [C3].

The response convention is Z=K−ω²(M+A)−iω(B+Bextra), Zq=F. Extra damping is applied only to the implementation-selected components. This example sets it to zero and does not qualify that damping model [C1].

5. Detailed Results

Independent eigenvalue calculation [H1]

For j=1,…,6, det(K−ω²I)=∏(j²−ω²). The positive roots are ω=j rad/s and T=2π/j s. These follow by factoring the diagonal determinant independently of the numerical search. Both sampled endpoints and strictly bracketed roots are exercised [S1, C3].

Independent complex calculation [H2]

At ω=1, Z=5−(2+1)−i=2−i. Multiplying 2/(2−i) by (2+i)/(2+i) gives q=(4+2i)/5. Its amplitude is √20/5 and phase is atan2(2,4) rad. Every unforced component is zero; phase at zero amplitude is undefined. The full complex vector is tested, rather than compared with another solver [C3].

6. Key Findings

Strict sign-change bracketing missed exact sampled roots. Explicit sampled-root candidates correct that defect while retaining the modal assignment step [C1, C3]. The diagonal response control establishes complex sign, amplitude and phase for this impedance convention.

Calculated report values are rounded to 12 decimal places before scratch output, HTML serialization and artifact hashing. Rounding contributes at most 5e-13 in the stated normalized units, below the 1e-12 absolute test tolerance. Undefined phase remains null. This precision applies to these bounded synthetic controls; it does not change solver arithmetic or establish reproducibility for arbitrary inputs [S1, H1, H2, C3].

Period tests use relative tolerance 1e-11 and absolute tolerance 1e-12 s, allowing the bracket solver's default absolute frequency tolerance and binary64 rounding over this bounded range. The diagonal impedance has condition number one; relative and absolute response tolerances of 1e-12 are conservative relative to binary64 rounding for this small solve. These are software-test tolerances, not engineering acceptance criteria [H1, H2, C3].

7. Limitations and Not Covered

  • campaign_convergence: NOT_EVALUATED
  • mooring_design_acceptance: NOT_EVALUATED
  • native_bem: NOT_EVALUATED

No native BEM results, hull mesh, hydrostatic stiffness, mooring solution or campaign load cases are generated. The grounded catenary formula is inextensible; elastic seabed behaviour is delegated by the mooring workflow. Horizontal statics constrain draft and attitude. Finite-difference mooring tangents, geometry topology, spectral tails, symmetry projection, retained-coefficient digests and campaign convergence still require independent qualification [C2].

Linear response excludes nonlinear line dynamics, second-order drift and coupled aerodynamic/control behaviour. A synthetic matrix cannot establish physical resonance accuracy, line strength, anchor capacity or stationkeeping acceptance. No conformity with a standard is claimed.

8. Recommendations

Before physical use, establish attributed geometry, mass origins, line properties, environmental data and acceptance criteria. Independently check geometry volume and hydrostatic restoring, test catenary and elastic line demand, refine native panels and frequency coverage, and compare coupled response against an independent physical benchmark. Qualify constrained reactions and damaged-line cases. Leave the issue open while these methods remain unqualified [C2].
