4. Theory and equations

Integrating dz/dx=sinh(x/a) and ds/dx=cosh(x/a) from the horizontal tangent gives z=a*(cosh(x/a)−1), s=a*sinh(x/a), a=H/w. Eliminating x gives s²=z²+2az. For Case A, a=z=100 m, cosh(x/a)=2, so s=100*sqrt(3) m and x=100*ln(2+sqrt(3)) m. Force balance gives T_top=H+w*z=1962 kN, T_touchdown=H=981 kN and tan(theta)=sqrt(3), hence theta=60 degrees. The remaining grounded length is 500−100*sqrt(3) m. These identities are independently derived, rather than inferred from module outputs.

Textbook basis: Classical Mechanics, online revision 8 August 2022, section 18.3, Equation of the Catenary in Rectangular Coordinates, and Other Simple Relations, equations 18.3.2 to 18.3.9 (Physics LibreTexts page 7046). The coordinate translation is z=y-a. Unit conversion uses the exact SI kilo prefix; the prescribed gravity and numerical criterion come from synthetic Case A.

6. Validation evidence

Numerical agreement criterion met: differences are within 0.1% against the carried-forward 0.1% numerical criterion. The bound is rounded upward from unrounded relative differences; it is not design acceptance.

The existing catenary geometry suite covers both closed-form cases, an explicit-height override, an inverse-tension check and buoyancy rejection. The series stiffness suite checks common-force elongation, splitting invariance and invalid segment guards. Those regressions do not establish geometric stiffness accuracy.

The report suite checks all unrounded quantities against hand arithmetic and a fresh module call; parsed HTML checks every visible value and unit. Controlled mutations of every solver result field must change visible results and the numerical disposition. Two isolated rebuilds compare every delivered output byte. Documented CLI routes are checked against registered commands and invoked with --help only; no command analysis is run. Reproducibility does not establish physical validation.

The roundoff budget is 256 binary64 spacings at unity: fewer than 32 elementary operations, a factor-two library-rounding margin and a factor-four conditioning allowance. The largest subtraction condition is (500+100*sqrt(3))/(500−100*sqrt(3)), below four for this Case A. It is a test precision allowance, independent of the carried-forward 0.1% numerical criterion.

7. Limits of use

  • Design-code compliance: NOT_EVALUATED. DNV-OS-E301 is named by the package; edition, governing clauses, load cases and acceptance mapping are not established here.
  • Actual line strength: NOT_EVALUATED. No qualified material or breaking-load data are supplied.
  • Stiffness qualification: NOT_EVALUATED. Neither tangent geometric stiffness nor physical elastic response is qualified by this example.
  • Environmental loading: NOT_EVALUATED. No environmental input, heading combination or system equilibrium is evaluated.
  • Dynamic mooring behavior: NOT_EVALUATED. No dynamic simulation or licensed solver is invoked; the DYNMOOR port is excluded.

Source-inspection findings: solve_catenary clips negative grounded length and does not reject a suspended length exceeding total length; finite-input and vertical-span validation remain incomplete. check_touchdown retains height/coordinate expressions inconsistent with the corrected geometry route; calculate_stiffness uses a separate geometric expression without independent qualification here. The designer uses the first segment, applies a distance-ratio correction without an equilibrium residual, ignores yaw in that correction and has a broad fallback. Environmental forces are combined in one heading. These concerns are findings for further work, not fixes or qualification claims.

Analyzer evidence; Designer evidence

8. Way forward

  1. Retain Case A as an arithmetic regression. Add independent boundary, finite-input, short-line and general-endpoint checks before extending applicability.
  2. Reconcile touchdown-search geometry and derive tangent stiffness for the intended boundary conditions; compare with independent derivatives.
  3. Establish qualified material properties, environmental cases, equilibrium criteria and the governing standard edition before assigning design acceptance.
  4. Qualify the pinned-end route independently and obtain reviewed dynamic evidence separately. Keep the broader module-report issue open for numerical qualification.

10. Appendix and rebuild

Source-inspected CLI routes: mooring-analysis catenary accepts mass per length and a horizontal or fairlead tension, then also calculates stiffness. It is therefore not executed by this report. mooring-analysis design builds a screening system, calculates environmental loads and analyzes conditions; the shared material object is mutated by its length assignment. mooring-analysis generate-model is a placeholder; mooring-analysis list-materials lists the library.
