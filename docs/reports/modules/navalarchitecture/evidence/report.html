3 Inputs and basis

All dimensions and damping ratios below are synthetic mathematical inputs. Draft 7.0 m and depth 10.0 m demonstrate completeness; length 150.0 m, beam 20.0 m and block coefficient 0.65 demonstrate an absent measured section modulus. These values are selected arithmetic examples, not measured geometry or acceptance limits.

Each compliance row records status, missing_inputs (including invalid data), clause_reference and reference_status. A null clause reference is explicitly UNVERIFIED. The legacy dictionary fields remain available; pass is derived from the typed status.

4 Method and source

Mechanics Map, 2021 online edition, section 15.5, magnification-factor equation and Figure 2: H = 1/sqrt((1-r²)² + (2ζr)²), with r = ω/ωn. At r = 1.0 and ζ = 0.0 the denominator vanishes; no finite steady response exists. ValueError follows the module's refusal style.

Completeness is evaluated before each compliance screen. Aggregate FAIL takes precedence over NOT_EVALUATED; PASS requires a nonempty selection with every selected row passing. Legacy compliance expressions and limits are unchanged and are not qualified by this supplement.

7 Limits of use and NOT_EVALUATED list

Legacy freeboard, proportion, displacement/length, strength, immersion and trim coefficients have no qualified clause here. Their clause references are null; a preliminary PASS does not establish regulatory acceptance. Section-modulus coefficient and units require qualification. IMO Part A 2.3 weather, downflooding applicability, full stability compliance, hull-specific damping, radiation and wave excitation remain NOT_EVALUATED. The spectrum guard rejects absent arrays; it does not qualify spectral discretization.

8 Recommendations and follow-up

Supply measured geometry and strength; select the applicable rule edition and clause; use an approved stability and structural assessment for acceptance. For motion, use qualified system damping and excitation before making an operating decision.

The sweep appendix lists every explicit mapping default, all reduction and broad exception handler in the package. Physical defaults in maneuvering, loading and resistance and partial geometry-reader fallbacks need method-specific qualification. They are follow-ups, not newly accepted inputs. Function-argument defaults and unrelated formula correctness are outside this pattern sweep.
