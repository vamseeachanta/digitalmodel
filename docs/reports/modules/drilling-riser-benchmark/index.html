This internal program benchmarking analysis prepares a comparison; it contains no completed program benchmark.

Method and equation sources

Offset X = p H / 100 (percentage definition), implemented by operability.watch_circle_radius_m. The factor 100 converts percent to a fraction. Uniform drag q = rho Cd D U² / 2 uses the normal Morison drag term in [OrcaFlex WebHelp, Line theory: Hydrodynamic and aerodynamic loads](https://www.orcina.com/webhelp/OrcaFlex/Content/html/Linetheory,Hydrodynamicandaerodynamicloads.htm), Standard formulation, accessed 2026-10-22. No wave or vessel motion is applied.

For constant tension T and pinned ends, EI y'''' − T y'' = q; y(0)=0, y(L)=X and y''(0)=y''(L)=0. [MIT OpenCourseWare, Structural Mechanics (2013), Lecture 6](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-080j-structural-mechanics-fall-2013/resources/mit2_080jf13_lecture6/), equations (6.3)–(6.6), supplies equilibrium and moment-curvature relations; the constant-tension specialization and boundary conditions are derived in riser_response.py. Reported slopes are absolute small-angle estimates in degrees. Variable effective tension, actual joint stiffness and assembly geometry are omitted; these estimates are not qualified assembly joint rotations.

Top force T = W f uses stackup.top_tension_required. W = 6676.6 kN comes from generic_campaign.column_basis (annular steel plus bore contents minus displaced water, multiplied by the recorded gravity assumption). The existing campaign factor is retained explicitly; it has no sourced engineering acceptance status. The column excludes attached members and treats the above-water portion as submerged. Mechanical stroke has no applicable screen here. Chord extension sqrt(L²+X²)−L follows Pythagoras and is only geometric extension.

Stored-summary difference (%) = 100 (result − screen) / |screen|. An agreement PASS requires |difference| ≤ supplied criterion and a source. No agreement criterion is supplied in this report. A zero screen has undefined percentage difference. Unit, quantity definition, case identity and result origin must match; absent, malformed or stale data are NOT_EVALUATED. An agreement PASS would validate only that declared comparison, not assembly acceptance. The required engineering angle, tension and stroke criteria are reference to be selected: candidates must be public.

Worked example

Case o1-c0: offset 1.0% gives X = 10.2 m using the template depth; U = 0.0 m/s makes q = 0.0 N/m. The pinned column is a straight tilt y=Xz/L. Therefore both slopes are X/L radians, or 0.6 degrees, with zero bending moment. The length L = 1020.0 m is taken from the template main line. T = W f = 8345.7 kN; f is inherited from the campaign assumption (1.25; assumed, based on previous experience). The chord extension is 0.1 m. The independent regression example uses simple mathematical inputs; its full arithmetic is written in test_screen_offset_hand_calculation. Mechanical stroke and engineering acceptance remain NOT_EVALUATED.

Limits of use

Approximate riser-column screen; constant assumed top tension, uniform drag, pinned ends; lower joint angle is not established because effective tension varies along the column. Both joint-angle comparisons are omitted: constant tension can introduce an error of about a factor of two. Weight treats the above-water 20 m as submerged, underestimating column weight by about 1.0 %. Stress retains the constant-tension approximation. Screen tension is the legacy screen reading, not graded, not the modelled tension. attached members, nonlinear tensioner response, pressure stress and wave loading omitted. Geometric extension is not mechanical tensioner stroke. No assembly acceptance is established.

The proposed range has no independent public benchmark justification. No licensed solver is run or imported. No wave response, heave, drift-off, recoil, fatigue, VIV, equipment capacity or qualified operating envelope is established. Native assembly comparisons require variable tension and matched definitions; the simplified curves cannot support equipment acceptance.
