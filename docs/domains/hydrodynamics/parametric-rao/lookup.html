Criteria and numerical record

Solver: Capytaine 3.0.0. Reference: docs/reports/2026-10-09-compute-program/barge-capytaine.json at revision 096b76dc242841726c5c8b41ca8f18b55fdab1fa; the no-lid run is compared. Geometry and loading source digests are retained with the database manifest.

Pointwise benchmark bound: 0.002 m/m plus 2.0% of the reference amplitude. Finest-pair convergence bound: 0.02 m/m. These are beforehand pilot screening inputs.

Matched box heave uses 1.2% of the beforehand pointwise bound. Criterion satisfied: true. The comparison is reproduction of an open-source calculation, not independent experimental validation.

Panel counts: [365, 1460, 3285]. Finest-pair heave change uses 0.6% of the stated absolute bound. Criterion satisfied: true. Head-sea heave at the three reference sample frequencies only.

Basis and limits

Linear, inviscid, zero-speed potential flow; rigid bodies; no viscous or mooring damping. The box uses the merged reference loading and scaled gyradii; the analytic ship uses uniform-density mesh inertia. These are pilot design assumptions, not measured loading conditions. All forms are fore-aft symmetric. Coordinates use x along the hull, y across the beam and z upward, with waterline z=0. Headings are wave propagation angles measured counterclockwise from +x; 0 deg is the matched longitudinal case. No lid is used to retain the reference condition. Solver warnings identify possible irregular frequencies at upper sampled points; these remain unqualified for design use. Range coverage is limited to the listed discrete dimensions and frequencies. Roll resonance and independent experimental qualification remain outside this pilot.
