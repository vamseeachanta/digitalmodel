Parametric hull motion analysis

Revision 2

Summary

16 discrete hull variants are solved with Capytaine. Six motion amplitudes and phases are stored for each unmasked frequency and heading; the lookup is read from the database. Data readiness is partial; design use remains unverified.

Criteria and numerical record

Solver: Capytaine 3.0.0. Reference: docs/reports/2026-10-09-compute-program/barge-capytaine.json at revision 096b76dc242841726c5c8b41ca8f18b55fdab1fa; the no-lid run is compared. Geometry and loading source digests are retained with the database manifest.

Pointwise benchmark bound: 0.002 m/m plus 2.0% of the reference amplitude. Finest-pair convergence bound: 0.02 m/m. These are beforehand pilot screening inputs.

Matched box heave uses 1.1% of the beforehand pointwise bound. Criterion satisfied: true. The comparison is reproduction of an open-source calculation, not independent experimental validation.

Panel counts: [365, 1460, 3285]. Finest-pair heave change uses 0.5% of the stated absolute bound. Criterion satisfied: true. Head-sea heave at the three reference sample frequencies only.

Basis and limits

Linear, inviscid, zero-speed potential flow; rigid bodies; no viscous or mooring damping. The box uses the merged reference loading and scaled gyradii; the analytic ship uses homogeneous closed-body inertia about its uniform-density volume centroid, evaluated with waterplane-origin tetrahedral integrals and the parallel-axis theorem. These are pilot design assumptions, not measured loading conditions. All forms are fore-aft symmetric. Coordinates use x along the hull, y across the beam and z upward, with waterline z=0. Headings are wave propagation angles measured counterclockwise from +x; 0 deg is the matched longitudinal case. No lid is used to retain the reference condition. Identified response points removed from the Parquet response arrays and the graphs: box_01 at 1.32 rad/s (within declared interior-mode band); box_02 at 1.32 rad/s (negative heave added mass, within declared interior-mode band); box_03 at 1.32 rad/s (negative diagonal radiation damping: Pitch, within declared interior-mode band); box_05 at 1.32 rad/s (within declared interior-mode band); box_06 at 1.32 rad/s (negative diagonal radiation damping: Heave, within declared interior-mode band); box_07 at 1.32 rad/s (within declared interior-mode band). Negative diagonal radiation damping or negative heave added mass also requires masking. The box estimate uses omega^2 = g*k*coth(k*T), k = pi*sqrt(1/L^2 + 1/B^2). Lowest box interior-mode estimates: box_00: 1.354 rad/s; box_01: 1.330 rad/s; box_02: 1.319 rad/s; box_03: 1.293 rad/s; box_04: 1.353 rad/s; box_05: 1.329 rad/s; box_06: 1.318 rad/s; box_07: 1.291 rad/s. Assumed screening range from 1 percent below to 3 percent above the estimated interior mode, with inclusive edges. The affected range reaches at least +2.2 percent above the estimate; the retained box_00 and box_04 samples bound the lower side. The band edges have not been measured. Masked native coefficients and diagnostic responses remain flagged in the retained JSON; they are unqualified for design use. Retained box values at 1.32 rad/s repeat to about 1.3 %; two records with identical mesh digests differ by 0.46 % there. The cause is not established. The 0.02 m/m convergence bound does not discriminate at 1.32 rad/s; the measured finest-pair change is 8.9e-5 m/m (1.3 % of the amplitude). Hydrostatic stiffness uses face-centre quadrature, whereas ship inertia uses exact polyhedral integrals. Box sway-roll reciprocity remains unresolved; box beam-sea roll is not mesh-qualified. Range coverage is limited to the listed discrete dimensions and frequencies. Roll resonance and independent experimental qualification remain outside this pilot.
