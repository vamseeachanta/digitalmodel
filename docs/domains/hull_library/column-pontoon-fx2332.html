Column pontoon review correction

Scope: draft PR 2332, issue 2318, owner board card A01.

Correction plan

The fix will replace circular fixed-facet profiles with target-derived, area-preserving polygons. Structured Coons grids will cover caps, and axial spacing will account for section edge lengths. Convex clipping and shared seam vertices will remain conforming. Coplanar diagonal flips and an interior point selected against the emitted quad edge ratios will remove avoidable clipping slivers. Conforming edge bisection will target the quad criterion, rather than attempt to change angles fixed by geometric intersections. The full spar and OC4 meshes will be tested against maximum edge-length aspect ratios of 10 and 20 respectively.

The hard tank will occupy the waterline section with its own diameter and depth, while the heave plate will remain at the keel. Unsupported pontoon widths will be rejected. Existing size, overlap and draft validation will be reproduced before any change is made.

Each primitive will be integrated from its emitted closed panel mesh and compared with independent analytic volume, waterplane area, KB and BM at 0.5%. The check will expose mesh values, analytic values, relative differences, tolerances and pass/fail. A perturbed mesh will exercise failure reporting.

The optional geometric lid will inherit wetted-panel orientation and its panels will point upward. Closure will distinguish open-at-waterline, closed-with-lid and invalid-lid. Three/six square-column and six circular-column ring layouts will be exercised. The original 0.5% hand-formula and 1% OC4 acceptance assertions will remain, including the independent 0.05% OC4 model check.

Plan review dispositions

Adversarial review required original-dimension section inertia, shared seam vertices, complete-mesh quality assertions, direct coarse/fine panel-count comparison and integration of actual primitive panels. These criteria will bind implementation acceptance. Small rounded-corner edges will remain a documented refinement cost; solver qualification will not be inferred from hydrostatic checks.

Model limitations

Area-preserving circular facets slightly expand the nominal vertex radius; this compensates polygon area loss but does not reproduce exact circular intersection curves. Braces, column rotation and pontoon end overhangs remain outside this generator. Clipped intersection polygons require conforming local subdivision; cap and uncut face grids have no high-valence radial centre fan. Local intersection refinement can dominate the count for very coarse profiles; total count is not guaranteed to be monotone for every target. An OC4 10/5 m probe exhibited a count inversion because coarser intersections required more quality repair. A blanket monotonic-density claim is therefore not established. The spar 3/1.5 m and OC4 5/2.5 m controls will establish tested scaling above 2× and at least 1.5× respectively. Near-circular rounded corners will retain the 0.5% primitive criterion using at least ten chords per quarter. Rounded arcs are inscribed: displacement has a negative bias, bounded by 0.411% of corner-sector area. The generic reported edge-ratio bound is 20; the spar fixture will meet the stricter test criterion below 10. The aspect metric is longest divided by shortest panel edge, not a solver qualification or angle-quality criterion. Circular Coons cap corner angles approach 180° as the facet count increases; positive turns and bounded edge ratios do not establish angular quality. Angular/Jacobian criteria are tracked in issue 2346. An exceeded edge-ratio bound will be explicit in the report rather than silently qualified. Unresolved ear triangulation will raise a clear error, and cap-grid padding knots will be shared with the side rings before meshing.
