Longitudinal curvature contribution analysis

Summary

The diagnostic separates supplied vertex quadrature into lower end, middle, and upper end contributions to dimensionless double-curvature intensity. It supports investigation of end-closure effects; no hull is qualified by this calculation. Native estimator integration and end-closure acceptance remain NOT_EVALUATED.

Method and source

Geometry-Based Metrics for Early-Stage Hull-Form Producibility Screening (2026), arXiv:2609.27544v1, equations (3), (4) and (39), gives the local density, valid-area average and discrete area-weighted quadrature. The paper was retrieved successfully on 2026-10-19. Regional contributions are derived by restricting the numerator to each region; their shares are ratios to the total intensity.

HullProd, Recommended metrics (2026), Total double-curvature intensity defines qD = |K| Lref2 and its valid-area average. The public source was retrieved successfully on 2026-10-19. Its SHA-256 is 31b62a410360ce2cb221769d0ed6716f1ae6dee2ecbe6f67b858b05a82129983.

The implemented quadrature is

A_valid = sum(A_i for valid i)
I_D = sum(A_i * abs(K_i) * Lref**2 for valid i) / A_valid
I_D_region = sum(A_i * abs(K_i) * Lref**2 for valid i in region) / A_valid
share_region = I_D_region / I_D

The last two equations are an additive partition of the source equation, not additional HullProd metrics. Each region uses the same global valid-area denominator; these are contributions, not separately averaged regional indices. Shares use absolute Gaussian curvature, so saddle contributions remain positive.

Inputs and interpretation

The caller supplies longitudinal coordinates, Gaussian curvature, associated vertex areas, a boolean validity mask, a reference length, and an end fraction. Coordinates and reference length use one consistent length unit; areas use its square and Gaussian curvature its inverse square. No unit conversion is inferred. The axis direction is supplied by the caller; lower and upper do not imply bow and stern.

For coordinate limits x_min and x_max, the lower cut is x_min + f (x_max - x_min), and the upper cut is x_max - f (x_max - x_min). Half-span cuts are computed as the average of the endpoint coordinates. Cut vertices and the immediately adjacent binary64 values on either side belong to the middle to absorb boundary rounding. End intervals use strict inequalities against these expanded middle boundaries. The mathematical bound 0 < f ≤ 1/2 prevents overlapping end intervals; it is not a fabrication limit. The full coordinate span includes invalid samples. Vertices are assigned as whole quadrature samples; triangles are not clipped.

Quadrature combines binary mantissas and exponents before rescaling each contribution, so small normalized areas do not erase representable intensity. Overflow, invalid arithmetic and underflow raise ValueError; nonzero contributions and intensity shares are never silently rounded down to zero.

Areas shall come from the same represented quadrature as the curvature. Matching array lengths alone does not establish correspondence. Supplying barycentric areas for curvature integrated with other weights need not reproduce HullProd's signature. This helper does not extract native fields or establish their vertex ordering, reliability or source status.

A developable input with valid area gives I_D = 0; intensity shares are then undefined because their denominator is zero. No default end fraction, fairness threshold, fabrication limit, or estimator reliability decision is applied.

Masking the second sample removes area 2 and weighted absolute curvature 4. Valid area becomes 6, I_D becomes 4 × 4 / 6 = 8/3, and the middle contribution becomes 4 × 2 / 6 = 4/3. The tests also cover zero curvature, missing valid area, scale invariance, shape errors, nonfinite inputs, overflow, and excluded areas smaller than floating-point sum resolution. Decimal midpoint membership is checked through area and contribution. Independent 450-digit decimal controls cover extreme finite area ratios; unrepresentable nonzero contributions and shares are rejected.

Later slices

Native field and area-weight extraction, vertex-order qualification, matched-density hull comparisons, and end-closure acceptance remain separate work. The present diagnostic does not replace the existing global signature, change interpolation, or establish mesh convergence.
