Delivered calculation and boundary

liquid_critical_drop(P1, Pv, FL, FF) returns the no-attached-fittings liquid choking differential, in psi, for supplied fluid and valve factors. Both pressures are absolute. Vapor pressure is evaluated at inlet temperature and shall be below inlet pressure. FL shall correspond to the operating opening. The numerical input domain requires positive unit-interval factors; unity is a normalized ratio boundary, not a qualified equipment acceptance limit. No factor or property has a default. The result feeds the existing liquid_choke_flow for the same inlet and valve condition.

Equation reference: Emerson/Fisher, Control Valve Sourcebook — Oil & Gas, 2013 edition, Chapter 3: p. 3-5 gives FL²(P1 − FF·Pv); p. 3-3 steps 4–5 and Table 3-2 give differential capping and the US gpm/psi Cv relation. The public PDF text and equation page were inspected on 2026-10-18. Only the equation reference is retained; no source document or equipment table is copied.
