SCR model preparation analysis

Summary

The preparation module calculates a uniform analytical catenary, an equal-arc mesh, and translated hang-off boundary positions. It renders explicitly supplied mdl, inf, and off templates for storm, fatigue, or VIV selection. The worked case gives a suspended length of 4.6 m, a horizontal span of 3.1 m and a mean top effective tension of 50.0 N. These results are checked against the hand calculation below.

The legacy workbook and archived model outputs are absent from the inspected checkout. Workbook extraction, sheet-to-file mapping, native model validity, and response calculations are NOT_EVALUATED. Template expansion does not establish a runnable finite-element model.

Inputs and method

The uniform, inextensible, perfectly flexible cable has a horizontal tangent at the touchdown origin. Z is upward; the mean hang-off lies in the positive X/Z plane. The model contains only the suspended segment. Height h, horizontal effective tension H and submerged weight per unit length w shall be positive. The number of elements shall be an integer from 1 through 10,000; this allocation cap limits list size and does not establish mesh convergence. A caller-supplied offset list is required; an explicit zero offset represents the mean condition.

From Catenary Curve (2019), equations (1), (3), (4), (9), (10), the catenary parameter is a=H/w, the suspended length is S=sqrt(h(h+2a)), and x(s)=a asinh(s/a). Height above touchdown is z(s)=sqrt(a²+s²)-a. The implementation uses its rationalized form z=s[s/(hypot(a,s)+a)] to retain shallow heights. Top vertical effective tension is V=wS; top effective tension is T=hypot(H,V).

The submerged-weight substitution uses the closed-end effective-tension convention described in OrcaFlex, Line theory: Line pressure effects, Effective tension and wall tension formula and Explanation of wall tension formula (accessed 2026-10-19). For a uniform fully submerged pipe with hydrostatic internal and external fluids, the closed-end force balance gives distributed weight equal to pipe plus contents weight less displaced-water weight. This is the supplied w; applying the cable relations to that balance gives effective tension. Wall tension also requires internal and external pressures and their pressure areas, which are absent here; wall tension is NOT_EVALUATED. No pressure correction or wall-stress acceptance is inferred.

Mesh stations are s=iS/N for indices from zero through N. Element lengths are arc lengths S/N, rather than straight-line distances. Cartesian translation gives offset boundary coordinates (x_top+dx, dy, h) and magnitude hypot(dx,dy). This is geometry, with no empirical coefficient. Offsets do not modify the mean mesh or recompute equilibrium; mean effective tension does not represent an offset case. Hang-off X shall remain positive; offsets at or behind touchdown (dx <= -x_top) are rejected.

API and template contract

SCRModelConfig requires analysis_type, height_m, horizontal_tension_n, submerged_weight_n_m, element_count, offsets, template_sets, template_source, and parameters. Offset names shall be nonempty, unique and single-line. Supplied extra parameters are named finite numeric values or single-line text without CR, LF or NUL; their physical meaning, units and sources remain the caller's responsibility.

build_model_context(config) returns geometry, nodes, elements, offset boundary coordinates, supplied parameters and qualification statuses. The existing key top_tension_n means mean top effective tension; top_vertical_tension_n and horizontal_tension_n are its effective-force components. Nodes contain one-based id and s_m/x_m/y_m/z_m; elements contain one-based id/start/end and arc_length_m. Offset records contain name/dx_m/dy_m/x_m/y_m/z_m/magnitude_m.

generate_flexcom_model(config) returns exactly {"mdl": text, "inf": text, "off": text}. Each selected mode requires its own template set with exactly those keys. Missing modes, missing variables and empty results fail. There is no automatic file write, template filesystem loader or solver execution. LF line endings are used by Jinja. Templates are trusted application inputs; native keyword completeness is not parsed. template_source shall identify the supplied template basis and revision. The existing Jinja dependency is reused.

The public Flexcom Data Inputs documentation (accessed 2026-10-19) describes keyword sections for model, analysis and fatigue processing. That page does not establish the unavailable workbook's legacy three-file mapping. No native deck grammar is inferred from it.

Qualification and way forward

qualification_status() returns NOT_EVALUATED for workbook extraction, archive equivalence, native model validity, storm response, fatigue damage, VIV response and wall tension. Rendering success cannot change those dispositions. The analytical test suite establishes only the stated cable equations, connectivity, translations and template behavior. It does not establish a mesh convergence criterion, bending response, stress, fatigue life or VIV response.

To complete conversion, the missing workbook and matched archived input/output files shall be made available through an authorized source. VBA routines and sheet formulas shall then be inventoried and mapped to input parameters and templates. Generated files shall be compared against those retained references; native qualification remains a separate review step. No licensed solver is required by the preparation module or its tests.
