Independent analytical method

For steel outside diameter D and wall t, steel area is π[D² − (D − 2t)²]/4. Each coating adds its annular area times density. The external displaced area includes every coating. Empty-pipe submerged weight w equals gravity times (total mass per length minus displaced water mass per length). Inputs use tonne/m³, m and m/s², yielding kN/m. Flooded contents require another basis and are not assumed here.

For positive weight w, endpoint height h and total upper tension T greater than wh: H = T − wh, a = H/w, suspended length s = √(h² + 2ah), horizontal span x = a acosh(1 + h/a), upper tension = √(H² + (ws)²), and touchdown curvature = 1/a. The independent circular overbend strain is D/(2R), using steel diameter and stinger radius R. These are inextensible, weight-only, planar comparisons: no bending stiffness, contact force, friction, buoyancy discontinuity or vessel motion. No standard allowable is inferred.

For a native comparison, h shall be the measured stinger-exit height above touchdown, not automatically water depth. T shall be effective tension at the same upper endpoint. The current screens use water depth as an explicitly idealized h. Substituting matched native endpoints will require a new evidence record. The full supported S-shape is not a free catenary.
