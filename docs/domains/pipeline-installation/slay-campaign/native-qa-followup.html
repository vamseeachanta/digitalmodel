Readback correction

Only the exact individual variable Support reaction force is accepted. Aggregate Supports force and Connections + supports force, including their components, cannot substitute. Support indices are one-based: the retained bounds probes accept 1 through 25 or 31 and reject zero and the next index. Line-specific Support contact arc length is read with SupportedLineName; the unqualified probe returned an unusable large value. The force result itself remains independent of line name.

The five retained cases have 401 X/Z node stations and 402 common moment/tension endpoint-and-midpoint stations. Position pairs and force pairs are independently validated for finite readings, strict station order, complete pairing and identical endpoints. Only X/Z are interpolated, within bounds, for grounded-force classification. Native moment and tension values are retained without interpolation. Departure and sag curvature are calculated on the original position nodes. Bounded nearest-node mapping, with ties at the representable interval midpoint choosing the lower material station, transfers curvature regions to the force grid. Mesh sensitivity and interpolation error qualification remain open.

The active support is retained by its order, and its measured contact material station locates the native position node. The declared stinger arc remains the lift-off QA quantity. Firing-support count is derived from declared length and spacing, and the full count is checked before indexing. Failed static rows are recorded before the native acceptance assertion, so an engineering rejection remains visible.
