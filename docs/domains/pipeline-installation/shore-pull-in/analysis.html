Method and criterion

For each straight supported segment: net weight w equals submerged weight minus buoyancy aid force per length; sin(theta) = rise / arc length. Normal load N = w L cos(theta), friction = mu N, gravity = w rise. Head tension is trailing tension plus the ordered force increments. Winch demand is head tension divided by mechanical efficiency. Loss of contact and compression are rejected. Equivalent axial friction follows the cited soil paper; gravity resolution is a straight-segment work balance.

The independent check uses separately computed annular areas and horizontal projection. Agreement criterion: relative difference no greater than the force-balance tolerance in criteria.json. All prepared cases meet that computational identity; this is not physical validation. Shore winch capacity is unassigned.

Limits and way forward

  • Four dataset anchors supply pipe outer diameter and hull length, beam and draft; three friction-paper entries supply the case friction. Fifteen inherited scenario assumptions supply gravity, steel and water density, seabed slope and stiffness, supported length, mesh length, hull GM, centre of gravity and radii of gyration. Five wall and coating properties are assumed, based on previous experience, with bases below. Nine declared study parameters supply water depth, tail tension, winch efficiency, and each case rise and buoyancy fraction. No measured pipe property is claimed.
  • The diameter bounds and hull geometry are public dataset anchors; no vessel identity or equipment capacity is retained.
  • Friction anchors describe the cited soft-clay numerical model; breakout, drainage evolution, adhesion, bends, bending stress and hydrodynamic loads are not represented.
  • The prepared native models carry each case axial and lateral pipe seabed friction, a flat seabed and no buoyancy aids. Lateral friction uses the same scalar factor as axial friction; directional soil behaviour remains unqualified. The generator S-lay end arrangement connects the winch from the lay hull to free pipe end A, with end B anchored; it is not a qualified shore-pull topology. Case rise and buoyancy fraction enter the analytical check only; native contact geometry does not represent them.
  • Native execution remains refused; a qualified adapter and an approved manifest digest are required. Native criteria, timing and results are not evaluated (https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/issues/1739, https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/issues/133).
  • HDD, trenching, roller/beach stress, flooding and sinking stability, and method selection remain open (https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/issues/1739). Floating-tow equipment and broader campaign qualification remain open (https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/issues/133).
  • Optional standard design-factor and strain-limit settings from the shared builder are omitted; no standard-based acceptance check is performed.
  • The unchanged dataset record (CSV line 68; header is line 1) is internally inconsistent: draft 15.5 m exceeds minimum lay depth 10.7 m. The 21 m depth meets both numerical requirements using max(10.7, 1.3*15.5); the incorrect field is not established.
  • Winch efficiency 1.0 is ideal: winch demand equals head tension. No shore winch capacity is supplied; the capacity comparison is Not Evaluated. Generated tensioner capacity is a schema placeholder equal to demand, not equipment capability.
  • Builder defaults outside the numeric-input audit include elastic modulus 207 GPa, Poisson ratio 0.293, drag coefficients, other line-type friction, winch wire, supports and ramp shapes. The defaults source identifies the schema entry point only; these defaults are not qualified design inputs.
  • Recorded declarations of criteria timestamps are not independent proof of historical ordering. Geometry criteria are specific to the one-segment, surface-origin template and the assumed wave limit of 1 m; no wider topology or clearance acceptance is claimed.
  • Model bundles are generated and reproduced with the current main shared generator at d4246f224520a65529124de192816f4c03e2e960, combined with this correction. The earlier branch-only generator emits different endpoint seeds; reproduce on the integration tree.

Assign traced pipe and winch properties; qualify the end connections, buoyancy representation and contacts; approve a manifest digest and native comparison criteria; then measure the first native case before a campaign run.

Primary-text transcription opened 2026-10-17. Reference. A = 0.10 m^(1/3) is tabulated at D = 0.20 mm. A = 0.15 m^(1/3) is assumed, retained from the ruling as a rounded endpoint: the table gives 0.1498 at D = 0.43 mm and 0.1514 at D = 0.44 mm. The selected coefficient range is assumed, not a site measurement. h = A*x^(2/3) gives x = (21/A)^(3/2) = 1656.5 to 3043.2 m for A = 0.15 to 0.10 m^(1/3); 2000 m implies A = 0.13229 m^(1/3). At 21 m this is an order-of-magnitude extrapolation; closure depth and site profile are not established.
