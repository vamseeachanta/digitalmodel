Method and criterion

For each straight supported segment: net weight w equals submerged weight minus buoyancy aid force per length; sin(theta) = rise / arc length. Normal load N = w L cos(theta), friction = mu N, gravity = w rise. Head tension is trailing tension plus the ordered force increments. Winch demand is head tension divided by mechanical efficiency. Loss of contact and compression are rejected. Equivalent axial friction follows the cited soil paper; gravity resolution is a straight-segment work balance.

The independent check uses separately computed annular areas and horizontal projection. Agreement criterion: relative difference no greater than the force-balance tolerance in criteria.json. All prepared cases meet that computational identity; this is not physical validation. Shore winch capacity is unassigned.

Limits and way forward

  • Wall thickness, material, coatings, hull response, submerged weight, route, buoyancy and efficiency are scenario assumptions, based on previous experience; public data do not supply them. No measured pipe property is claimed.
  • The diameter bounds and hull geometry are public dataset anchors; no vessel identity or equipment capacity is retained.
  • Friction anchors describe the cited soft-clay numerical model; breakout, drainage evolution, adhesion, bends, bending stress and hydrodynamic loads are not represented.
  • Builder defaults and end restraints are structurally checked only; they are not a qualified shore-pull topology. Buoyancy aids and route geometry enter the analytical check, not validated native contact geometry.
  • Native execution remains refused; a qualified adapter and an approved manifest digest are required. Native criteria, timing and results are not evaluated (#1739, #133).
  • HDD, trenching, roller/beach stress, flooding and sinking stability, and method selection remain open (#1739). Floating-tow equipment and broader campaign qualification remain open (#133).
  • Optional standard design-factor and strain-limit settings from the shared builder are omitted; no standard-based acceptance check is performed.

Assign traced pipe and winch properties; qualify the end connections, buoyancy representation and contacts; approve a manifest digest and native comparison criteria; then measure the first native case before a campaign run.
