Input basis and assumptions

  • A sampled generic wire diameter is assumed; the held property table supplies empirical submerged mass and breaking strength.
  • The force-to-weight-and-depth ratio is assumed from the existing force-based catenary test, rather than derived from environmental loads.
  • Total length relative to depth is assumed from the existing prescribed-pretension test; a horizontal frictionless seabed and surface fairlead are assumed.
  • Gravity follows the existing marine-cable test. Elastic extension, chain segments and vessel coupling are not represented.
  • Platform mass, hydrostatics, numerical sea-state inputs and anchor properties are not stated on the page and are not assigned.

These assumptions are not stated on the source page. The generic wire property examples are held public-source data; no certified chain make-up is inferred. Operating and survival labels are retained in the record; numerical environmental inputs are not stated on the page and are not assigned.

Criteria and way forward

Each computed line has positive horizontal tension, suspended length no greater than the assumed total line length, and a recomputed resultant matching the prescribed force within the recorded numerical tolerance. These are mathematical checks, not code acceptance.

DNV-OS-E301, Position Mooring, edition July 2021: the repository registry identifies Section 2.2.3, intact quasi-static mooring; the governing clause and required factor are not independently qualified here. API RP 2SK, Design and Analysis of Stationkeeping Systems for Floating Structures: the design module identifies Section 5; the governing edition, clause title and required factor are not established. No licensed table values are reproduced or applied.

Environmental demand, actual component certificates, endpoint geometry and the governing code criteria are required before tension, offset, fatigue or station-keeping acceptance can be assessed.
