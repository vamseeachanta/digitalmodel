Box-tank gauging analysis

Summary

A synthetic rectangular tank is used to check sounding, ullage, volume, attitude correction, mass and free-surface GM loss by hand. At a level sounding of 1.50 m the volume is 60.0 m³. A positive 1° surface slope at a gauge offset of +2.00 m gives centre sounding 1.47 m and volume 58.6 m³. At the stated reference density, the level slack tank produces a GM loss of 0.0427 m for the stated displacement. These are calculation checks; an overall stability criterion and real tank calibration are not established.

Method and independent hand calculation

  1. Level calibration. The horizontal area is LB = 40 m², so V = 40h. Table points (h in m, V in m³) are (0,0), (1,40), (2,80), (3,120). Between adjacent points, V = V₀ + (h − h₀)(V₁ − V₀)/(h₁ − h₀) [1]. At h = 1.5 m, V = 60 m³. The inverse gives h = V/40 = 1.5 m. Ullage is the complementary depth u = H − h = 1.5 m; an ullage table has descending volumes as u increases.
  2. Attitude correction. Define h(x,y) = hc + x tan(trim) + y tan(list), with positive slopes rising towards positive offsets. Thus hc = 1.5 − 2 tan(1°) [2]. The correction is 0.0349 m, giving hc = 1.47 m and V = 58.6 m³ to three significant figures. Here the centre-to-end distance is 5 m; end depths are hc ± 5 tan(1°), or 1.38 and 1.55 m. Both lie between 0 and 3 m, so this plane does not clip the roof or bottom. Its integrated volume equals LB hc because the signed x term integrates to zero.
  3. Mass at equal measured volume. At 15 °C, a measured 60 m³ has mass 60 × 800 = 48,000 kg [3]. At 40 °C, the linear expansion factor is 1 + 0.001(40 − 15) = 1.025 [4]. Density is 800/1.025 = 32000/41 kg/m³, or 780 kg/m³ to three significant figures. A measured 60 m³ then has mass 1920000/41 kg, or 46,800 kg to three significant figures. These are two equal-volume cases. A sealed batch initially occupying 60 m³ instead expands to 61.5 m³ and retains mass 48,000 kg.
  4. Free surface in the level slack case. Independent integration gives it = L ∫ y² dy from −B/2 to +B/2 = 10 × (2³ − (−2)³)/3 = 160/3 m⁴. The textbook relation is it = LB³/12 [5]. At reference density, FSM = 800 × 160/3 = 128000/3 kg m. GM loss = FSM/displacement mass = 16/375 m = 0.0427 m to three significant figures. Effective GM = 1 − 16/375 = 359/375 m, or 0.957 m. No stability acceptance follows without a governing criterion.

from digitalmodel.naval_architecture.tank_gauging import (
    CalibrationTable, centre_sounding, density_at_temperature,
    gm_loss_m, liquid_mass_kg, rectangular_free_surface_moment_kg_m,
    sounding_to_ullage,
)

table = CalibrationTable(((0, 0), (1, 40), (2, 80), (3, 120)), "sounding", 3)
centre = centre_sounding(1.5, 3, x_gauge_m=2, trim_deg=1)
rho40 = density_at_temperature(800, 15, 40, 0.001)
fsm = rectangular_free_surface_moment_kg_m(10, 4, 800, fill_state="slack")
results = {
    "ullage_m": sounding_to_ullage(1.5, 3),
    "volume_m3": table.volume_at(1.5),
    "inverse_sounding_m": table.depth_at_volume(60),
    "centre_sounding_m": centre,
    "corrected_volume_m3": table.volume_at(centre),
    "mass15_kg": liquid_mass_kg(60, 800),
    "mass40_kg": liquid_mass_kg(60, rho40),
    "fsm_kg_m": fsm,
    "gm_loss_m": gm_loss_m(fsm, 1000000),
    "effective_gm_m": 1 - gm_loss_m(fsm, 1000000),
}
for name, value in results.items():
    print(name, format(value, ".3g"))

Limits and way forward

The datum and readings are tank-axis depths. The angle convention describes two independent planar slopes. General hull trim/list rotations, inclined measurement tapes and clipped tank surfaces require additional geometry. The free-surface expression is a static small-angle model for a fully exposed rectangular surface. Empty and pressed-full states have zero free surface; nearly full slack tanks do not. Baffles, subdivisions, sloshing and a full stability assessment are not evaluated. The thermal coefficient and its qualified range require a fluid-specific source before engineering use.

Later work will add attitude-indexed calibration tables with an explicit correction convention and clipping qualification. Correction columns are currently refused. Real calibration data and custody-transfer procedures remain outside this example.
