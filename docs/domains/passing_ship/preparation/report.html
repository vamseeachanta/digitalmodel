2. Scope

Parallel passing-ship forces are sampled with the existing native calculator. Tension, motion and fender response remain open under #2016. Published benchmark qualification remains open under #2017.

3. Input basis

Public hull dimensions and coefficients: paper Table 3. Separation and depth ratios: Table 2. Speed and density remain assumed inputs. The layout assumptions and sizing results are recorded in model.json.

Four assumed inextensible lines retain 600 kN fairlead pretension. A submerged weight of 100.0 N/m and fairleads at water level are assumed, based on previous experience. The repository touchdown catenary sizes each independent depth layout: line lengths are 286.3, 313.0, 538.1 m at the three increasing depths. Anchors at lateral offsets of plus or minus 20.0 m lie inside the closest passing hull edge (22.3 m); longitudinal anchor positions vary with depth. These isolated static lines pass the excess-length and swept-track checks. Elastic extension, seabed properties and whole-vessel equilibrium remain unqualified before #2016.

4. Method

Existing surge, sway and yaw integrals are used unchanged. Samples at half a hull length astern, abeam and half a hull length ahead are not coefficient peaks. The sectional-area approximation is unvalidated. There is no time-domain response integration. Wind is excluded unless an explicit force and moment vector is supplied.

The existing finite-depth correction in formulations.py is an uncited placeholder. Its source labels the implementation simplified and says the actual formulation is still needed. The shallow-water multiplier is an uncited linear factor: 1 + 0.5 * (1 - (h/D)/1.5) for h/D < 1.5, otherwise 1. Recomputed sway factors over the three separations are 1.131 at h/D 1.108; 1.059 at h/D 1.324; 1.000 to 1.009 at h/D 3.919. The existing correction factor supplies the whole depth trend of all 18 force cases; this trend does not qualify shallow-water behaviour. #2017 remains open.

5. Case matrix

18 Cartesian entries vary passing speed, centreline separation and water depth divided by moored draft. The case data are recorded in campaign.json.

8. Benchmark criteria

Equation (9) defines Cx and Cy as force divided by 0.5 rho D L V^2, and Cm as moment divided by 0.5 rho D L^2 V^2. Positive maxima are compared with the model-test series. The full peak-search stagger window is not established from the opened text. Fixed force samples cannot supply these peaks. Proposed relative tolerance: 5.0%. The dimensionless absolute floors and their SI scaling basis are frozen in criteria.json. These limits do not represent experimental uncertainty. Numerical paper references remain unavailable; no benchmark comparison has been made.
