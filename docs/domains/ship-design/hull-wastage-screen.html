Hull-structure wastage analysis workflow

Summary

The hull-wastage-screen workflow screens representative item thicknesses, recomputes measured hull-girder properties, and optionally evaluates a uniformly thinned unstiffened plate with the existing metal-loss FFS solver. Missing checks remain NOT_EVALUATED; a missing pitting basis prevents overall acceptance. This is engineering decision support. Class survey acceptance is not established.

Inputs and method

All thicknesses are in mm; section geometry is in m; plate stresses are in MPa. Each element requires a unique ID, as-built thickness, representative measured thickness, and nonnegative measurement uncertainty. The lower bound is measured thickness less uncertainty, bounded below by zero. Representative item values, survey coverage, zone membership and uncertainty require an external survey basis; raw TM report parsing and representative averaging are not performed.

CSR mode requires corrosion and voluntary additions. Renewal thickness equals as-built thickness minus these additions, following CSR BC&OT July 2025, Part 1 Chapter 13 Section 2, Local corrosion. Below renewal the local disposition is RENEW. At renewal and below renewal plus the selected reserve it is MONITOR; at the upper boundary it is ACCEPT. MONITOR denotes follow-up, not approval of an alternative solution. Percentage mode requires an explicit renewal thickness and substantial-corrosion fraction; strict excess of that fraction of the allowable margin follows IACS UR Z7 Rev.21 (2014), Substantial Corrosion.

There are no numeric rule defaults. limits.source records the selected basis and edition; reserve_mm or substantial_fraction is mandatory. These values are supplied engineering inputs, not internally resolved code constants. The result retains method references and the supplied source alongside the numeric record. Changing a limit does not establish applicability to a particular hull.

The optional global block requires complete_section: true, depth_m, modulus_fraction, na_corrosion_fraction, a source, and contributing members. Each member references a measured element_id and defines gross_offered_mm, orientation (horizontal or vertical), span_m, z_m, and zone (deck, bottom, neutral_axis or other). Every required zone must be represented. Zone membership is supplied from the applicable approved section definition.

Gross and measured area, centroid, inertia and extreme-fibre section moduli are recomputed by naval_architecture.hull_girder_screening.section_properties. Both measured moduli are compared with the selected fraction of gross offered moduli. The neutral-axis-zone area is separately compared with area calculated at gross offered thickness less the selected fraction of corrosion addition. Basis: CSR BC&OT July 2025, Part 1 Chapter 13 Section 2, Global corrosion. The alternate deck/bottom zone-area route and the item-loss shortcut are not implemented.

An optional buckling block references one element and requires length_mm, width_mm, sigma_x_mpa, sigma_y_mpa, tau_mpa, gamma_m, a source and material values matching MaterialProperties. Material fields are yield_strength, ultimate_strength and youngs_modulus (MPa), poissons_ratio (dimensionless) and density (kg/m³). Loads are nonnegative compression/shear magnitudes at the reduced section; the adapter does not redistribute loads.

The existing assess_plate_uniform_loss method is called at the lower-bound thickness. Its utilization criterion is retained. Transverse compression is explicitly NOT_EVALUATED because the existing plate solver does not evaluate that component. Pitting, stiffener wastage, stiffened-panel capacity, nonuniform loss and full CSR buckling qualification are not established by this adapter. A failed optional plate check produces RENEW as a screening action requiring strength/repair assessment; it does not establish that renewal is the only possible engineering solution.

Worked example

examples/workflows/hull-wastage-screen/input.yml contains synthetic values selected solely to demonstrate arithmetic. Renewal is 12.000 − 3.000 − 1.000 = 8.000 mm. The uncertainty-adjusted value is 8.400 − 0.100 = 8.300 mm. The selected reserve is 0.500 mm, so the local result is MONITOR. The overall result is NOT_EVALUATED because the example supplies no global, buckling or pitting basis.

from pathlib import Path
import yaml
from digitalmodel.hull_wastage_screen.workflow import router
cfg = yaml.safe_load(Path("examples/workflows/hull-wastage-screen/input.yml").read_text())
cfg["hull_wastage_screen"]["output_dir"] = "results/hull-wastage-initial"
router(cfg)

The workflow is registered for the engine and registry consumers, including Deckhand, as hull-wastage-screen, basename hull_wastage_screen. The direct router above avoids importing unrelated workflow adapters. It writes hull-wastage.json and hull-wastage.html. Existing analyses are never overwritten; re-issues require a fresh output directory.

Validation record

tests/hull_wastage_screen/ contains independent arithmetic and boundary tests. Expected values are derived in test docstrings; no comparator calls the implementation under test. Local reserve and percentage boundaries, fully consumed material, uncertainty, invalid inputs, missing data, duplicate IDs, global modulus and neutral-axis failures, report readback, escaping and retained-output preservation are covered.

The symmetric section benchmark uses synthetic horizontal strips at elevations 0.0, 1.0 and 2.0 m. At 10.000 mm measured thickness and 1.0 m breadth, the neutral axis is 1.0 m and inertia is the sum of two parallel-axis terms and three strip own-inertia terms. The exact independent expression is retained in the test.

The elastic plate benchmark uses the simply-supported compression coefficient from NACA TN 3781 (1957), equation (20), with the half-wave length equal to plate width. The elastic plate stress expression and a hand-derived utilization are evaluated independently in the test. This checks orchestration and elastic arithmetic; it does not qualify the inherited inelastic or combined-load model.

Disposition and later slices

Priority is RENEW, then NOT_EVALUATED, then MONITOR, then ACCEPT. The shared FFS Verdict vocabulary and hull action_for mapping are reused: REPAIR for renewal, ESCALATE for missing checks, MONITOR for follow-up. Pressure heuristics and pressure derating are not used. In this revision pitting always remains NOT_EVALUATED, so overall ACCEPT is deliberately unavailable.

Later slices require an opened primary hull-survey pitting criterion with confirmed Rec.84 applicability, a pitting-intensity classifier, stiffened-panel wastage inputs and benchmarks, raw TM-report ingestion, and approved survey applicability/zone definitions. No API 579 pressure-equipment pitting verdict is substituted for hull renewal criteria.
