Calm-water resistance study (indicative)

Readiness: partial. Qualified resistance results: 0

Summary

The retained short starts provide indicative computation-cost evidence only. Resistance prediction cannot yet support an engineering decision. Settled runs, numerical uncertainty and experimental agreement are not established.

Revision history

The retained starts were recorded on 2026-10-14. This report is regenerated from the committed manifest and its recorded criterion snapshot.

Study basis and criteria

A generic benchmark hull is fixed at even keel without appendages in calm water at a requested Froude number of 0.26. Geometry and fluid particulars are inherited case inputs, not independently verified experimental inputs. Each run records the geometry digest and coefficient normalisation inputs; the rendered flow speed, rather than the unrounded request, is used.

The planned mesh multipliers are 0.5, 1.0, 2.0. Only the coarse and medium starts are retained; a fine-mesh start is not retained. A constant division-count ratio does not demonstrate uniform realised mesh refinement or asymptotic grid convergence.

The proposed total-resistance comparison tolerance is 3.0 percent and the medium-mesh grid uncertainty target is 1.0 percent. These are assumed study criteria recorded before the starts, not a standard requirement or a demonstrated validation threshold. An opened experimental table and page are required; no experimental comparison value is retained.

Method and resource measurements

The OpenFOAM force histories contain 100 iterations per start at 8 ranks. The intended full length is 25000 iterations, with the final 4000 iterations proposed for averaging. These lengths do not establish convergence.

Peak memory is the recorded sampled aggregate resident memory of the launcher and descendants at the time of measurement. Sampling implementation and native solver logs are not retained here; transient peaks, kernel memory and independent log verification are not established. Solver version is not established by the retained files.

Projected duration is the measured short-start wall time divided by completed iterations and multiplied by intended full iterations. Mesh construction is excluded. Projections are indicative; steady-run performance is not established.

Findings and limitations

The coarse streamwise force history becomes unstable and changes sign. The medium history still drifts; neither history establishes settled resistance. A successful process return code does not establish a physical solution. Full-duration results and a fine-mesh start are absent from this evidence set.

Resistance comparison: not available. Grid uncertainty: not established; 3 completed settled meshes with a justified refinement relationship are required. No accepted resistance coefficient, numerical uncertainty or experimental validation is reported.

Next evidence required

The unstable coarse history requires diagnosis before extending it. Settled force histories, complete mesh evidence and an opened tabulated experimental reference are needed before the proposed criteria can be evaluated. Resource policy calls for 6 GB memory reserve, 15 GB free disk and no more than 80 projected solver hours in total. The incomplete mesh set cannot establish a total resource projection.
