2 Introduction

The analysis concerns one laboratory-scale bare-hull calm-water condition at nominal Froude number 0.26. The hull is fixed at even keel; sinkage, trim response, appendages, incident waves and full-scale extrapolation are excluded.

4 Analysis Methodology

The free surface and hull forces are calculated with OpenFOAM v2312 using a two-phase volume-of-fluid model, the k–ω SST turbulence model and local pseudo-time stepping; these are read from the retained case, including the native solver banner and phase, turbulence and time-discretization dictionaries [I3]. The following boundary description is stated, not read by this report renderer: the inlet prescribes negative-x velocity, the outlet uses a phase-mean velocity condition, the atmosphere permits pressure-driven inflow or outflow, and the fixed hull has zero wall motion.

The executed inlet speed and water properties in Table 3.1 define the normalization. Half-domain forces are converted to full-hull resistance by Equation (1).

RT = −2 Fx,half   (1)

CT = RT / (½ ρ S U²) = CP + CV   (2)

Fr = U / √(g Lpp),   Re = U Lpp / ν   (3)

Mean CT = Σ CT,i / N;   σCt = √[Σ(CT,i − Mean CT)² / N]   (4)

The force window contains iterations 21001–25000 after completion at iteration 25000. Dispersion in Equation (4) is descriptive; a separate temporal-stationarity limit is not established. Three full meshes at nominal refinement ratio r = 2 are required before the grid-convergence-index method [I5] can be applied. Validation is planned against the matched towing-tank data described in Section 5.

Known limit: offsetting components

As a descriptive reading only, unqualified and not a validation, medium Ct differs from the fixture by -1.0 % and coarse Ct by +9.9 %. At the executed Re, ITTC-57 gives Cf = 0.002832; medium Cv differs by -7.3 % and coarse Cv by -16.6 %. The fixture-derived residuary Ct − Cf is 0.000728; medium Cp differs by +23.6 % and coarse Cp by +112.9 %. Coarse Ct exceeds medium Ct by 11.0 %, using medium as denominator. The offsets are computed from unrounded inputs and are displayed to one decimal place. The near-match of medium Ct comes from offsetting components and must not be read as agreement. V2a (pressure against residuary), V2b (viscous against ITTC-57) and V3 (two-level consistency) are not applied: the criteria fixed before results adopt only V1 and the three-mesh grid convergence index. 7 Limitations

The following evidence gaps govern use of the calculated coefficients.

  • The missing third full mesh prevents a three-grid uncertainty estimate; the cost basis is given in Appendix A.
  • The missing matched experimental table prevents validation; source access and condition matching are explained in Appendix B.
  • Original geometry qualification, fluid-property sensitivity, domain-size influence and independent temporal stationarity are not established; their affected conclusions are described in Appendix B.

8 Recommendations

Engineering resistance use should remain on hold while the uncertainty and validation quantities required by Table 3.3 remain unevaluated.

  1. Obtain a primary geometry source and a printed towing-tank coefficient at the matched physical condition before evaluating experimental error using Equation (2).
  2. Provide additional duration or resources for the missing full mesh, using the measured projection in Table A.1 as the scheduling basis; then evaluate the declared three-mesh uncertainty target.
  3. Quantify domain-size and temporal sensitivities before qualifying absolute coefficients; the present final-window calculation Equation (4) does not supply those bounds.

Appendix B – Interpretation and missing evidence

The following details explain the limitations that affect engineering use.

  • Primary geometry-source access and a matched printed experimental table remain unavailable. Recalculation of the retained inputs does not establish original input qualification.
  • Final-window averages and dispersion describe the computed histories. No independent temporal-stationarity tolerance was predeclared; neither dispersion nor the two-mesh difference supplies a numerical uncertainty bound.
  • The nominal linear refinement ratio is two. The reference lateral division count was rounded upward from 19 to 20 before scaling, so the intended block division ratios remain constant. Surface clipping and local refinement mean that total cell-count ratios need not be exact cubes of two.
  • Domain placement is inherited and was not varied. Its influence on absolute resistance is not bounded by the available runs.
  • The history plot groups at most 200 bins in the final 4000-iteration window. Every value is used in the tabulated mean and dispersion; plotted shading preserves each bin's extrema. The half printed coefficient increment, 5 × 10⁻⁵, defines the near-zero display threshold for relative mesh differences, not an acceptance tolerance.
