Part 9 Level 1 crack screening readiness

Summary

The readiness screen records explicit crack requests for plates, cylinders and spheres, each with a quarter-thickness or full-thickness reference-flaw family. The result is NOT_EVALUATED for every family because qualified Level 1 curves and their validation evidence are unavailable. No allowable crack size or fitness-for-service acceptance is established. The decision is ESCALATE for a qualified Part 9 Level 2 assessment.

Method

  1. An explicitly identified crack is supplied as a Part9Level1Case. Thickness-grid morphology classification does not identify or exclude cracks.
  2. Software input checks reject unsupported geometry and reference-flaw names, non-finite or non-numeric quantities, non-positive dimensions or material properties, inconsistent flaw position and depth, temperatures below absolute zero, and ultimate strength below yield strength. These checks do not establish standards applicability.
  3. Omitted inputs are listed separately from missing method evidence. Zero stress and negative assessment temperatures at or above absolute zero remain supplied values. Inside radius is requested for cylinders and spheres. Total flaw length is used, not half-length. Flaw orientation and position, base/weld/HAZ location, post-weld heat treatment, cyclic service and toughness basis are required context. Explicit false service and heat-treatment flags remain supplied values.
  4. All curve requests return NOT_EVALUATED, with acceptable: null and allowable_flaw_length_mm: null. The decision adapter returns ESCALATE without a metal-loss strength factor or reduced rating. Routing records level_attempted: 1 and level_reached: null, including for an empty intake.

The reference-flaw family selects a requested curve; it does not set the measured depth or establish a depth-to-thickness acceptance limit. Surface and embedded flaws require depth below thickness; through-wall flaws require depth equal to thickness. A supplied through-wall flaw remains unevaluated under either reference family. Loading signs are retained without claiming their applicability to a Level 1 curve.

The temperature bound is a physical input-domain check based on the Celsius definition in BIPM's SI Brochure, 9th edition (2019), section 2.3.1, kelvin, also published in NIST SP 330 (2019), section 2.3.1. It is not a Part 9 applicability or acceptance limit. Direct construction of a result applies the same input checks and requires its missing-input list to match the supplied case.

Remaining evidence and way forward

  • Readable Level 1 applicability rules and reference-flaw regeneration assumptions for the selected edition and geometry.
  • A qualified Part 9 Level 2 basis with the applicable stress-intensity, reference-stress, material, residual-stress and other required treatments. The existing plate FAD alone does not qualify the requested curves.
  • Independent, source-identified digitized figure points for both curve families and all three geometries, with documented comparison tolerance.

Future numeric screening will require this evidence before an acceptance branch is enabled. The current intake is a software readiness checklist; a complete standards applicability assessment, curve regeneration and figure-point validation remain deferred. Intake consolidation with the separate inspection workflow also remains deferred; completeness here does not establish completeness there.
