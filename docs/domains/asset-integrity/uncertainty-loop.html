Uncertainty loop

Expand a step by clicking its name or by focusing it with Tab and pressing Enter or Space. The arrows show the workflow order; they do not execute a model.

  1. Measure
    Input
    Inspection readings, survey evidence, metocean and operations records enter the common evidence table.
    Output
    A time-aligned evidence register retaining units, provenance and qualification gaps.
    Missing evidence
    Dated readings, coverage, calibration and process history are absent in this synthetic schematic.
    Decision criterion
    Required input: maintainer-defined evidence quality and coverage criteria before fitting a trend.

    ↓ Next: Model

  2. Model
    Input
    Qualified evidence register and a documented process-history basis.
    Output
    A proposed trend model with its validity period, assumptions and validation needs.
    Missing evidence
    A common dated thickness record and an independently tested forecasting basis are required.
    Decision criterion
    Required input: a validation method and acceptance criteria; a short-record fit alone does not establish forecast skill.

    ↓ Next: Quantify uncertainty

  3. Quantify uncertainty
    Input
    Proposed model, measurement uncertainty and model assumptions.
    Output
    A proposed uncertainty definition separating latent trend from future observation uncertainty.
    Missing evidence
    Coverage, noise assumptions, uncertainty propagation and validation evidence are required.
    Decision criterion
    Required input: the band meaning, coverage and validation criteria before interpreting any uncertainty range.

    ↓ Next: Decide

  4. Decide
    Input
    Evidence gaps, validated forecasts if available, and stated decision criteria.
    Output
    NOT_EVALUATED: collect missing evidence and obtain maintainer review before choosing an inspection or intervention.
    Missing evidence
    An engineering threshold, inspection policy and validated likelihood mapping are absent.
    Decision criterion
    Required input: applicable engineering limits and an inspection decision rule. Remaining life is not probability of failure.

    ↓ Next: Reinspect

  5. Reinspect
    Input
    A reviewed inspection plan with its scope, timing and required measurement quality.
    Output
    New dated readings and coverage evidence return to Measure for reconciliation with the historical record.
    Missing evidence
    An approved inspection plan and new observations are required; no inspection date is calculated.
    Decision criterion
    Required input: a reviewed inspection interval and evidence collection plan; reconcile new observations before reusing a trend.

    ↓ Next: Measure

↰ Return to Measure with new inspection evidence.

Process-change feedback → Model

Process change: reassess the historical trend

Trigger: A process change, intervention or changed operating state is recorded in the operations evidence.

Feedback path: Operations → Model → Quantify uncertainty → Decide.

Reassess the historical trend at Model: review whether earlier readings represent the changed conditions, document the valid period and re-quantify uncertainty before deciding again. No change point is detected by this page.

Decision supported: identify missing evidence and review the historical trend basis before choosing the next inspection. An inspection date or intervention is not selected here.

Assumptions and limitations

No forecast is calculated. No trend uncertainty band, end-of-life threshold, remaining-life range, probability of failure or risk reclassification is established.

Remaining life is not probability of failure. A short-record fit alone does not establish forecast skill.

Missing engineering criteria remain required inputs. Numerical comparisons and the heading animation are later work; this page is the first precursor only.
