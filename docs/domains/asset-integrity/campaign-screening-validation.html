RBI and interval hand case

Input: diameter 10 in, pressure 1440 psi, yield strength 50000 psi, nominal and uniform measured wall 0.400 in, loss rate 0.010 in/yr. The existing B31.8 screener's configured design factor gives required thickness 0.200 in. Remaining life is (0.400 - 0.200) / 0.010 = 20 yr. Half life is 10 yr. The supplied maximum is 8 yr.

The input strength-margin edges assign the capped strength ratio to category 1; the life/reference ratio is 20/5 = 4, category 2. Rate 0.254 mm/yr is below the supplied 0.300 mm/yr escalation threshold. PoF is max(1,2) = 2; consequence B maps to category 2, hence risk score 4 and LOW under the supplied policy. The interval is min(10,8,8) = 8 yr. The uniform grid satisfies the inherited sufficiency screen.

Repair selector hand case

A grid with 24 cells at 0.190 in and one interior cell at 0.160 in has mean 0.189 in (rounded). Mean divided by required thickness is above the supplied strength threshold, but the minimum is below required thickness. Projected life is zero, so the existing coordinator returns REPAIR. The supplied non-leaking, non-axial pressure context selects internal classes C and D. These labels are not ISO or PCC-2 categories. Repair life 6 yr times supplied review fraction 0.5 gives a candidate repair review interval of 3 yr. The unrepaired component interval remains zero and requires engineering action now.

Local-loss and missing-data cases

The local-loss minimum is 0.300 in against required thickness 0.200 in at 0.010 in/yr loss: remaining life = (0.300 - 0.200) / 0.010 = 10.0 yr. Life/reference = 10.0/5.0 = 2.0, category 3 at the inclusive edge; RSF/RSFa = 1.0/0.9 gives category 1. PoF = max(1,3) = 3; consequence E maps to 5, hence score 15 and MEDIUM-HIGH (edge 15 inclusive). The RBI cap is 4.0 yr and min(5.0,8.0,4.0) = 4.0 yr; the campaign interval is withheld for TAKE_MORE. Repair-loss scores 20 and ranks before local-loss at 15, then uniform-loss at 4.

Descending strength-margin and life-ratio bands include a relative edge tolerance of 1e-9 to absorb unit-conversion roundoff; values below that tolerance retain the worse category. This also protects the uniform case at life/reference = 4.0. Equal scores rank by action severity (immediate engineering action, engineering review, more measurements, planned inspection), then remaining life ascending (unbounded life last), then identifier. The zero-life REPAIR or REPLACE verdict supersedes inherited sufficiency messages about office re-rating; it cannot authorize continued use.

A single interior thin cell does not resolve the local profile. Its sufficiency actions are retained, and its next interval is NOT_EVALUATED until the measurements and assessment are completed. Missing repair context produces NOT_EVALUATED with no candidate class. Duplicate identifiers and non-finite or non-positive measurements are rejected.
