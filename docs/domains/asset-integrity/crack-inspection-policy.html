Synthetic example

All numerical inputs below are artificial controls. Depth is in metres, DeltaK in MPa sqrt(m), C in m/cycle/(MPa sqrt(m)) raised to the growth exponent, and time in years. A constant cycle rate is assumed throughout the predicted life. No automatic unit conversion occurs.

from digitalmodel.asset_integrity.crack_inspection_policy import plan_crack_inspection

result = plan_crack_inspection(
    table=[[0.001, 5.0], [0.002, 2.0]],
    law=dict(c=1e-10, m=2, threshold=2, e_ratio=1,
             threshold_rule="unchanged", basis="Synthetic growth law"),
    cycles_per_year=1000000,
    interval_divisor=2,
    policy_cap_years=10,
    policy_basis="Synthetic divisor and cap; not a service policy",
    target_depth_basis="Supplied assessment target; not a critical depth",
)

For linear DeltaK decreasing from 5 to 2, the independent integral is N = (1/2 − 1/5)/(C × 3000) = 1000000 cycles. Dividing by 1000000 cycles/year gives 1.0 year; dividing by 2 gives 0.5 year, below the supplied 10.0 year cap.

Result interpretation

A non-null target cycle count gives DIAGNOSTIC_POLICY_ONLY and min(cycles / cycles_per_year / interval_divisor, policy_cap_years), without rounding. Positive-threshold arrival at the last depth retains finite life even with an ARRESTED growth status.

Initial or interior arrest, and divergent arrival, retain null target years and interval with REVIEW_REQUIRED. Actual arrest depth and cycles-to-arrest remain in the growth record; unknown target life never selects the cap. Results retain copied inputs and shared-integrator provenance. All numerical output is finite or null. Nonfinite arithmetic raises an error before a cap can hide it.

Remaining requirements

The final depth is a supplied assessment target. Published-example qualification, calibrated stress-ratio correction, material and detection qualification, automatic critical-depth calculation, leak-before-break and risk-based inspection integration remain open under #2177.
