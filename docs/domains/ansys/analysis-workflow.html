ANSYS analysis workflow

Revision 1 · 13 September 2026 · Common operating workflow · Governing criteria.

This mapping implements common workflow Revision 1 and analysis criteria Revision 1. The workflow applies to component, connection, assembly and structural analysis. Each response retains its own governing basis. The initial automation scope is the offline evidence adapter in the reviewed issue 2119 plan; Stage A is authorized by the recorded user approval. The implementation report records its coverage. This document does not execute ANSYS or expand existing run approvals.

First application and handoff

Stage A denotes the offline evidence-package adapter phase in the issue 2119 plan at commit ec54195ba74900806f684011d43e4fe1de589b32, plan SHA-256 3ad0710a146a464703a71c906c5f93a0e28e396d05e487cb6c00848fc2dd31e5. Its recorded plan verdicts are Claude MINOR and independent Codex APPROVE. Stage A is not a numbered common-workflow stage and does not bypass any of them.

The delivered examples/ansys/analysis-evidence/intake.json distinguishes original/corrected pressure-vessel, padeye and mudmat captures, retained files, rights and eligibility. At least one real eligible capture must be imported and read back for Stage A completion; synthetic fixtures test refusal behavior only. Zero qualified responses must be reported as zero engineering coverage. No eligible real capture means a blocked stage, not a weakened acceptance rule.

Pressure-vessel records require the specified pressure/thermal response definition; padeye records require qualified load/connection and section/local-stress interpretations; mudmat records require the stated soil/contact and restraint basis. No family inherits another's qualification. The handoff names the assigned engineering owner/checker, exact artifacts, unresolved checks and next authorized action. The next native campaign requires its own frozen case manifest, verification register and execution approval.
