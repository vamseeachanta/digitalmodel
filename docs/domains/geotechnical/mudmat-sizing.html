Mudmat candidate sizing

The smallest supplied width that meets both allowable bearing and sliding demands is selected. This is a centred-unembedded-undrained screening operation, not complete foundation acceptance.

Synthetic example

Widths and lengths are in m, loads and resistances in kN, shear strength and pressure in kPa, and submerged unit weight in kN/m³. Aspect ratio is length divided by width. The factor of safety is explicitly supplied and is not selected by the software. All values below are synthetic test inputs, not a design basis.

from digitalmodel.geotechnical.mudmat_sizing import size_mudmat

inputs = dict(aspect_ratio=1, vertical_load_kn=100, horizontal_load_kn=10,
              undrained_shear_strength_kpa=10, submerged_unit_weight_kn_m3=8,
              factor_of_safety=2)
result = size_mudmat(width_candidates_m=[1, 2, 3], **inputs)
assert result.status == "SELECTED" and result.selected.width_b_m == 2
infeasible = size_mudmat(width_candidates_m=[1], **inputs)
assert infeasible.status == "NO_CANDIDATE" and infeasible.selected is None

The 1 m square fails both demands. The 2 m square provides approximately 123.4 kN allowable vertical resistance and 20.0 kN allowable sliding resistance, exceeding the supplied 100 kN and 10 kN demands. Every supplied width is evaluated, including those after selection.

Inputs and retained evidence

Widths shall be nonempty, positive, finite and strictly increasing. Aspect ratio shall be at least one. Supply widths as a list or tuple; NumPy arrays are not supported. Vertical demand, shear strength and factor shall be positive; horizontal demand and unit weight may be zero. Factors below one are permitted and can make allowable capacity exceed ultimate capacity; this is not design acceptance. Booleans, nonfinite values and unrepresentable geometry or capacities raise ValueError. Unsupported condition, embedment and moment keywords raise TypeError. Immutable results retain detached inputs, dimensions, the existing method description, ultimate and allowable capacities, the supplied factor and both comparison outcomes. Equality meets a demand.

Limits and way forward

Selection is restricted to the actual grid; a continuous or global optimum is not established. Embedment and moment are fixed at zero. The existing calculator supplies the numerical method unchanged. Drained and eccentric sizing, gravity-base overturning, load inclination, cyclic loading, settlement, the optional groundhog adapter and qualification against Brinch Hansen (1970) and DNV-RP-C212 examples remain outstanding under issue #264. Those checks require separate evidence before foundation acceptance.
