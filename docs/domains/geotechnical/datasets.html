Records and the dataset mapping are immutable. Unknown IDs raise KeyError. Older files without a datasets section return an empty mapping; a present section requires a list of validated records.

Evidence and limits

The metadata basis is the research note dated 2026-10-12, sections 4 and 6, in issue #1205. The access route identifies the public CPT release. Citation and reuse terms remain owned by the existing S11 source record. Its retrieval and rights statements are historical; no fresh retrieval or rights verification is claimed.

The note describes sounding and index CSV files plus legacy text archives. Size is recorded as not stated; omitted size descriptions remain None. Source bytes and their digests are absent. No download, measured values, unit conversion or profile ingestion is performed.

Linking any source preserves its stated terms and read status, including unresolved terms. Independent source-file retention, digest checks, rights review, units and engineering applicability are required before numerical use. Soil units, methods and foundation integration remain later work.
