Starting-shape admission: known limits

The C06 screen compares the starting-shape polyline length with unstretched section lengths. A relative mismatch above 0.001 (0.1%) produces FAIL, and the YAML validator converts every starting-shape FAIL to ERROR. Consequently, validate_file returns valid=False even when the file has no other validation error. This policy currently applies to generated and hand-authored models alike. Rejection by this screen does not establish that a model cannot load or that its strain is physically invalid.

Repository impact

The second-provider review at c2ee2f80 reports a screen of 135 repository YAML files containing user-specified starting shapes. Of 44 files without other errors, 25 change from valid to invalid: 22 in installation, two in pipeline and one in reference. Of these, 24 fail the arc-length check and one lacks the required table. The reported worst mismatch is about 0.10-1.7% in 22 of the 24 arc-length cases; the other two are about 52% and 79%. These corpus counts are review evidence, not a repeated corpus measurement in this fix-up.

Two repository-file regression cases establish the current public-validator behavior: the D04 library example passes, while a floating pipeline example is rejected solely for an arc-length mismatch between 0.26% and 0.27%, above the 0.1% limit. These tests read the existing files directly; they do not invoke a solver or copy model data.

Decision still required

The maintainer must decide whether an arc-length FAIL on hand-authored models remains ERROR or becomes WARNING, and how generated models will be identified if the policies differ. The existing criterion does not establish whether its reference length should be stretched or unstretched. No severity change is made here. The regression records current behavior and will need a deliberate update if that policy changes.
