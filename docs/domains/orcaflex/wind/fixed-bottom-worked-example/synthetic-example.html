Methodology and assumptions

For each approved soil factor a, K_case = a K_held for all selected translational and rotational soil coefficients; retain zero coefficients, damping and DOF definitions. The unchanged baseline has identity factor one. Soft/stiff levels require a cited input; none is supplied by default. This workflow writes no model and makes no stiffness-to-frequency approximation.

Sample mean = sum(x)/N: NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook (online edition read 2026-10-20), section 1.3.5.1, Measures of Location. Minimum, maximum and absolute peak are selected from samples in the inclusive window. Means are sample means, not time-weighted. Relative change is defined as (variant − baseline)/baseline; zero load baselines give NOT_EVALUATED. Compare within a wind/control arrangement using reviewed shape labels, matching windows, force conventions and non-soil comparison-basis digests. No rank pairing or acceptance tolerance is used.

Worked sample calculation

Explicitly synthetic arithmetic, not a turbine prediction: Fx samples −3.0, 0.0, 6.0 kN give (−3.0 + 0.0 + 6.0)/3.0 = 1.0 kN and absolute peak 6.0 kN. Tower frequencies 1.0, 2.0, 3.0 Hz for softened, baseline, stiffened soil give relative changes −50.0% and +50.0%. These arbitrary test inputs and their independent hand calculation are in test_fixed_bottom_workflow.py.

Conclusions and limitations

Confirm equivalence to the defining reference, approve soil factors, wind/control reconciliation, seeds and sampling/convergence plan before native execution. Retain native static convergence, mode shapes and full time histories. Export loads at the mudline section; do not substitute tower-base loads or sum independent component peaks into a resultant. Confirm paired mode shapes at every soil level. No structural capacity, fatigue, frequency-separation or soil adequacy criterion has been evaluated. Steady versus turbulent differences are confounded by speed and control changes until reconciled.

Stored exports only; native convergence and shape evidence require independent review.
