Summary

Offline preparation of 24 generic combined-load cases. Imported scalar comparison: NOT_EVALUATED. Engineering acceptance and native solver results: NOT_EVALUATED. Native solver loadability, coupled equilibrium and dynamic response are NOT_EVALUATED. Generic tanker RAOs lack qualified wind/current/drift coefficients. The inherited tanker is excluded from statics and requires equilibrium configuration. Supplied transfer-hose properties require qualification; synthetic arithmetic fixtures have no physical or manufacturer basis. Whole-line extrema must cover the analysis stage; time and mesh sensitivity, pretension, offsets, anchor capacity, fatigue and tension-dependent hose limits require separate assessment. No environmental force or hawser load is inferred.

Analysis methodology

Flatten generic template includes; retain original sources and SHA-256 digests; set each combined wind, regular wave and current case; validate native YAML structure. No native load or solve is performed. Te is the whole-line maximum effective tension. Ut = Te / allowable tension. Uk = maximum curvature × constant minimum bend radius. Bend radius R = 1 / curvature; a straight line has infinite radius, represented by null. Normalised values at or below unity meet the supplied scalar limit. Missing data/criteria are NOT_EVALUATED; hand calculations are ILLUSTRATIVE. Definitions: https://www.orcina.com/webhelp/OrcaFlex/Content/html/Lineresults,Forces.htm and https://www.orcina.com/webhelp/OrcaFlex/Content/html/Lineresults,Moments.htm (public web help, accessed 2026-10-20).
