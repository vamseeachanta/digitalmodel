Executive Summary

The jacket is a fixed steel structure in 84.1 m water depth. Analysis is performed to determine aggregate anode requirements and compare published monitoring readings. The key results are summarized below.

This report is a worked example of the published 84.1 m jacket, calculated by the workflow. The paper’s printed values are compared with the workflow results in Appendix A. Hand calculations check the three phase-current sums. Tool checks compare the saved currents and anode counts with a fresh calculation and check the report rebuild. The checkpoint table compares six printed quantities; it does not establish local protection. Temperature and resistivity sensitivity is not part of this report; it is a separate reviewed issue.

  • Aggregate sizing is conditional on the stated inputs.
  • The installed count is 569; the independent cylinder estimate requires 575 anodes.
  • Local protection requires separate attention.
  • The two lowest monitored levels fail the stated potential criterion at day 518. Arrange a retrofit assessment and repeat surveys after intervention.

Introduction

The published arrangement is analysed with the repository calculation workflow. Printed demand, unit output and count values are compared with calculated values. Differences in assumed anode geometry are retained.

Design Data

The four published area groups and effective phase densities are used without a separate coating deterioration law. Nine connected wells add 18 A at every phase. The resistivity conversion is 22 ohm.cm × 0.01 = 0.22 ohm.m. Design life is 25 years and temperature is 10 °C; these are assumed, based on previous experience. Capacity, utilisation, net alloy interpretation and the two electrode potentials are also assumed. Equivalent-cylinder radius is calculated from nominal mass, length and an assumed alloy density of 2750 kg/m³. The −0.800 V protection criterion is a stated assumption. The paper gives a 0.25 V driving voltage, but not the separate electrode potentials. No standard citation is used to qualify the assumed −0.800 V criterion. The depth diagram shows the available monitoring data; a realistic member elevation cannot be established from the published information.

Analysis Methodology

Current demand is calculated as area multiplied by effective density, plus connected allowances. Lifetime net mass equals mean current multiplied by service hours, divided by capacity and utilisation. Initial and final current outputs equal driving voltage divided by anode resistance. Required count is the largest of the mass, initial-output and final-output counts. DNV-RP-B401, May 2021 [2], supplies the method headings Anode mass, Anode resistance and Anode current output. No standard table is reproduced. Aggregate capacity passes only when provided capacity is at least the corresponding demand. Potential must be more negative than −0.800 V versus Ag/AgCl/seawater; stated assumption, not a value printed in the paper or established by a standard citation. Missing or invalid inputs stop report generation with a reason.

Detailed Results

The calculated phase currents and anode counts are presented in Appendix A. Printed outputs give passing aggregate checks for the installed arrangement. The independent equivalent-cylinder estimate fails initial current output at 569 anodes and requires 575. These are different output bases, not interchangeable verdicts.

Key Findings

The printed demand totals are reproduced within 0.1 %. Aggregate capacity does not establish protection at each member. At day 518, the two lowest monitored levels remain more positive than the stated −0.800 V criterion. The measured-potential table prints the criterion and assumption beside each status.

Limitations

Member dimensions, buried geometry, sediment resistivity, per-level anode allocation and present condition are not established. Local protection, intervention timing and a per-level retrofit split are not evaluated. Temperature and resistivity sensitivity is not part of this report; it is a separate reviewed issue. The report does not establish a qualified installation design.

Recommendations

Retrofit assessment is recommended for the two lowest monitored levels because the day-518 readings fail the stated potential criterion. Assess clamped anodes at these levels, as proposed in [1]. Establish allocation, electrical continuity and remaining anode condition before selecting quantities and an intervention date. Repeat potential surveys at the same positions after intervention. A reading at or more positive than −0.800 V triggers investigation under the stated assumption. For aggregate design using the cylinder estimate, increase the count by six, to 575; recheck geometry, attachments and distribution before use.

Acronyms and Symbols

Symbols and units are defined here. Cathodic protection limits steel corrosion by supplying current. A is ampere; kg is kilogram; m is metre; yr is year; Ah/kg is ampere-hour per kilogram; ohm.cm and ohm.m are resistivity units. Ag/AgCl is the silver/silver-chloride reference electrode in seawater. Lower-case i denotes current density; upper-case I denotes current demand. The coating multipliers f_ci, f_cm and f_cf apply to initial, mean and final phases, respectively; all are unity for the effective-density inputs. PASS means that the adjacent criterion is met on the stated basis. FAIL means that it is not met. NOT_EVALUATED means required evidence is missing, invalid or not applicable.
