## Component demand, allocation and installed adequacy

The component route validates component-local life and separate seawater temperature, electrolyte resistivity and anode-surface temperature. An electrical graph describes explicitly supplied continuity for the protected duration; per-family reciprocal allocation is checked independently. These are implementation requirements, not measured installed facts ([connectivity](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/blob/09f90bf7d67be13a493eb5ede29e4a6f246f58c8/src/digitalmodel/cathodic_protection/b401_component_connectivity.py), [allocations](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/blob/09f90bf7d67be13a493eb5ede29e4a6f246f58c8/src/digitalmodel/cathodic_protection/b401_component_allocations.py)).

**Supported disposition:** distinguish required/recommended capacity and count from installed capacity/count. Preserve source-backed component-demand diagnostics with their excluded surfaces and temperature assumptions. A recommendation cannot prove installed adequacy or current sharing between disconnected local families. Actual allocation, continuity and installed geometry need controlled project drawings, inspection/installation records and responsible-engineer confirmation. An electrical edge accepted by the schema does not establish acceptable resistance or a measured bond.

**Alternatives:** evaluate each isolated local family independently; or use a verified common network and explicit load allocation. The second option is disqualified when continuity or lifetime evidence is missing. An alternate edition is a separate declared comparison, not a replacement for the historical governing edition. 

## Depleted geometry and temporary/foundation structures

The family model distinguishes fresh geometry, remaining metal and final geometry, and derives separate initial/final output checks. See [family implementation](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/blob/09f90bf7d67be13a493eb5ede29e4a6f246f58c8/src/digitalmodel/cathodic_protection/b401_anode_families.py). Required dimensions and shape eligibility must be supplied or independently qualified; do not fit depleted geometry until a source layout passes.

R1 locates B401 2021 Tables 8-1 through 8-8 on printed pages 58–60. Reinforcing-steel drain is Table 8-3 with caption reference [3.3.12]. Electrochemical values are Table 8-6, keyed by anode-surface temperature and exposure. Table 8-7 equations were graphics and are **not** transcribed in the owned text-derived dataset. Therefore exact formula and depleted-shape/distribution applicability still require authorized rendered-source verification; implementation agreement alone cannot close that gate.

**Alternatives:** manufacturer/drawing-qualified final dimensions; or a documented governing-standard depletion model after verifying its shape and mounting assumptions. Neither option determines the actual installed shape. Temporary-service duration, assemblies deliberately excluded from protection and their electrical boundaries remain explicit project decisions.

## Ship hull applicability and bilge-keel layout

Use the December 2017 ABS Ships basis despite the route name ending in 2018. The 2026-10-01 owner decision already accepted arithmetic-mean coating treatment, the depleted long-flush resistance interpretation and required explicit dynamic bare-steel current density. Preserve these as **recorded owner interpretations** rather than general standards conclusions (R4; [ships implementation](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/blob/09f90bf7d67be13a493eb5ede29e4a6f246f58c8/src/digitalmodel/cathodic_protection/abs_ships.py)).

The layout implementation returns NOT_EVALUATED when explicit bilge-keel fitment is absent. A fitted keel additionally requires alternation confirmation. It separately retains unevaluated special geometry. See [layout implementation](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/blob/09f90bf7d67be13a493eb5ede29e4a6f246f58c8/src/digitalmodel/cathodic_protection/abs_ships_layout.py). These Boolean fields are confirmations of project evidence, not defaults to infer from the hull label.

**Alternatives:** apply fitted-keel checks using the actual arrangement; or document non-fitment and assess the remaining hull layout. Drawings/as-built evidence decide the branch. Keep static-storage/offshore guidance as a separately qualified scope comparison; it cannot silently replace the historical ships basis.

## Concrete reinforcement and sediment-exposed families

The retained R1 Table 8-3 caption and area-unit metadata refer current drain to **reinforcement steel surface area**. This is table-caption evidence, not a new readback of clause [3.3.12]. Outer concrete area is not the stated table input. The component/family route keeps concrete, sediment and seawater load/environment groups separate ([zone mapping](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/blob/09f90bf7d67be13a493eb5ede29e4a6f246f58c8/src/digitalmodel/cathodic_protection/b401_component_zones.py)). The implementation identifies buried-metal current-density citation [3.3.8]; this is a code locator, not a new independent readback of that clause.

**Supported disposition:** reuse a qualified embedded-steel demand diagnostic while stating that it does not validate buried-family output, layout or continuity. Determine reinforcement inventory/exposure, concrete and sediment boundaries, resistivity, protection criteria, continuity, anode material/temperature and fresh/final geometry from project evidence. R2 Table A-1 concerns linepipe coating; R1 Table 8-3 concerns reinforcing steel. Combining these distinct inputs would be an unqualified engineering assumption. Uncertainty in burial or continuity invalidates a complete-layout claim.

## Pipeline field joints, infill and temperature

R2 places F103 2019 Table 6-2 on printed page 26, Table 6-3 on page 27 and Appendix A coating Tables A-1/A-2 on pages 51–53. Current-demand selection uses internal fluid temperature; anode material design values use anode-surface temperature and exposure. These are different inputs; copying one into the other without an applicable thermal basis is unsupported.

The adapter supports edition-specific field-joint classifications and separate infill choices, including distinct FBE variants, while retaining compatibility/qualification limits ([table routing](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/blob/09f90bf7d67be13a493eb5ede29e4a6f246f58c8/src/digitalmodel/cathodic_protection/f103_tables.py), [pipeline calculation](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/blob/09f90bf7d67be13a493eb5ede29e4a6f246f58c8/src/digitalmodel/cathodic_protection/dnv_rp_f103.py)). R2 confirms that the May 2021 amendment changes identifiers editorially while retaining numeric values; preserve the actual supplied system identity in a qualified mapping.

**Alternatives:** confirmed installed coating/infill with qualification records; or explicitly labeled bounding alternatives after separate approval. “PU” alone cannot determine an installed material or governing table row. Tentative temperature entries and omission of qualification prose mean table selection is not complete PQT/owner acceptance. A source-method review is not numerical acceptance.

**Uncertainty and alternatives:** under otherwise fixed assumptions, omitted protected surface can understate demand; greater electrolyte resistance can reduce output. Unknown geometry, burial, thermal coupling and continuity can affect demand and supply in different directions, so there is no universal conservative multiplier or quantified range. Proposed screening alternatives need response-specific bounds, assumption owner and replacement trigger; they cannot turn missing physical evidence into an installed fact.

**Closed at method level:** separate demand/capacity/output/distribution; distinguish recommendation and installation; explicit continuity evidence; keel geometry relevance; actual metal/electrolyte partition; coating/infill distinction and temperature roles. **Still open:** historical contractual clauses/exceptions, graphics-only B401 resistance qualification, actual protected boundaries/geometry/continuity/infill/thermal basis, exact private artifacts, custody/rights, comparator tolerances and engineering acceptance. No findings change prior FAIL, NOT_EVALUATED or 11/16 coverage.
