Well intervention screening

digitalmodel.well.intervention connects problem types to intervention options, compares supplied decline fits with measured oil rates, and bounds constant-uplift payback including deferred production. Outputs support candidate investigation. They do not approve a treatment or establish well integrity.

Method and access

method_options(Problem.SAND, subsea=True) returns slickline bailing, qualified powered e-line cleaning, and coiled-tubing circulation options. Each option contains its purpose, diagnostic status, separate access classes and public sources. Tubing failure maps to HWU or rig workover. A valve is assumed tubing-retrievable unless wireline_retrievable_valve=True is supplied. Declining rate and integrity include diagnostics; no uplift is assigned to logging by default.

Subsea wireline options include riserless light well intervention vessels, riser-based intervention and rigs. CT requires riser-based or rig access in this generic screen. Special open-water CT and tubing-pull systems require separate capability checks. Sources: 2003 technical study, method and access sections and RPSEA 08121-1502 Task 2 (2010), Tables 5–6.

Decline and economics inputs

DeclineFit(qi_bopd, di_per_day, b, start_date, basis) accepts supplied Arps parameters. RatePoint(date, rate_bopd, source) accepts measured history. Quantity rates and decline constants use the shared Pint registry; bare values are bbl/day and nominal 1/day. The supported classical Arps range is 0 ≤ b ≤ 1. Exponential, hyperbolic and harmonic evaluation follows Texas A&M PETE 613 Lecture 8 (2023), Appendix B.

candidate_screen requires a shortfall threshold, consecutive-point count, assessment date, maximum data age and maximum sample gap. Every point in the qualifying tail must be positive and strictly below the threshold-adjusted fit. Unsorted, duplicate, future and pre-fit dates are rejected. Empty, stale, gapped, insufficient and zero-rate histories are distinguished from positive candidate evidence. Use one well/completion and fluid stream with a consistent producing-day or calendar-day rate basis. A rate below fit does not distinguish depletion, choke changes, water breakthrough, allocation errors or removable damage.

uplift_from_shortfall(candidate, Band(0.25, 0.75)) converts a positive fit-to-measurement gap to an assumed 25%–75% recovered share. Recovery fractions are required scenario inputs in [0,1], not success probabilities or treatment coefficients. Alternatively supply incremental oil directly as below.

from digitalmodel.well.intervention import Band, screen_economics

result = screen_economics(
    baseline_bopd=20.5,
    uplift_bopd=Band(10, 20),         # assumed incremental oil, not total liquid
    cost_usd=Band(20_000, 40_000),    # assumed total direct job cost
    duration_days=Band(2, 4),        # assumed full production outage
    net_price_usd_per_bbl=50,        # net realized margin, not gross price
    horizon_days=180,                # elapsed days from job start
    basis="illustrative assumptions; not a field estimate",
)
print(result.payback_from_start_days)  # PaybackBand(low=24.05, high=92.2)

The oil balance is u·max(H−d,0) − q₀·min(d,H), relative to continued baseline production. Deferred oil is q₀·d; restart payback is (C + p·q₀·d)/(p·u). Elapsed payback adds d. Cost, duration and uplift endpoints are enumerated to bound the scenarios. These are explicitly defined accounting identities using the DOE simple payback definition (2009), item 1. No discount, uplift decline, success probability or later recovery of deferred oil is assumed. A None payback bound denotes no finite recovery under that constant-uplift case. Finite extrapolated payback beyond the horizon is reported, but pays_out_in_all_cases is false. Oil and net-cash pairs are ordered (minimum, maximum) and can be negative; input Band limits cannot.

Wireline conveyance hook

For deviated or horizontal slickline/e-line options, supply method_options(..., wireline_inputs=conveyance_inputs), or call wireline_conveyance directly, with measured drift, casing ID, tool OD, required clearance, pump rate, fluid density, buoyed weights, lateral length, inclination, friction, eccentricity and restriction coefficient. Physical inputs accept Pint quantities or bare SI; inclination is from vertical. The adapter calls the existing completion.pump_down clearance, resistance and solved-speed functions. It returns a clearance failure, stall, pump-down screen pass, or a gravity-assisted branch needing review. A positive speed does not establish retrieval, tension, weak-point, pressure, trajectory or barrier qualification. No default pump-down coefficients are introduced. Supplied wireline inputs are evaluated once and the result is attached to each line option; failed options remain visible with their status. Without these inputs, option.conveyance is None means not evaluated. method_options alone does not establish access.

With the hypothetical economic inputs above, deferred oil is 41–82 bbl, elapsed payback is 24.05–92.20 days and 180-day net cash is USD43,900–155,950. These scenarios recover their outlay within the chosen 180-day horizon. The BSEE records do not establish any intervention, treatment outcome, cost, uplift or removable production loss. A candidate flag therefore does not establish that the actual well will pay out.

Integration limits and follow-ups

The external decline pipeline can pass qi, nominal Di, b, fit start and measured rate history directly; no sibling repository is read. Project-specific subsea spread ranges, historical analog uplift, success probability and decline-dependent cash-flow forecasting remain follow-ups. Explorer and website integration belong in their owning repositories. The unit day-rate source is unreviewed supplier guidance; replacing it with a stronger public cost basis is a follow-up.
