Diffraction G4 acceptance evidence

The G4 evidence record separates mesh screening from acceptance. All documented sub-checks remain visible when evidence is missing. Only the free-flotation mass residual is evaluated against a supplied criterion; no overall G4 PASS is available.

API and disposition

g4_acceptance.assess_g4 returns a fresh JSON-ready dictionary. The optional mesh_screen_status is retained as context, with no effect on G4 status. The function does not change the packaging gate, the geometry checker or the legacy displaced-mass screen.

The record inventories each sub-check in the diffraction knowledge pack, revision 4, section 5 (AQG r0 section 8, G4.1a–G4.8e). Unsupported checks retain NOT_RUN, NOT_ASSESSED applicability, missing criterion and measurement fields, and a description of the required evidence. Absence never implies NOT_APPLICABLE.

Free-flotation mass arithmetic

For input mass M in kg, independently calculated displaced volume V in m³ and declared water density rho in kg/m³:

relative residual = abs(M - rho V) / M
arithmetic PASS when relative residual <= relative_tolerance

The formula is transcribed from the knowledge pack revision 4, section 5, G4.1a. Common gravity cancels only for free flotation. This check does not evaluate restrained preload, environment agreement between programs or any other G4 sub-check. The primary AQG document is not bundled; no numerical AQG acceptance limit is encoded.

FreeFlotationMassEvidence requires the three physical inputs, relative_tolerance, and criterion_source, mass_source, displacement_source and density_source. Supplying this evidence declares free-flotation applicability and a common case/environment. No tolerance or density default is supplied. The result retains the inputs, references, derived displaced mass, dimensionless residual and criterion. References are declarations; their contents, digests and AQG qualification are not authenticated.

Independent arithmetic examples

The synthetic test mass of 1000 kg, volume of 0.9 m³ and density of 1000 kg/m³ give a displaced mass of 900 kg and residual abs(1000 − 900) / 1000 = 0.1. A supplied synthetic limit of 0.1 therefore passes inclusively. The legacy denominator would give 100 / 900, which exceeds that limit. Volume of 0.8 m³ gives residual 0.2 and fails that limit. These are independent hand calculations in the test docstrings, not qualified engineering inputs or published acceptance limits.

Remaining evidence

Native hydrostatics comparisons, centres and stability, panel resolution and resource limits, waterline/draft, outward orientation, symmetry, native paired irregular-frequency removal and interior-lid evidence, required views and response checks remain unevaluated. Existing callers do not invoke this API. Integration and qualification of those checks remain later slices of the tracked G4 acceptance issue. No solver is loaded by this module.
