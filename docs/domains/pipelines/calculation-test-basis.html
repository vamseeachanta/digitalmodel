Pipeline calculation test basis

Summary

Independent synthetic examples test annulus properties, linear wave dispersion, velocity transfer, spectral integration, Weibull current tails and integrity workflow dispatch. Every numerical oracle is reduced independently in the test docstring. Production calculations are unchanged. Strict expected failures retain the unmet criteria described below. These examples establish arithmetic and adapter behaviour; they do not establish site-specific design qualification.

Acceptance criteria and known gaps

Floating-point, rounded-oracle and quadrature tolerances are separated in each test docstring. The shallow-water tolerance includes the finite-depth asymptotic error. Current fit inputs are exact quantile pairs from fixed uniform variates, not a random sample; the fitted interface returns tail shapes, not a fitted scale. Scale is exercised through the independently known extrapolated speeds. Return periods cannot be inferred without an occurrence rate.

Independent spectral tail derivation

For gamma = 1, assume the dimensionless spectral shape family x−5 exp(−b/x4), where x = ω/ωpeak and b > 0. The logarithmic derivative is −5/x + 4b/x5. Requiring the peak at x = 1 gives b = 5/4 = 1.25; the derivative is positive below the peak and negative above it. This derives the coefficient within the assumed shape family; it does not establish that family as a physical sea model. With u = b/x4, du = −4b x−5 dx, so the shape integrates over (0, ∞) to 1/(4b). The unit-integral density is therefore q(x) = 4b x−5 exp(−b/x4).

An antiderivative of q is exp(−b/x4), giving the upper tail ∫R∞ q(x) dx = 1 − exp(−b/R4) and the lower tail ∫0L q(x) dx = exp(−b/L4). At R = 20 the upper fraction is below b/R4 = 1/128000 < 8 × 10−6; at L = 0.01 the lower fraction is exp(−125000000) < 10−100. For the synthetic target variance of 1 m², these fractions give omitted variance below the absolute test tolerance of 10−4 m², leaving room for quadrature. The derivation applies only to gamma = 1. The expected variance remains independently established by Hm0 = 4 sqrt(m0); the cited wave-height section is not used as a source for the exponent coefficient or tail integral.
