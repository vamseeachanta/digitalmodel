Computed curves

Every curve calls the library functions directly. The inertia curve uses the library's frozen-fluid prismatic roll inertia; it is distinct from a validation error for rigid-column conduit inertia.

Damping is evaluated at forcing period 20.0 s with imposed level-difference amplitudes from the public damping test. It is amplitude-dependent, not a fixed tank property.

The moment calculation imposes 5.0° roll at the illustrative conduit area.

Coupled calculation inputs from the public response test: vessel roll period 20.0 s, hull damping 5.0 %, tank authority 12.0 %. Authority is a normalized stiffness scale, not a mass ratio. The library fixes its forcing reference through the bare-roll amplitude; the returned response is roll per unit wave moment at that finite reference. Quadratic loss prevents an amplitude-independent RAO. Shading marks periods outside the published moment-fit band. No wave-spectrum or dimensional vessel calibration is implied.

Validity band

The moment model degrades below about 16 s and the CAUSE IS NOT ESTABLISHED. An earlier explanation was withdrawn. The residual also depends on roll amplitude.

The reported moment RMS applies at forcing periods of 20 s or longer for the published calibration. Intermediate periods are not qualified by that aggregate statistic. Even inside the band, transfer to the invented geometry is an assumption. No new physical mechanism is proposed.
