Preload settlement that stops is compared with settlement that accelerates. In the elevated-storm example, leg inclination and strain rise toward illustrative limits. The instrument table connects six failure modes to observable precursors, explicit alert inputs and conditional lead times.

Synthetic demonstration only. The mappings are engineering proposals based on the open literature below. The sources support mechanisms and measured quantities; they do not validate these warning algorithms or thresholds. No measured, client or earlier study data is used.

The motion-forecast demonstrator shows phase-resolved seconds ahead within a predictable wave zone. Qualified metocean forecasts can provide hours for preparation; monitoring confirms the response as it happens. This page does not connect to a forecast feed.

Preload is sampled every 10.0 s; it ramps to 1000.0 kN at 4.0 min and then holds. Both logs start with exponentially decaying settlement. The accelerating log adds quadratic penetration after the hold begins, followed by an imposed jump at 11.0 min. The storm is sampled every 60.0 s with linearly increasing inclination and strain; illustrative limits are 450.0 mdeg and 1200.0 µε. Inclination is plotted in millidegrees (1000.0 mdeg = 1.0 deg).

Each rule requires a channel, metric, threshold, unit, persistence and maximum gap. Magnitude uses absolute values; rise rate and acceleration retain their sign, with positive settlement downward. Backward differences use actual elapsed time, with acceleration between interval midpoints. Equality counts as a crossing; a non-crossing sample resets persistence. Confirmation is timestamped at the final required sample. Invalid values, missing channels and excessive gaps raise an error: the monitor is unavailable, not safe.

All settings and synthetic formulas are declared in digitalmodel.marine_ops.self_elevating_monitoring. Use evaluate(time_s, channels, rules) with caller-supplied settings and consistent units. Preload rules apply only to the selected loading phase; a real adapter must confirm the preload hold. No automatic hold detection, sensor ingestion or unit conversion is implemented.

Limits and way forward

Soil layering, foundation capacity, structural utilization, sliding resistance, overturning margin and intact or damaged stability are not solved. IMU attitude does not measure stability reserve; inclination and strain alone cannot reliably detect pure sliding. A position reference, load assessment and geotechnical investigation are needed. The wave experiment cited below supports displacement and acceleration monitoring, not a field warning performance claim.

Before field use, the maintainer should obtain site and equipment assessments, survey references, sensor calibration and temperature compensation, clock synchronization, noise filtering, data-quality alarms and independent protective trips. Set thresholds below assessed limits with sufficient time for the approved response; qualify them on representative logs and a holdout set. A warning calls for review under approved procedures. No configured alert is not permission to continue.
